Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

Aspiran is a study companion for UPSC aspirants. This page says what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. SnapSkill LLP ("we") is the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What stays on your device

Your journal, mood check-ins, Close the Day notes, profile details beyond the four matching fields, your roadmap and goal answers, and your focus logs stay on your phone or in your browser. We never receive them.

What we keep on our servers

  • Your account. Sign-in is with Google only. We keep your Google account id and email address, to sign you in and to help if you contact us.
  • Pod matching. A random member id and four fields (stage, attempt band, language, time band), so we can place you in a pod.
  • Pod messages. What you post in your pod, so the pod can read it, and reports and moderation notes when something is reported.
  • Usage counts. Which features were used and when (for example "joined a pod" or "built a roadmap"), one-tap ratings and the day-7 check-in answers. Never the text of your mood, journal or chats. We use no advertising SDKs and sell nothing.
  • What we count while you study. That you opened or finished a note, an audio or a video (by its code, never what you wrote about it), answered a practice question and whether it was right, finished a review or a mock (which paper, how many you tried and how long, never the score), wrote or rewrote a Mains answer (how many words, never the words), how long you were in your pod and how many messages you sent (never a word of them), and that you searched (how many results, never what you typed). Only codes and numbers, under your random member id. Pod counts stay on our own server and never go to PostHog.
  • If you join a coaching centre. The centre sees what you agreed to when joining, from the day you joined: your name and batch, days you used Aspiran, focus minutes, your answers to its own question sets and tests, your doubts and your messages in its chat. Never your own practice, mocks, pod, Chotu, Calm or journal. Its batch figures show only for batches of 5 or more, and it never sees a ranking. Leave the centre and you are out of its reports.
  • Usage analytics (PostHog). Which screens you open, what you tap, errors the app hits, and a replay of how a session went, sent to PostHog, an analytics service, under your random member id (never your name or email). In replays every word on the screen and everything you type is hidden, so we see where people tap and get stuck, not what they read or write. Your pod, rooms, Chotu's Talk, Calm, Close the Day, Your room and Me are never recorded. You can switch this off in Me → Privacy → Usage analytics (in the Android app: Me → Privacy). On our website and public topic pages PostHog runs without cookies and without an id that follows you between visits.
  • Payments. For Plus: the order id, amount, status and Cashfree's payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details go to Cashfree, not to us. The mobile number Cashfree asks for is passed to Cashfree and not stored by us. If you buy Plus in the Android app, Google Play takes the payment and sends us the order id and purchase status, not your payment details.
  • Consent records. What you agreed to, and when.

Chotu, Jeetu and the pod friends

Chotu (the study buddy), Jeetu (the method coach) and the pod friends (Meera, Arjun, Ira and Kabir) are computer programs, not people. When you ask Chotu or Jeetu online, the messages in that chat are sent to a language-model provider to write a reply. They never see your journal, mood, nickname or stage. Messages that look like distress are not sent; the app shows help instead.

The providers we use are Google (Gemini), Groq and NVIDIA. Some of them run on free or developer tiers whose terms let the provider keep and review what is sent, for safety and to improve their services, so the provider may keep your messages for a time under its own terms. Please don't put anything personal in a question to Chotu.

Your Chotu chats

Your chats with Chotu come to Aspiran and are used to improve the app. This applies to every member and there is no switch for it. Agreeing to the terms at sign-up covers it. If you joined earlier, when we said chats stayed on your phone, the app shows you a notice once before any of your chats are sent.

  • What we keep. Your messages to Chotu and Chotu's replies, from online Chotu and, in the Android app, from Chotu on your phone, under your random member id.
  • Why. To improve Aspiran: the team reads chats to fix wrong or unhelpful replies and make Chotu and the app better. For Plus members, your recent chats also go into your study profile (below).
  • How we protect them. Kept securely on our servers, never sold, and never shown to coaching centres or other members.
  • Distress. Messages that look like distress are not kept as chats; they follow our care steps (Chotu points you to real people and helplines).
  • How long. 180 days, then they are deleted. "Download my data" includes them; "Delete everything" deletes them at once.

Your study profile (Plus)

For Plus members, once a night a language model writes a short study profile from your own study counts: what you did, what looks weak and your plan, from the same codes and numbers described above under "What we count while you study". Your recent Chotu chats go into it too. Chotu (online and on your phone) and Jeetu read it so their help fits you. It never says anything about your feelings, rank, results or other people. It is included in "Download my data" and deleted with your account.

Fair use

Online Chotu has a daily number of chats for free members. To keep it working for everyone, we may pause online Chotu for the rest of the day for anyone using it in a way it isn't meant for (very many messages, messages sent by a script, many accounts on one device, or a long run of questions unrelated to study). For this we keep counts, times and a scrambled code for your device for up to 8 days, never what you wrote.

On our website (aspiran.in), we save what you type to Chotu and its replies, with a random visitor id and no name unless you give one, so we can improve Chotu and the site. We keep these chats for 180 days, then delete them. Messages that look like distress are not saved. To have yours deleted sooner, write to hello@aspiran.in.

When you send an answer to Answer Studio, the photo or text goes to the same providers to be marked. We don't store the photo; we keep only a count of how many answers you sent this week, for the free allowance.

Who we share with

Only the services that run the app: Google Firebase (sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting and storage), PostHog (usage analytics, described above), the language-model providers for Chotu and Jeetu's online replies, study profiles and answer marking (Google, Groq and NVIDIA), and Cashfree and Google Play (payments), and Resend (sending account emails). We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your rights

We keep your data while you have an account, and delete it when you delete your account.

You can export your data or withdraw consent at any time in the app (Android: Me → My Path; web: Me → Privacy).

Delete your account

In the Android app: Me → My Path → Delete my account and data. On the web: Me → Privacy → Delete everything. Either one deletes, at once, your account and sign-in, your pod membership, your pod messages, your consent records, any Chotu chats we kept and your study profile, cuts the link between you and the usage counts, and clears what the app keeps on that phone or browser. Reports other members made stay, with your name and text removed, so moderators can keep a record.

Can't open the app? Write to hello@aspiran.in from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Aspiran account". We delete everything above within 7 days and write back when it's done. Payment records for Plus are kept for as long as Indian tax law requires.

For anything else, or to raise a grievance, write to our Grievance Officer at hello@aspiran.in. We reply within 7 days.

Age

Aspiran is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change applies.
