Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

Aspiran is a study companion for UPSC aspirants. This page says what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. SnapSkill LLP ("we") is the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What stays on your device

Your journal, mood check-ins, Close the Day notes, profile details beyond the four matching fields, your roadmap and goal answers, and your focus logs stay on your phone or in your browser. We never receive them.

What we keep on our servers

  • Your account. Sign-in is with Google only. We keep your Google account id and email address, to sign you in and to help if you contact us.
  • Pod matching. A random member id and four fields (stage, attempt band, language, time band), so we can place you in a pod.
  • Pod messages. What you post in your pod, so the pod can read it, and reports and moderation notes when something is reported.
  • Usage counts. Which features were used and when (for example "joined a pod" or "built a roadmap"), one-tap ratings and the day-7 check-in answers. Never the text of your mood, journal or chats. We use no advertising SDKs and sell nothing.
  • Usage analytics (PostHog). Which screens you open, what you tap, errors the app hits, and a replay of how a session went, sent to PostHog, an analytics service, under your random member id (never your name or email). In replays every word on the screen and everything you type is hidden, so we see where people tap and get stuck, not what they read or write. Your pod, rooms, Chotu's Talk, Calm, Close the Day, Your room and Me are never recorded. You can switch this off in Me → Privacy → Usage analytics (in the Android app: Me → Privacy). On our website and public topic pages PostHog runs without cookies and without an id that follows you between visits.
  • Payments. For Plus: the order id, amount, status and Cashfree's payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details go to Cashfree, not to us. The mobile number Cashfree asks for is passed to Cashfree and not stored by us. If you buy Plus in the Android app, Google Play takes the payment and sends us the order id and purchase status, not your payment details.
  • Consent records. What you agreed to, and when.

Chotu and the pod friends

When you ask Chotu online, the messages in that chat are sent to an AI provider to write a reply. Chotu never sees your journal, mood, nickname or stage. Messages that look like distress are not sent; the app shows help instead. Chotu and the pod friends (Meera, Arjun, Ira and Kabir) are AI, and are always labelled "AI".

The AI providers we use are Google (Gemini), Groq and NVIDIA. Some of them run on free or developer tiers whose terms let the provider keep and review what is sent, for safety and to improve their services. We don't save your Chotu chats in the app, but the provider may keep them for a time under its own terms. Please don't put anything personal in a question to Chotu.

On our website (aspiran.in), we save what you type to Chotu and its replies, with a random visitor id and no name unless you give one, so we can improve Chotu and the site. We keep these chats for 180 days, then delete them. Messages that look like distress are not saved. To have yours deleted sooner, write to hello@aspiran.in.

When you send an answer to Answer Studio, the photo or text goes to the same providers to be marked. We don't store the photo; we keep only a count of how many answers you sent this week, for the free allowance.

Who we share with

Only the services that run the app: Google Firebase (sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting and storage), PostHog (usage analytics, described above), the AI providers for Chotu's online replies and answer marking (Google, Groq and NVIDIA), and Cashfree and Google Play (payments), and Resend (sending account emails). We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your rights

We keep your data while you have an account, and delete it when you delete your account.

You can export your data or withdraw consent at any time in the app (Android: Me → My Path; web: Me → Privacy).

Delete your account

In the Android app: Me → My Path → Delete my account and data. On the web: Me → Privacy → Delete everything. Either one deletes, at once, your account and sign-in, your pod membership, your pod messages and your consent records, cuts the link between you and the usage counts, and clears what the app keeps on that phone or browser. Reports other members made stay, with your name and text removed, so moderators can keep a record.

Can't open the app? Write to hello@aspiran.in from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Aspiran account". We delete everything above within 7 days and write back when it's done. Payment records for Plus are kept for as long as Indian tax law requires.

For anything else, or to raise a grievance, write to our Grievance Officer at hello@aspiran.in. We reply within 7 days.

Age

Aspiran is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change applies.
