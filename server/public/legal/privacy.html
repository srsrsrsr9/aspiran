Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026.

UPSC Pods is a study companion for UPSC aspirants. This page says what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. SnapSkill LLP ("we") is the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What stays on your device

Your journal, mood check-ins, Close the Day notes, profile details beyond the four matching fields, your roadmap and goal answers, and your focus logs stay on your phone or in your browser. We never receive them.

What we keep on our servers

  • Your account. Sign-in is with Google only. We keep your Google account id and email address, to sign you in and to help if you contact us.
  • Pod matching. A random member id and four fields (stage, attempt band, language, time band), so we can place you in a pod.
  • Pod messages. What you post in your pod, so the pod can read it, and reports and moderation notes when something is reported.
  • Usage counts. Which features were used and when (for example "joined a pod" or "built a roadmap"), one-tap ratings and the day-7 check-in answers. Never the text of your mood, journal or chats. We use no third-party trackers or advertising SDKs.
  • Payments. For Plus: the order id, amount, status and Cashfree's payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details go to Cashfree, not to us. The mobile number Cashfree asks for is passed to Cashfree and not stored by us. If you buy Plus in the Android app, Google Play takes the payment and sends us the order id and purchase status, not your payment details.
  • Consent records. What you agreed to, and when.

Chotu and the pod friends

When you ask Chotu online, the messages in that chat are sent to an AI provider to write a reply. Chotu never sees your journal, mood, nickname or stage. Messages that look like distress are not sent; the app shows help instead. Chotu and the pod friends (Meera, Arjun, Pooja and Kabir) are AI, and are always labelled "AI".

The AI providers we use are Google (Gemini), Groq and NVIDIA. Some of them run on free or developer tiers whose terms let the provider keep and review what is sent, for safety and to improve their services. We don't save your Chotu chats, but the provider may keep them for a time under its own terms. Please don't put anything personal in a question to Chotu.

When you send an answer to Answer Studio, the photo or text goes to the same providers to be marked. We don't store the photo; we keep only a count of how many answers you sent this week, for the free allowance.

Who we share with

Only the services that run the app: Google Firebase (sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting and storage), the AI providers for Chotu's online replies and answer marking (Google, Groq and NVIDIA), and Cashfree and Google Play (payments), and Resend (sending account emails, such as password recovery). We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your rights

We keep your data while you have an account, and delete it when you delete your account.

You can export or delete your data at any time in the app: Me → Privacy. Deleting removes your server data, your pod membership and your sign-in account. You can withdraw consent the same way. For anything else, or to raise a grievance, write to our Grievance Officer at hello@aspiran.in. We reply within 7 days.

Age

UPSC Pods is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change applies.
