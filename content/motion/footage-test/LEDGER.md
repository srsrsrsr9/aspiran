Not used, and why:
- NASA "Monsoons: Wet, Dry, Repeat" and the NASA SVS monsoon globes (public domain): they draw national borders on
  India, which our map rule doesn't allow (and India's official outline differs).
- Pragyan rotation clip: ISRO title cards take up most of it.
- Two CC BY-SA pictures (Supreme Court) mean the finished video should carry "CC BY-SA 4.0" too if we publish it.
  For a real series, prefer public-domain or GODL-India pictures.

Diagrams (monsoon heat, winds, hills, the 3/4 ring) are drawn by `content/tools/story/explainer.py`: schematic, no maps.
Sound effects (whooshes, pulse, drone) are made from tones and noise in the same tool: nothing to license.
