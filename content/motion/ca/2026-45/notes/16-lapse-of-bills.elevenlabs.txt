=== Dissolving the Lok Sabha kills some bills, not all (NEUTRAL) ===
When the Lok Sabha is dissolved, do all pending bills die? Not all of them.
Article one-oh-seven sets the rule. A bill pending in the Lok Sabha lapses. So does one the Lok Sabha passed that is pending in the Rajya Sabha.
But a bill pending in the Rajya Sabha, not yet passed by the Lok Sabha, survives.
Article one-oh-eight adds one more exception. If the President notified a joint sitting before the dissolution, that sitting can still be held, and the bill passed.
And prorogation, which only ends a session, makes no bill lapse.
So ask where the bill is sitting, not how old it is.
