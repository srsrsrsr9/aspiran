=== A new GDP base year can change growth without changing output (LIGHT) ===
India's economy grew nine point two percent in twenty twenty-three twenty-four. Now the official figure says seven point two.
The economy didn't change. The measuring tape did.
On the twenty-seventh of February, twenty twenty-six, the statistics office moved G D P's base year from twenty eleven twelve to twenty twenty-two twenty-three.
Rebasing updates the prices and weights used to add up output, and brings in new data, like G S T records and fresh surveys.
So some years went down, and some went up: twenty twenty-four twenty-five rose from six point five to seven point one percent.
Same farms, same factories. A new ruler.
