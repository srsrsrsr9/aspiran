=== Seats are frozen until a census, not a date (NEUTRAL) ===
When does the Lok Sabha seat freeze end? In twenty twenty-six?
Not quite. The Constitution ties it to a census, not a date.
Article eighty-two says seats need not be readjusted until figures from the first census taken after twenty twenty-six are published.
Until then, seats among states rest on the nineteen seventy-one census.
The eighty-fourth Amendment, in two thousand one, set that rule. The eighty-seventh, in two thousand three, let boundaries be redrawn on the two thousand one census.
The next census counts people as of the first of March, twenty twenty-seven, its reference date.
In April twenty twenty-six, a bill to remove that link failed to get the two-thirds majority it needed.
So the clock is a census, not the calendar.
