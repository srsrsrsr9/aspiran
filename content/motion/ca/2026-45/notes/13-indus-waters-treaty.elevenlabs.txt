=== The Indus Waters Treaty has no exit clause for one side (SERIOUS) ===
Can one country exit the Indus Waters Treaty by itself?
Signed in nineteen sixty, it gives India the eastern rivers: the Sutlej, the Bee-aas and the Raa-vi. Pakistan, the western rivers: the Indus, the Jhay-lum and the Chi-naab.
Under Article twelve, it can be changed or ended only by a new treaty, ratified for that purpose.
On the twenty-third of April, twenty twenty-five, India said it would hold the treaty in abeyance, until Pakistan abjures support for cross-border terrorism.
The next day, Pakistan said the treaty has no provision for unilateral suspension.
On the thirty-first of August, twenty twenty-six, a Court of Arbitration held that the treaty remains in force. India rejected the award.
One text. Two positions.
