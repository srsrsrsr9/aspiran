=== India's carbon market has no cap (NEUTRAL) ===
India now has a carbon market. So it must cap total emissions, right?
It doesn't. There is no cap on the total.
The Carbon Credit Trading Scheme, notified in twenty twenty-three, under the Energy Conservation Act, sets emission intensity targets: emissions per tonne of product.
Beat your target, and you earn carbon credit certificates. Miss it, and you must buy them.
Targets are notified for sectors like cement, aluminium, paper and refineries. As of November twenty twenty-six, iron and steel is still a draft.
So a plant can grow, and its total emissions can rise, while it still meets its target.
The scheme limits how dirty each tonne is, not how much is made.
