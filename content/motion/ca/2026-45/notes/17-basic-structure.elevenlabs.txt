=== Parliament can amend almost anything, but not the Constitution's identity (NEUTRAL) ===
Parliament can amend the Constitution, even fundamental rights. So can it rewrite the whole thing?
No. Not its basic structure.
That line was drawn in Kay-sha-vaa-nanda Bhaa-rati, on the twenty-fourth of April, nineteen seventy-three, by thirteen judges, the largest bench the Court has sat.
By seven to six, it held that Parliament may amend any part, but cannot alter the Constitution's basic structure.
It overruled Go-lak-naath, which had said fundamental rights could not be amended.
In Minerva Mills, in nineteen eighty, the Court added: a limited amending power is itself part of that structure.
Amend the text, yes. Change its identity, no.
