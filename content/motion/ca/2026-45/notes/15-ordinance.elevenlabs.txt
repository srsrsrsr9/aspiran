=== An ordinance can't be renewed forever (NEUTRAL) ===
An ordinance is a law made while Parliament is in recess. Can a government just keep renewing it?
No. The Supreme Court called that a fraud on the Constitution.
Under Article one twenty-three, an ordinance must be laid before both Houses. It ceases six weeks after Parliament reassembles, or sooner, if both Houses disapprove it.
In Krishna Kumar Singh, in twenty seventeen, a seven-judge bench examined ordinances re-issued again and again in Bihar.
The majority held that re-promulgation is a fraud on the Constitution, that laying an ordinance before the legislature is mandatory, and that the satisfaction behind it is open to judicial review.
So an ordinance is a bridge, not a road.
