Can one country exit the Indus Waters Treaty by itself? Signed in nineteen sixty, it gives India the eastern rivers: the Sutlej, the Beas and the Ravi. Pakistan, the western rivers: the Indus, the Jhelum and the Chenab. Under Article twelve, it can be changed or ended only by a new treaty, ratified for that purpose. On the twenty-third of April, twenty twenty-five, India said it would hold the treaty in abeyance, until Pakistan abjures support for cross-border terrorism. The next day, Pakistan said the treaty has no provision for unilateral suspension. On the thirty-first of August, twenty twenty-six, a Court of Arbitration held that the treaty remains in force. India rejected the award. One text. Two positions. Your turn. Which rivers go to India under the treaty?
