=== A Money Bill and a Financial Bill are not the same thing (NEUTRAL) ===
Every Money Bill deals with money. But not every bill about money is a Money Bill.
Article one-ten says a Money Bill contains only provisions on matters like taxes, government borrowing, and the Consolidated Fund.
If there's a doubt, the Speaker's decision is final, and the Speaker's certificate goes on the bill.
Under Article one-oh-nine, the Rajya Sabha gets fourteen days. It can only recommend. The Lok Sabha may accept or reject those recommendations.
A Financial Bill under Article one-seventeen also covers these matters, but not only these.
It too needs the President's recommendation, and can't start in the Rajya Sabha. But after that, both Houses must agree to it.
