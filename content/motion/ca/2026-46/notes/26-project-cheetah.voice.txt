India's cheetahs didn't just come back. A court had to say yes first. The cheetah was declared extinct in India in nineteen fifty-two. In January twenty twenty, the Supreme Court allowed African cheetahs to be brought to a suitable habitat. It called this an introduction, not a re-introduction, and set up an expert committee to guide it. The first eight came from Namibia, released at Kuno in September twenty twenty-two. Twelve more came from South Africa in twenty twenty-three. As of September twenty twenty-six, India has over fifty cheetahs, most of them born here. Permission came first. The work came after. Your turn. Where did the first cheetahs come from?
