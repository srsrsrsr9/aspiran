=== Western disturbances bring winter rain the monsoon doesn't (LIGHT) ===
India's rain comes with the monsoon. So why does Delhi get rain in winter?
Blame a storm from the Mediterranean.
A western disturbance is an extratropical storm, born over the Mediterranean or Caspian Sea.
The westerlies carry it east, across Iran, Afghanistan and Pakistan, into India.
When it meets the Himalayas, it brings rain to the plains and heavy snow to the hills.
That winter rain matters for rabi crops. Too much can damage them, and bring landslides and avalanches.
The monsoon is tropical, and carries its moisture low. A western disturbance carries it higher up.
Different storm. Different season.
