=== 1857 lost the war, and still ended Company rule (NEUTRAL) ===
The revolt of eighteen fifty-seven was crushed. Yet it ended the East India Company's rule.
On the tenth of May, eighteen fifty-seven, sepoys at Meerut rose, and marched to Delhi.
Rebels proclaimed Bahadur Shah Zafar, the last Mughal ruler, their emperor.
The trigger: new cartridges, reportedly greased with cow and pig fat. Behind it: annexations, and long grievances.
Why did it fail? It stayed mostly in the north and centre, with no single plan or leader, and many rulers sided with a better-armed British force.
In eighteen fifty-eight, an Act of Parliament moved power from the Company to the Crown.
A lost revolt. A changed Raj.
