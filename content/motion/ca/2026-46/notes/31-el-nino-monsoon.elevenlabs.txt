=== El Niño usually weakens the monsoon, but not always (NEUTRAL) ===
El Nino means a weak monsoon. Mostly. Not always.
El Nino is unusual warming of the sea surface in the central and eastern Pacific. La Nina is unusual cooling.
El Nino tends to weaken India's June-to-September monsoon; La Nina tends to strengthen it.
But in nineteen ninety-seven, one of the strongest El Ninos, India's monsoon rain was about normal.
A positive Indian Ocean Dipole, a warmer western Indian Ocean, can push back.
That's why I M D's forecasts watch both oceans.
A signal, not a sentence.
