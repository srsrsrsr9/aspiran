Can a court give a Governor a deadline to decide on a bill? Article two hundred gives the Governor options: assent, withhold assent, or reserve the bill for the President. It sets no fixed time limit. In April twenty twenty-five, a two-judge bench set timelines, and treated ten Tamil Nadu bills as having received assent. The President then sought the Court's opinion under Article one forty-three. In November twenty twenty-five, five judges advised: courts cannot fix timelines, and there is no deemed assent. But a Governor cannot sit on a bill indefinitely. For prolonged inaction, a court may direct a decision. No fixed clock. But no endless wait. Your turn. Which Article covers the Governor's assent to bills?
