=== India's data law gives you rights, and the rules decide when (NEUTRAL) ===
India passed its data protection law in twenty twenty-three. Most of it still isn't in force.
It gives you, the Data Principal, rights: to access your data, correct or erase it, seek redress, and nominate someone.
Whoever decides why and how your data is processed is a Data Fiduciary, and owes you those duties.
But the Act lets the government switch on different parts on different dates.
The rules came in November twenty twenty-five. The Data Protection Board part started at once.
Consent managers come a year later. Most duties, after eighteen months: around May twenty twenty-seven.
The rights are written. The rules set the clock.
