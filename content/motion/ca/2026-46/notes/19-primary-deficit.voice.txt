The fiscal deficit gets the headlines: four point three percent of G D P in the twenty twenty-six twenty-seven budget. But much of that gap pays for the past. The primary deficit is the fiscal deficit minus interest payments. It shows the gap from today's spending alone. The same budget puts it at just zero point seven percent of G D P. So about three point six points of the deficit is interest. The medium-term anchor is debt: about fifty percent of G D P, give or take one point, by March twenty thirty-one. Shrink the primary deficit, and new borrowing mostly services old debt. Your turn. What is the primary deficit?
