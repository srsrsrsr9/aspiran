=== A bill can become law even if the Rajya Sabha says no (NEUTRAL) ===
Can a bill become law if the Rajya Sabha rejects it? For an ordinary bill, yes.
A bill must pass both Houses, then get the President's assent.
If one House rejects it, if they finally disagree on amendments, or if six months pass, the President can call a joint sitting.
There, a majority of members of both Houses, present and voting, decides.
It never applies to a Money Bill, and constitutional amendments can't go to one.
It has happened only three times: in nineteen sixty-one, nineteen seventy-eight, and two thousand two.
Two Houses. One way out of a deadlock.
