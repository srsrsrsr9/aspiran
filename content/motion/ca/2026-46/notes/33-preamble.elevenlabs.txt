=== Two words in the Preamble weren't there in 1949 (NEUTRAL) ===
The Preamble opens with: We, the people of India. The authority starts with us.
It calls India a sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic.
But socialist and secular weren't in the text adopted on the twenty-sixth of November, nineteen forty-nine. The forty-second Amendment added them in nineteen seventy-six.
Then come four aims: justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity.
In Kesavananda Bharati, in nineteen seventy-three, the Supreme Court treated the Preamble as part of the Constitution.
In November twenty twenty-four, it dismissed challenges to the nineteen seventy-six words: the amending power extends to the Preamble.
Read it word by word. Some words have dates.
Your turn. On which date was the Constitution adopted?
