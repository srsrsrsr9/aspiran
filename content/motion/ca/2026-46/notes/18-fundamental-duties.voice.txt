No court can force you to perform a Fundamental Duty. So why do judges keep citing them? Article fifty-one A lists the duties of every citizen. The forty-second Amendment added ten of them, in nineteen seventy-six. The eighty-sixth Amendment, in two thousand two, added an eleventh: parents and guardians must provide chances for education to children aged six to fourteen. The duties can't be enforced directly by a court. But in the A I I M S Students' Union case, the Supreme Court called them as important as rights, and a guide to interpreting laws. Courts have also read the duty to protect the environment into environmental cases. Not a command. A lens. Your turn. Which amendment added Article fifty-one A to the Constitution?
