=== The WTO's peace clause shields India's grain stocks, for now (NEUTRAL) ===
India buys grain at support prices, and stores it for food security.
Under W T O farm rules, that support has a cap: ten percent of the crop's value, priced at nineteen eighty-six to eighty-eight prices.
Cross it, and India could face a W T O dispute. Unless the peace clause applies.
Agreed at Bali in twenty thirteen, and extended in twenty fourteen until a permanent solution is found, it stops members from challenging staple-crop stockholding in a dispute.
India first invoked it in twenty twenty, for rice, and has invoked it again since.
But there is still no permanent solution, not even at the W T O's March twenty twenty-six ministerial meeting.
A shield, not a settlement.
