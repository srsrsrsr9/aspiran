=== India's inflation target is a band, not a number (NEUTRAL) ===
India's inflation target is four percent. But the R B I isn't told to hit exactly four.
The target comes with a band: two to six percent, on retail inflation.
Under the R B I Act, the government sets it with the R B I, every five years.
In March twenty twenty-six, it kept the target at four percent, plus or minus two, until March twenty thirty-one.
Stay outside the band for three quarters in a row, and the R B I must report to the government: why, what it will do, and by when.
It sent its first such report in November twenty twenty-two.
The number is the aim. The band is the accountability.
