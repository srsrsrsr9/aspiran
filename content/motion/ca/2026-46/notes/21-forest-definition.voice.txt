What counts as a forest in India? In twenty twenty-three, Parliament wrote a narrower answer. In nineteen ninety-six, in Godavarman, the Supreme Court read forest in its dictionary sense, whatever the ownership. The twenty twenty-three law covers land notified as forest, or recorded as forest since the twenty-fifth of October, nineteen eighty. Unrecorded land that looks like forest may fall outside it. But in February twenty twenty-four, the Court ordered that Godavarman still applies while states record forest-like land. In September twenty twenty-six, it agreed to examine whether the new definition is valid. Narrower in the Act. Wider, for now, in court. Your turn. Under Godavarman, what meaning does forest take?
