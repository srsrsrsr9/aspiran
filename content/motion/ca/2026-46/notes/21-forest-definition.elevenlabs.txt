=== After 2023, "forest" means something narrower on paper (SERIOUS) ===
What counts as a forest in India? In twenty twenty-three, Parliament wrote a narrower answer.
In nineteen ninety-six, in Go-daa-var-man, the Supreme Court read forest in its dictionary sense, whatever the ownership.
The twenty twenty-three law covers land notified as forest, or recorded as forest since the twenty-fifth of October, nineteen eighty.
Unrecorded land that looks like forest may fall outside it.
But in February twenty twenty-four, the Court ordered that Go-daa-var-man still applies while states record forest-like land.
In September twenty twenty-six, it agreed to examine whether the new definition is valid.
Narrower in the Act. Wider, for now, in court.
