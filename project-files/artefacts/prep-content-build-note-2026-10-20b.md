## What editors should check first
### Games (polity, economy, history, ethics, mains)
- Mnemonic "SSSDR" for the Preamble is just its initials, not taken from a source; check the Hindi label too.
- Atal Pension Yojana: income-tax payers barred since Oct 2022; replaced Swavalamban.
- Global Hunger Index: India in the "serious" band in recent editions. Gender Gap: weakest sub-indices are economic participation and health.
- Committee report years: Malhotra 1994, Nachiket Mor 2014, Bimal Jalan 2019. Hartog year left blank; Sargent Plan 1944 is the report year.
- Champaran: wording that the order against Gandhi "was soon withdrawn".
### Games (IR, art, science, society, ancient, geography, environment)
- Foreign travellers timeline: years are approximate (Faxian 399, Xuanzang 629 = departure years; Al-Biruni c. 1030; Ibn Battuta 1333 arrival).
- Groupings flashcards: BIMSTEC secretariat Dhaka, IORA Mauritius; African Union joined G20 in 2023.
- Daughters' coparcenary rights: cites Vineeta Sharma (2020). Fission vs fusion: India is an ITER member.
- Folk dance pairings that could be argued: Theyyam (ritual form, Kerala), Dumhal (J&K), Gaur Maria (Chhattisgarh).
- Confirm the NCERT Class XI art book title "An Introduction to Indian Art".
### Long reads
- India and its neighbourhood: the Indus Waters Treaty is described historically with no current status. India put the treaty in abeyance in April 2025, so add a status line before approving. Also check: the Ganga treaty (1996, 30 years) and Bhutan not ratifying the BBIN motor vehicles pact.
- GST: only the 2017 launch slabs are given, noted as since revised; compensation runs to June 2022; check 14% growth on a 2015-16 base.
- Elections: Model Code of Conduct from Kerala 1960; first EVMs at Paravur 1982; first VVPAT in Nagaland 2013; multi-member commission 1989-90.
- Mauryan: Rock Edict XIII figures; Maski edict names "Ashoka"; Brihadratha c. 185 BCE.
- Temple architecture: Brihadeshwara vimana about 66 m, completed c. 1010.
- Safeguards: Punjab v Davinder Singh (2024) on sub-classification; 106th Amendment includes Delhi.
- Climate: 2022 updated NDC figures and the 2070 net-zero goal are stated as current. Biotechnology: cry gene names, ADA 1990 case.
### Mocks (polity 3, modern history 2, world geography 1, economy 2)
- History: N.G. Ranga as AIKS general secretary in 1936; Tana Bhagat "from about 1914"; Muslim League "fared poorly in 1937, even in Muslim seats".
- Geography: Atlas as a young fold range of the Alpine phase; Kalahari trap wording; Gobi as a mid-latitude desert.
- Economy: NFSA maternity benefit of at least Rs 6,000 (Section 4); IIP from the NSO; Eight Core index from the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT.
- Trap explanations that state side facts (e.g. "Cornwallis left India in 1793") need a quick look.
### Mocks (CSAT, art, society)
- CSAT papers are filed as "prelims-gs1" (the only allowed value), labelled CSAT Paper II in the section name. Hindi coding options read "कूट: BQQMF".
- Art: Purandara Dasa as "Pitamaha" of Carnatic music; Bidri blackening soil; Nuakhai, Hemis and Bonalu placements; UNESCO years (Kumbh 2017, Durga Puja 2021, Garba 2023).
- Society: Census 2011 sex ratios (Haryana 879, Punjab 895, Kerala 1,084); SC 16.6%, ST 8.6%; Jains notified as a minority in 2014; 106th Amendment takes effect after delimitation.
