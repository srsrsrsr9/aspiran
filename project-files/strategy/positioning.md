# Positioning: community first

_11 Oct 2026, from the "Community-first repositioning" thread, for the Marketing plan thread (landing page and video). Detail behind it: [community-first-spec.md](/mnt/project-files/strategy/community-first-spec.md)._

## The one line

**The free adda for UPSC aspirants. Your people, your pod, and everything you need to prep, without paying a rupee. Go Plus when you're ready to go all in.**

Shorter, for video and ads: **"Never prep alone."** / **"अकेले तैयारी मत करो।"** (native speaker to check)

## What we are

A community first, an app second. You land among people, not on a task list. The study engine is there, and it's free to start, but it's the second thing you see.

## The two spaces (say them in this order)

1. **Adda: your people.** A small pod of 6 to 8 aspirants at your stage, a Commons where hundreds study together live, Chotu (a chai-cup AI who keeps you company and runs your pod's rituals), Close the Day, Calm now, and a shelf of free honest tools (Attempt Compass, Cost-of-a-Year, 5-source maps, routine builder). **Free, no card needed. No ads and no upsells inside the community.**
2. **Prep: your study engine.** Today's 3, daily Chai news cards with a UPSC lens, 70 practice formats, mocks, long reads, Focus mode. Free to use; **Plus** adds unlimited answer feedback, all mocks with breakdowns, a personal plan and more of Chotu.

## Proof points we can say (all true today or by launch)

- Free, no card needed: pods, the Commons, Chotu, Close the Day, Calm, the honest tools, distress support.
- Every news card is checked by a human editor, in English and Hindi.
- Hindi and English from day one; Telugu in pilot.
- No leaderboards, no streaks to lose, no countdowns. "You showed up. That counts."
- Chotu is always labelled AI. No fake members.
- Prices include everything; cancel in two taps.

## Say / don't say

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Find your people" | "Crack UPSC", "Guaranteed selection", "Topper's secret" |
| "Free, no card needed" (for the Adda list above) | "Free forever" (dropped by the consumer-protection check), or "Free" for Prep features that have limits |
| "Your pod" | "Join 1 lakh+ aspirants" until it's true |
| "Honest tools" | Rank or result claims (CCPA misleading-ads rules) |
| "Chotu, your AI chai cup" | "Your AI mentor" or anything that implies a human |

## For the video brief

- Lead with the feeling: 11 pm, alone in a PG room, then a pod pings. Community before content.
- Show Chotu (the kulhad with steam), labelled "AI".
- Show the free shelf briefly as "and all of this is free".
- End on "Never prep alone" and the Play Store or web link. Plus can appear only as one closing line, if at all.
- Names "Adda" and "Chotu" are working names until the name test with about 10 users.
