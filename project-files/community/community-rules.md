# Community rules

> **DRAFT for human review. Not approved. Do not use with members until the safety lead and a qualified mental-health professional have signed off.**

Version 0.1 · drafted 11 Oct 2026

UPSC Pods is a place to prepare together, honestly and kindly. Most of us have been through a hard result, a long year, or a family conversation we dreaded. These rules exist so that this stays a place where you can say "I'm on my fourth attempt" or "I'm struggling" and be met with respect.

## The short list

1. **Be honest about results.** No inflated claims about ranks, marks or selections, yours or anyone else's.
2. **No selling in pods.** Don't sell, promote or recruit for courses, notes, test series or anything else.
3. **Say if you have a commercial tie.** If you work for, are paid by, or get a referral fee from any coaching, publisher or service you mention, say so.
4. **A post about distress always gets a human.** If someone is struggling, reply kindly and let a moderator know. Never mock or ignore it.
5. **No comparison-bait.** No rank bragging, no shaming, no "how are you still on attempt 5?"
6. **Ask before you share someone's work.** No screenshots of other people's answers, messages or profiles without their consent.
7. **Respect everyone.** No insults or stereotypes about caste, religion, region, gender, language, medium, age or attempt number.
8. **Discuss politics like a civil servant.** Policies, both sides, evidence. No party campaigning.
9. **No piracy.** Don't share paid material you don't have the right to share.
10. **No professional advice you are not qualified to give.** Share experience, not medical or legal advice presented as professional.

If you see something that breaks these rules, report it. If a rule is used against you unfairly, you can appeal. Details below.

## The rules in detail

### 1. Be honest about results

UPSC is full of inflated claims. We don't add to them.

- Don't claim a rank, score or selection you didn't get.
- Don't post or forward "toppers from our pod" claims without the person's consent and proof.
- If you're sharing a mock test score, it's your score on that mock. Don't present it as a prediction.
- Mentors and pod leads who have been selected may show it on their profile. The team checks it first.

### 2. No selling in pods

Pods are for preparing, not for selling.

- No links to paid courses, test series, notes or Telegram channels that sell things.
- No "DM me for notes" or recruiting into another group or coaching.
- No affiliate links.
- This applies to members, pod leads, mentors and the UPSC Pods team. The app does not show paid offers in pods.

If you want to recommend a paid resource because it genuinely helped you, you may name it in the Resource Court thread, with your reasons, and with any commercial tie disclosed (rule 3).

### 3. Say if you have a commercial tie

If you mention a coaching institute, publisher, app, mentor or service, and you have any link to it, say so in the same post.

A link means any of these: you work there, you teach there, you're paid by them, you get a referral fee or free access in return for promoting them, or a close family member owns it.

> Example: "I used X's test series. Disclosure: I teach part-time at X."

UPSC Pods follows the same rule. If we recommend our own product, we say so.

### 4. A post about distress always gets a human

Everyone has hard days here. Some days are much harder than that.

- If someone posts that they are struggling, reply kindly or let your pod lead or a moderator know. Use the "Get help for this post" option.
- Never mock, meme or dismiss a distress post.
- Don't try to be their counsellor. Be kind, and let the wellbeing team take it from there.
- Don't share their post outside the pod.
- If you're the one struggling, you can post in the pod, message your pod lead privately, or tap "Talk to a person". Helpline numbers are pinned in the app.

Some posts are checked automatically for signs of distress. A match never blocks or deletes your post. It only asks a human moderator to look, and shows you helpline numbers. You will never get an automatic reply pretending to be a person.

### 5. No comparison-bait

Comparison is already the loudest voice in UPSC preparation. We turn it down.

- No posting ranks, marks or "AIR" to show off in pods. Celebrate wins in the wins thread, where people choose to look.
- No shaming anyone's attempt number, age, medium, optional or speed.
- No "if you can't do X you're not serious" posts.
- No posts designed to make people anxious: fake cut-offs, scare stories, "only 3 days left and you haven't..."

Repeaters are some of the most experienced people here. Attempt tags are optional and are a mark of experience, never a reason to look down on someone.

### 6. Ask before you share someone's work

- Don't screenshot or forward someone else's answer, essay, message, profile or mood post without their clear consent.
- Peer feedback stays in the pod unless the writer says it can go further.
- If you want to use someone's answer as an example, ask first, and remove their name unless they want it there.
- Don't share anyone's personal details: phone number, address, college, coaching, workplace, family details.
- Don't record voice or video rooms without everyone's agreement.

### 7. Respect everyone

Our members come from every state, every background and every medium. That's a strength.

- No slurs, insults, jokes or stereotypes about caste, religion, region, gender, sexuality, disability, language, medium, age or attempt number.
- No sexual comments, unwanted messages or pressure to meet. Report it, and we will act quickly.
- Hindi-medium, English-medium and regional-medium aspirants are equally welcome. Don't mock someone's English or Hindi.
- Debate ideas. Never attack the person.

### 8. Discuss politics like a civil servant

GS and the interview need you to understand politics. Party loyalty doesn't belong in a pod.

- Discussing policies, laws, judgments, government schemes and current affairs is welcome.
- Give more than one side. A good GS answer does, and so should a good post.
- Back claims with sources.
- No campaigning for or against any party or leader. No election appeals, slogans or party propaganda.
- No posts that target a community or invite hatred, even framed as "just discussion".
- Moderators may close a thread that turns into a fight. That's not taking a side.

### 9. No piracy

- Don't share PDFs, recordings, test series or notes that someone has paid for, unless the owner allows it.
- Free and public material (NCERTs, government reports, PIB, Yojana, upsc.gov.in) is fine to share with a link.
- Your own notes are yours to share.

### 10. No professional advice you are not qualified to give

- Share your experience: "This helped me sleep before Prelims."
- Don't tell someone what medicine to take, how to treat a health problem, or what to do in a legal dispute, as if you were a professional.
- If you are a doctor, lawyer or counsellor, you are welcome here as an aspirant. Please don't give professional advice in pods. Point people to a proper service instead.
- Nothing in UPSC Pods is medical, psychological or legal advice. That includes Chotu, our AI helper.

## Reports and appeals

### How to report

- Tap the three dots on any post or message and choose "Report". Pick a reason.
- Reports are private. The person you report is not told who reported them.
- For anything about safety or distress, use "Get help for this post" instead. It goes straight to the wellbeing team.
- For anything urgent and dangerous, call 112. (Verify before publishing.)

### What happens next

- A moderator reviews every report. The target time for a first look is **[__ hours, team to set]**.
- They may do nothing, remove a post, or apply a step from the ladder below.
- We tell you when your report has been dealt with. We may not share the details, to protect everyone's privacy.

### How to appeal

- If a moderator takes action against you, you'll be told what rule it was and what happens next.
- You can appeal within **14 days** through "Appeal this decision" in your notifications.
- A different moderator, not the one who decided, reviews your appeal. If it's a removal from the community, the safety lead reviews it.
- We aim to reply within **[__ days, team to set]**.
- We may keep a temporary step in place while the appeal is reviewed.

## Consequences ladder

Most people who break a rule do it once and by accident. We start small.

1. **A friendly reminder.** Usually a private message from your pod lead or a moderator.
2. **A formal warning.** Recorded, with the rule named.
3. **A short mute.** You can read but not post, for 24 hours.
4. **A longer mute or pod move.** Up to 7 days, or moving to a different pod.
5. **Removal from the community.** For repeated or serious breaks.

Some things skip straight to step 4 or 5:

- Threats, harassment or sexual misconduct.
- Sharing someone's personal details (doxxing).
- Mocking or encouraging someone who has said they want to harm themselves.
- Selling to, or recruiting, a member who is in distress.
- Hate speech against a community.

Steps fade. A reminder or warning stops counting after **[__ months, team to set]** with no further problems.

## संक्षिप्त नियम (हिंदी)

1. **नतीजों के बारे में ईमानदार रहें।** रैंक, अंक या चयन के बारे में बढ़ा-चढ़ाकर दावे न करें, न अपने बारे में, न किसी और के बारे में।
2. **पॉड में कुछ भी न बेचें।** कोर्स, नोट्स, टेस्ट सीरीज़ या कोई और चीज़ बेचना, उसका प्रचार करना या लोगों को कहीं और जोड़ना मना है।
3. **व्यावसायिक संबंध बताएँ।** अगर आप किसी कोचिंग, प्रकाशक या सेवा का ज़िक्र करते हैं और उससे आपका कोई संबंध है, तो साफ़ बताएँ।
4. **परेशानी वाली हर पोस्ट का जवाब एक इंसान देगा।** अगर कोई मुश्किल में है, तो विनम्रता से जवाब दें और मॉडरेटर को बताएँ। कभी मज़ाक न उड़ाएँ, कभी अनदेखा न करें।
5. **तुलना और ताने नहीं।** रैंक का दिखावा, किसी को शर्मिंदा करना, या "अभी तक पाँचवाँ अटेम्प्ट?" जैसी बातें मना हैं।
6. **किसी का काम साझा करने से पहले पूछें।** दूसरों के उत्तर, मैसेज या प्रोफ़ाइल का स्क्रीनशॉट उनकी सहमति के बिना न लें।
7. **सबका सम्मान करें।** जाति, धर्म, क्षेत्र, लिंग, भाषा, माध्यम, उम्र या अटेम्प्ट संख्या को लेकर अपमान या रूढ़िवादी बातें मना हैं।
8. **राजनीति पर एक सिविल सेवक की तरह चर्चा करें।** नीतियाँ, दोनों पक्ष, तथ्य। किसी दल का प्रचार नहीं।
9. **पायरेसी नहीं।** जिस सशुल्क सामग्री को साझा करने का अधिकार आपके पास नहीं है, उसे साझा न करें।
10. **पेशेवर सलाह न दें।** अपना अनुभव बताएँ, पर चिकित्सा या कानूनी सलाह को पेशेवर सलाह की तरह न दें।

अगर कोई नियम टूटता दिखे, तो रिपोर्ट करें। अगर आपको लगता है कि आपके साथ ग़लत फ़ैसला हुआ है, तो आप 14 दिनों के भीतर अपील कर सकते हैं।
