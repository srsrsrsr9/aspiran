# Community and safety documents

> **DRAFT for human review. Not approved. Do not use with members until the safety lead and a qualified mental-health professional have signed off.**

Drafted 11 Oct 2026. Everything in this folder is a draft. A safety lead, a lawyer and a qualified mental-health professional must review it before any of it reaches members, pod leads or moderators. Volunteers in UPSC Pods are not clinicians, and these documents say so plainly.

## The files

**`crisis-protocol.md`** is the distress and crisis protocol for wellbeing moderators and the safety lead. It covers how to recognise distress in three tiers, what to say and not say (sample replies in English and Hindi), the helpline sheet (every number marked "verify before publishing"), the escalation ladder with on-call slots and reply-time placeholders the team must fill, 2 a.m. in result season, immediate danger, follow-up, minimal record-keeping with the DPDP Act in mind, monthly review, what Chotu and the automated check may and may not do, a result-day playbook and moderator self-care. Write and approve this one first.

**`community-rules.md`** is the member-facing rule book. A short list of ten rules comes first, then the detail for each, then how reports, appeals and the consequences ladder work. It ends with the short list in standard Hindi.

**`pod-lead-playbook.md`** is the friendly handbook for volunteer pod leads: the role and its limits, the weekly rhythm, welcome templates for the first 72 hours, day 3 and day 14, running the Monday check-in, "one fix first, one strength last" feedback, handling conflict, spotting distress and handing over (never alone, never promising secrecy), result-day messages, repeaters and dignity, boundaries and burnout, and the hard lines.

**`distress-phrases-hi.md`** and **`distress-phrases-te.md`** are the phrase lists for Hindi (with Hinglish) and Telugu (with Tenglish). Each has three tiers (immediate danger, high concern, low mood), native script and romanised forms, common misspellings, false positives such as exam slang and GS topics, behavioural signs, and notes for moderators. A native speaker must check every line. Telugu stays blocked from going live until that is done.

**`distress-phrases.json`** is the machine-readable copy of the phrase lists for the on-device pre-filter, with Hindi, Telugu and English. It includes matching rules and the core rule: matches only route a post to a human and show helplines; they never auto-reply as a person and never block or delete a post. The two Markdown phrase files were generated from this JSON, so keep them in step.

## Review checklist

Before any of this is used:

- [ ] **Mental-health professional** has reviewed the crisis protocol, the distress sections of the playbook, the phrase tiers and all sample replies, and confirmed the guidance on asking directly about suicide and who may ask it.
- [ ] **Safety lead** has set every placeholder: T1 and T2 reply targets, the on-call roster with real names, night-shift length, report and appeal times, case-note retention (with the lawyer).
- [ ] **Safety lead** has test-called 112, Tele-MANAS 14416 and 1-800-891-4416, and recorded the date, hours and languages. No regional number is added until it has been checked.
- [ ] **Published response hours** in the app match the roster. No reply-time promise the team cannot keep.
- [ ] **Lawyer** has checked records, retention, access and deletion against the DPDP Act and Rules; what may be shared with emergency services; the community rules against the terms, privacy policy, IT Rules grievance process and consumer-protection rules.
- [ ] **Build team and lawyer** have confirmed what actually leaves the phone when the distress check fires (the protocol assumes private Chotu chat stays on the phone unless the member taps "Talk to a person", and a flagged pod post sends only its ID and tier to the safety queue).
- [ ] **Native Hindi speaker** has checked every Hindi line in every file.
- [ ] **Native Telugu speaker** has checked every Telugu line, and written Telugu sample replies and rules, before Telugu goes live.
- [ ] **Product** has confirmed the app matches the documents: Chotu rules, "no human online" message, result-day mode, the 72-hour no-upsell window, no countdowns, streaks or leaderboards, "Get help for this post", "Ask the lead privately", private mood tap, opt-in wins space.
- [ ] **Product** has confirmed pod size (these drafts use 6 to 8; the original brief said 8 to 10).
- [ ] **Training** for wellbeing moderators and pod leads exists and has been run before launch.
- [ ] A **monthly review** date is in the calendar.
- [ ] Each file's own review block is signed and dated.
