Book: Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning, by Peter C. Brown, Henry L. Roediger III and Mark A. McDaniel (2014).
One idea from the book: retrieval practice. Trying to recall something from memory, for example by self-quizzing, strengthens learning more than rereading it. Rereading can feel productive because the text becomes familiar, but this familiarity can be mistaken for mastery.
How an aspirant might use it this week: after each chapter, close the book and write down the main points from memory before checking.
Where to read it: many public and university libraries stock it. The authors' research on retrieval practice is also described in freely available articles by Henry Roediger and colleagues.
