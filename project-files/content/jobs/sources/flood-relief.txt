Editor's scenario (fictional, for GS4 practice): You are the district collector of a flood-hit district. Relief material (food, tarpaulins, medicines) can reach only one area today. Village A has been cut off for three days and the latest report says it is the worst hit. Village B is less affected, but a local MLA calls and insists that relief go there first, because it is in his constituency, and hints that he will complain about you to the state government.
Possible actions to show: send relief to Village A as planned and record the MLA's request; split the relief between both villages; send it to Village B first to avoid conflict.
Values involved: impartiality, compassion, courage, accountability, avoiding conflict of interest.
