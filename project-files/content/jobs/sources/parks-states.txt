National parks and tiger reserves, and the states they are in:
Kaziranga National Park: Assam.
Periyar Tiger Reserve: Kerala.
Sundarbans National Park: West Bengal.
Jim Corbett National Park: Uttarakhand. It was India's first national park, set up in 1936 as Hailey National Park.
Ranthambore National Park: Rajasthan.
Bandipur National Park: Karnataka.
Kanha National Park: Madhya Pradesh.
Commonly confused: Bandipur (Karnataka) with Mudumalai (Tamil Nadu), which adjoin each other; Kanha (Madhya Pradesh) with Tadoba (Maharashtra).
