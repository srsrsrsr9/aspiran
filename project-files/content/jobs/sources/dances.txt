Classical dance forms recognised by the Sangeet Natak Akademi, and the states they are most associated with:
Kathakali: Kerala
Kuchipudi: Andhra Pradesh
Odissi: Odisha
Sattriya: Assam
Manipuri: Manipur
Bharatanatyam: Tamil Nadu
Kathak is associated with north India, especially Uttar Pradesh. Mohiniyattam is also from Kerala.
