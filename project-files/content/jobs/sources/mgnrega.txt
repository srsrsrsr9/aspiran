MGNREGA stands for the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act. It was passed in 2005.
It guarantees at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.
If work is not given within 15 days of applying, the applicant is entitled to an unemployment allowance.
It is run by the Ministry of Rural Development. Gram sabhas recommend works, and at least one-third of the beneficiaries should be women.
