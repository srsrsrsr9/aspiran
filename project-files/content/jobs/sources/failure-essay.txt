Editor's essay topic for practice: "Failure is the best teacher."
Points the editor suggests across dimensions:
Personal: people often learn more from a mistake they reflect on than from an easy success.
Science and technology: ISRO's Chandrayaan-2 lander crash-landed in September 2019; the lessons from it shaped Chandrayaan-3, which landed near the Moon's south pole on 23 August 2023.
Economic: many start-ups fail; founders and investors use those failures to build better ones.
Policy and governance: evaluating schemes that did not work helps design better ones.
Ethical: owning a failure honestly builds trust; hiding it repeats it.
Counter-view: failure teaches only if it is examined; repeated failure without reflection can crush confidence and teaches nothing. Some failures, such as a collapsed bridge, are too costly to "learn from" and must be prevented.
