National Emergency under Article 352 has been proclaimed three times.
1962: on the ground of external aggression, during the war with China.
1971: on the ground of external aggression, during the war with Pakistan.
1975: on the ground of "internal disturbance", declared in June 1975 while the 1971 proclamation was still in force.
1978: the 44th Amendment replaced "internal disturbance" with "armed rebellion" as a ground for a National Emergency.
The three proclamations are a common Prelims fact; aspirants often mix up which one was on internal grounds.
