Mnemonic chosen by the editor: "He Must Pay Customs Quickly" for the five writs, in this order:
H: Habeas corpus, to produce a detained person before the court.
M: Mandamus, a command to a public official to do their duty.
P: Prohibition, to stop a lower court acting beyond its powers.
C: Certiorari, to quash an order a lower court has already passed.
Q: Quo warranto, to ask by what authority someone holds a public office.
The Supreme Court issues writs under Article 32 and High Courts under Article 226.
