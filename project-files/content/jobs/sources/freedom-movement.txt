Key moments of the Indian national movement:
1905: The partition of Bengal under Lord Curzon leads to the Swadeshi movement.
1920: The Non-Cooperation Movement is launched under Gandhi.
1930: The Dandi March begins the Civil Disobedience Movement.
1942: The Quit India Movement begins in August.
1947: India becomes independent on 15 August.
Across these decades, the movement grew from protests by the educated classes into mass campaigns.
