News item: India's Chandrayaan-3 lander, Vikram, touched down near the Moon's south pole on 23 August 2023. India became the first country to land a spacecraft near the lunar south pole, and the fourth country to make a soft landing on the Moon.
Background: In September 2019, Chandrayaan-2's lander, also named Vikram, lost contact during its final descent and crash-landed. ISRO studied what went wrong. For Chandrayaan-3 it strengthened the lander's legs, gave it a larger landing area and more fuel, and added more sensors and backup systems.
Since then: The Government of India declared 23 August as National Space Day.
