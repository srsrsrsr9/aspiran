PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) was launched in 2019. It is a central sector scheme, fully funded by the Government of India, run by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.
It gives income support of Rs 6,000 a year to eligible landholding farmer families, paid in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 directly into their bank accounts through direct benefit transfer.
Some groups are excluded, such as income tax payers and holders of constitutional posts.
Exam angles: direct benefit transfer, farm income support, and the debate on income support versus input subsidies.
