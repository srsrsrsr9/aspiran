Peninsular rivers and where they rise:
Godavari: rises near Trimbakeshwar in the Nashik district of Maharashtra.
Narmada: rises at Amarkantak in Madhya Pradesh.
Krishna: rises near Mahabaleshwar in Maharashtra.
Kaveri: rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of Kodagu district, Karnataka.
Mahanadi: rises near Sihawa in the Dhamtari district of Chhattisgarh.
Tapi: rises near Multai in the Betul district of Madhya Pradesh.
The Narmada and the Tapi flow west into the Arabian Sea; the Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri and Mahanadi flow east into the Bay of Bengal.
