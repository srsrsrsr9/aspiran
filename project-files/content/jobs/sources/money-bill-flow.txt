How a Money Bill passes Parliament (Articles 109, 110 and 117):
1. A Money Bill can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha, and only on the recommendation of the President.
2. The Speaker of the Lok Sabha certifies that it is a Money Bill; the Speaker's decision is final.
3. After the Lok Sabha passes it, it goes to the Rajya Sabha, which cannot reject or amend it. The Rajya Sabha can only make recommendations, and must return the Bill within 14 days.
4. The Lok Sabha may accept or reject any of the recommendations. If the Rajya Sabha does not return it within 14 days, the Bill is deemed passed by both Houses.
5. The Bill goes to the President, who may give or withhold assent but cannot return it for reconsideration.
There is no joint sitting for a Money Bill.
