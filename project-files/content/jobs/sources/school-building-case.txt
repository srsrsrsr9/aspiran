Editor's case (fictional, for GS4 practice): You are the Executive Engineer in charge of building a new government school before the monsoon. During an inspection you find that the contractor is using cement and steel below the grade in the contract. The contractor is a relative of a senior officer in your department. The senior officer calls you and says the work must finish on time, the school is badly needed, and "small issues" can be fixed later. If you stop the work, the school will not open this year and children will study in a temporary shed. If you allow it, the building may be unsafe.
Question: What are the ethical issues involved? What options do you have, and what would you do?
Values the editor lists: integrity, safety of children, accountability, courage of conviction, impartiality, avoiding conflict of interest.
Possible options to consider: allow the work and record your objection; stop the work, order the substandard material replaced and report in writing; allow only the parts of the work that are not structural while the material is replaced; quietly ask the contractor to fix it without reporting.
