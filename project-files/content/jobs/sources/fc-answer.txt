Practice question (GS2, 150 words): "Discuss the role of the Finance Commission in strengthening fiscal federalism in India."
Facts: The Finance Commission is a constitutional body under Article 280, constituted by the President every five years. It recommends the division of the net proceeds of taxes between the Union and the states (vertical devolution) and among the states (horizontal devolution), and the principles for grants-in-aid. The Fifteenth Finance Commission, chaired by N.K. Singh, recommended a 41 percent share for states in the divisible pool.
Issues often discussed: its recommendations are advisory; cesses and surcharges are outside the divisible pool, which reduces what states receive; the criteria for horizontal devolution, such as population and income distance, balance need against efficiency.
Ways forward often suggested: limiting cesses and surcharges, and more consultation with states.
