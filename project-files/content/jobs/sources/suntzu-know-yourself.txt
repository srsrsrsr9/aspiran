Quote id: suntzu-art-of-war-3-18. Sun Tzu, The Art of War, Chapter 3 (Lionel Giles translation, 1910, public domain): "If you know the enemy and know yourself, you need not fear the result of a hundred battles."
For preparation, "know the enemy" is best read as knowing the paper, not other aspirants: the pattern of past questions, which topics come up often, and how marks are given. "Know yourself" means knowing your own weak areas honestly, for example from mock test results and the topics you keep postponing.
A useful monthly check: which GS paper or topic have you avoided this month, and why?
A small step: list the three topics you have avoided, check how often each appeared in the last five years of papers, and give an hour this week to the one that appeared most.
