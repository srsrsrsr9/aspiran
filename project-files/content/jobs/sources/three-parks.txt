Kaziranga National Park in Assam is home to about two-thirds of the world's great one-horned rhinoceroses. UNESCO listed it as a World Heritage Site in 1985.
Keoladeo National Park in Bharatpur, Rajasthan, is a wetland famous for winter migratory birds, including, in the past, the Siberian crane. It became a World Heritage Site in 1985.
Silent Valley National Park in Kerala protects tropical evergreen forest in the Nilgiri hills. A people's movement in the 1970s and 1980s stopped a hydroelectric project there. It is a home of the lion-tailed macaque.
