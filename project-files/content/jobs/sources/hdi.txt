The Human Development Index (HDI) is published by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) in its Human Development Report. It was first published in 1990.
It combines three dimensions: a long and healthy life (life expectancy at birth), knowledge (mean years of schooling and expected years of schooling), and a decent standard of living (gross national income per capita, at purchasing power parity).
India is placed in the "medium human development" category.
Caution from the editor: India's rank changes with each report, and methods and data revisions mean ranks from different editions are not strictly comparable. Always name the edition when quoting a rank.
