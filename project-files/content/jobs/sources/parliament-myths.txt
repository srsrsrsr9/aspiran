The Rajya Sabha is a permanent body and is never dissolved. One-third of its members retire every second year.
The Vice-President of India is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha, but is not a member of it.
A Money Bill can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha. The Rajya Sabha can only make recommendations, within 14 days, and the Lok Sabha may accept or reject them.
The Lok Sabha's normal term is five years, but it can be dissolved earlier. During a national emergency, Parliament can extend its term by one year at a time.
