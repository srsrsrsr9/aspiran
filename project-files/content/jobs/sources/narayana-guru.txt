Editor's source notes: Sree Narayana Guru.

Sree Narayana Guru was born in 1856 near Thiruvananthapuram, in the Ezhava community, which faced caste discrimination in Kerala at the time.
In 1888, at Aruvippuram, he consecrated an idol of Shiva himself. At that time only Brahmins were allowed to consecrate idols in temples. When questioned, he is reported to have said that he had consecrated an Ezhava Shiva.
In 1903, the Sree Narayana Dharma Paripalana Yogam (SNDP Yogam) was formed to spread his ideas through education and organisation, with the Guru as its president.
His teaching is often summed up in the Malayalam line "one caste, one religion, one God for humankind". He also urged people to gain strength through education and through organisation.
He founded an ashram at Sivagiri, near Varkala, which became a centre of his movement. He supported the Vaikom Satyagraha of 1924 for access to the roads around the Vaikom temple.
He died in 1928.

Why it matters: GS Paper I covers social reform in the south, and Narayana Guru is a frequent name in questions on reform movements and their organisations.
