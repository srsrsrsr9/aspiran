Area and population of five large states, Census of India 2011:
Rajasthan: area 342,239 square kilometres; population 68,548,437.
Madhya Pradesh: area 308,252 square kilometres; population 72,626,809.
Maharashtra: area 307,713 square kilometres; population 112,374,333.
Uttar Pradesh: area 240,928 square kilometres; population 199,812,341.
Gujarat: area 196,024 square kilometres; population 60,439,692.
For questions, population may be shown in lakh (1 lakh = 100,000), rounded to the nearest lakh: Rajasthan 685, Madhya Pradesh 726, Maharashtra 1124, Uttar Pradesh 1998, Gujarat 604.
