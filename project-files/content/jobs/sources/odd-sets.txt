Set 1, rivers: the Narmada, the Tapi and the Mahi flow west into the Arabian Sea. The Godavari flows east into the Bay of Bengal.
Set 2, crops: rice, maize and cotton are kharif crops, sown with the onset of the southwest monsoon. Wheat is a rabi crop, sown in winter.
Set 3, bodies: the Election Commission (Article 324), the Union Public Service Commission (Article 315) and the Finance Commission (Article 280) are constitutional bodies. NITI Aayog was set up in 2015 by a resolution of the Union Cabinet, so it is not a constitutional body.
Set 4, writs: habeas corpus, mandamus and certiorari are writs the Supreme Court can issue under Article 32. A "stay order" is an interim order, not one of the five writs.
