Committees and commissions often asked about, with the year each was set up:
Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957): recommended three-tier Panchayati Raj.
Ashok Mehta Committee (1977): recommended a two-tier panchayat system.
Kothari Commission (1964): education.
Swaran Singh Committee (1976): Fundamental Duties.
Sarkaria Commission (1983): Centre-state relations.
Narasimham Committee (1991): banking sector reforms.
