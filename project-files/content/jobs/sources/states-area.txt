The five largest Indian states by area (square kilometres), from the Census of India 2011:
Rajasthan, 342,239
Madhya Pradesh, 308,252
Maharashtra, 307,713
Uttar Pradesh, 240,928
Gujarat, 196,024
Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra are close in area, about 540 square kilometres apart. Rajasthan has been the largest state since Madhya Pradesh was divided to create Chhattisgarh in 2000.
