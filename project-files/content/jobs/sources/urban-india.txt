Census of India figures. In 1951, India's population was 36.1 crore and 17.3 percent of Indians lived in urban areas.
In 2011, the population was 121.1 crore and 31.1 percent lived in urban areas.
The census defines a town by statutory status, or by having at least 5,000 people, a density of at least 400 per square kilometre, and at least 75 percent of the male working population outside agriculture. The definitions changed slightly between censuses.
