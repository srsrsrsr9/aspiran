The Finance Commission is a constitutional body set up under Article 280. The President constitutes it every five years, or earlier if needed.
It has a chairman and four other members.
It recommends how the net proceeds of taxes are divided between the Union and the states (vertical devolution), and how the states' share is divided among states (horizontal devolution). It also recommends the principles for grants-in-aid to states from the Consolidated Fund of India.
Its recommendations are advisory; they are not binding on the government.
The Fifteenth Finance Commission was chaired by N.K. Singh. It recommended that the states' share in the divisible pool of central taxes be 41 percent.
The Finance Commission is not the same as NITI Aayog, which is not a constitutional body and does not recommend tax devolution.
