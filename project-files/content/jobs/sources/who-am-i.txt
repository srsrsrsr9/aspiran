Dr B.R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly. He was independent India's first Law Minister. He called Article 32, the right to constitutional remedies, the "heart and soul" of the Constitution. He was born in 1891 in Mhow, in present-day Madhya Pradesh.
Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel was independent India's first Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister. He led the integration of more than 500 princely states into the Indian Union. He is remembered as the "Iron Man of India", and the Statue of Unity in Gujarat is dedicated to him. National Unity Day, 31 October, marks his birth anniversary.
Other leaders of the period who could appear as options: Jawaharlal Nehru, Rajendra Prasad, Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, C. Rajagopalachari.
