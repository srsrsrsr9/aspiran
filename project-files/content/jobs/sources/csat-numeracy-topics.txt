Editor's brief for a CSAT numeracy set (Prelims level, no calculator):
Topics: percentages (including percentage change), averages, and speed, distance and time.
Write four problems: one on percentage change, one on a weighted average, one on average speed for a round trip, and one more of your choice from the topics.
Keep numbers friendly. Typical slips to use for wrong options: taking the percentage of the wrong base, averaging speeds directly instead of using total distance over total time, forgetting the weights.
