Tropical cyclones over the North Indian Ocean are named by the India Meteorological Department's Regional Specialised Meteorological Centre in New Delhi.
Names come from a list prepared by 13 member countries of the WMO/ESCAP Panel on Tropical Cyclones. The list adopted in 2020 has 169 names, 13 from each country, used in order.
A name is used once and never repeated.
The Bay of Bengal usually sees more cyclones than the Arabian Sea. Cyclones are most frequent in two seasons: April to June, and October to December.
