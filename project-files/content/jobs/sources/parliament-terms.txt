Parliamentary terms, in English and the official Hindi:
Ordinance (अध्यादेश): a law the President can issue under Article 123 when Parliament is not in session. It must be approved by Parliament within six weeks of its reassembly.
Money Bill (धन विधेयक): defined in Article 110. It can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha.
Quorum (गणपूर्ति): the minimum number of members needed to hold a sitting, one-tenth of the total membership of the House (Article 100).
Zero Hour (शून्य काल): the time after Question Hour when members raise urgent matters. It is not mentioned in the rules of procedure.
Adjournment motion (स्थगन प्रस्ताव): a motion to set aside the normal business of the Lok Sabha to discuss an urgent matter of public importance.
Whip (सचेतक): a party official who ensures members attend and vote as the party decides; also the written direction itself.
