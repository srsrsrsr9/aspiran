The first six Mughal emperors, in order: Babur, Humayun, Akbar, Jahangir, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb.
Babur founded the empire after winning the First Battle of Panipat in 1526.
Humayun lost the throne to Sher Shah Suri and won it back in 1555.
Akbar expanded the empire and set up the mansabdari system.
Jahangir allowed the English East India Company's envoy Thomas Roe at his court.
Shah Jahan built the Taj Mahal in Agra.
Aurangzeb ruled until 1707, when the empire was at its largest.
