Quote id: kautilya-arthashastra-1-19. Kautilya, Arthashastra, Book 1, Chapter 19 (R. Shamasastry translation, 1915): "In the happiness of his subjects lies his happiness; in their welfare his welfare." The editor has yet to verify the exact wording.
Present-day reading: a public office exists for the people it serves. A public servant's success is measured by citizens' welfare, not by personal status or the comfort of the office.
Example the editor suggests: a district collector who holds public hearings in villages instead of only at the district office, so that people who cannot travel can be heard.
Related values: public service, empathy, accountability, citizen-centric governance (as discussed in the Second Administrative Reforms Commission's reports on ethics in governance).
