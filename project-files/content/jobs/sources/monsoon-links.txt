How the southwest monsoon links to other parts of the syllabus:
Agriculture: kharif sowing depends on monsoon rain, and a large part of India's farmland is rain-fed.
Inflation: a weak monsoon can reduce crop output and push up food prices, which affects the RBI's inflation outlook.
Water and energy: monsoon rain fills reservoirs and recharges groundwater, which matters for irrigation, drinking water and hydropower.
Disasters: heavy monsoon rain can cause floods and landslides, especially in the Himalayan states and the Western Ghats.
Climate: El Niño years often bring a weaker monsoon, while a positive Indian Ocean Dipole can help it.
Rural economy: farm incomes after a good monsoon can raise rural demand for goods.
