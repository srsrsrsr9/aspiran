The Seventh Schedule divides subjects between the Union and the states in three lists.
Union List (Parliament makes laws): defence, foreign affairs, banking, railways, atomic energy.
State List (state legislatures make laws): police, public order, public health and sanitation, agriculture.
Concurrent List (both can make laws; a Union law prevails in case of conflict): criminal law, marriage and divorce, education, forests.
Education and forests were moved from the State List to the Concurrent List by the 42nd Amendment in 1976.
