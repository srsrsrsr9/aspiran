Directive words in UPSC Mains questions, as coaching guides and toppers commonly explain them (Hindi forms as printed in the Hindi question paper):
Discuss (चर्चा कीजिए): examine the issue from different sides, bring out the main points and arguments, and give a balanced view. Common mistake: one-sided listing.
Critically examine (समालोचनात्मक परीक्षण कीजिए): look closely at the issue, judge its merits and demerits with evidence, and reach a reasoned judgment. Common mistake: only praising or only criticising.
Analyse (विश्लेषण कीजिए): break the issue into its parts and show how they relate and what causes what. Common mistake: describing without explaining links.
Elucidate (स्पष्ट कीजिए): explain clearly, with examples, so the concept becomes clear. Common mistake: giving opinions instead of explanation.
Comment (टिप्पणी कीजिए): give your opinion on the issue, backed by facts and reasoning. Common mistake: an opinion without evidence.
