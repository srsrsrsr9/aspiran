The President of India is elected by an electoral college made up of the elected members of both Houses of Parliament and the elected members of the legislative assemblies of the states and of the Union Territories of Delhi and Puducherry. Nominated members of Parliament and of the assemblies do not vote in the presidential election.
The Vice-President is elected by an electoral college of all members of both Houses of Parliament, elected and nominated. State legislators do not take part.
The President holds office for five years and is eligible for re-election.
Both elections are held by proportional representation by means of the single transferable vote, and voting is by secret ballot.
