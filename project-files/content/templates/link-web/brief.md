# Link web

Schema: `upsc-pods/link-web@1` · Folder: `content/link-web/` · Family: Think · Engine: hub

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-gs1, mains-gs3, mains-essay · formats: mains-answer, mains-essay

**Sound:** A tick per spoke; a chime when all are open.

**Motion:** Spokes draw out from the centre in 300 ms when tapped.

## How to fill it

One topic at the centre and 4 to 6 linked topics around it. Tapping a spoke shows how the two connect. Builds the cross-topic links Mains answers need.
- center: the topic, 4 words or fewer.
- spokes: 4 to 6, each a different syllabus area. link is 20 words or fewer and says how, with the source's hedges.
- question: 18 words or fewer.
