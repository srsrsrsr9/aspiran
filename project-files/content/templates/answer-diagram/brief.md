# Draw it in the answer

Schema: `upsc-pods/answer-diagram@1` · Folder: `content/answer-diagram/` · Family: Practise · Engine: steps

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-gs1, mains-gs2, mains-gs3 · formats: mains-diagram, mains-answer

**Sound:** A tick as each box appears; a chime when the diagram is complete.

**Motion:** Boxes appear one by one and arrows draw between them in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A diagram an aspirant can sketch in under a minute inside a Mains answer: a flowchart of a process, a cycle, a hub of factors, or a timeline.
- nodes: 3 to 8 boxes, in order, 7 words or fewer each, every fact from the source.
- caption: what the diagram shows. why: where it goes in the answer (usually the body) and what it saves.
