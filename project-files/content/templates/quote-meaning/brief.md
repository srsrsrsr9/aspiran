## How to fill it

GS4 Section A often gives a thinker's line and asks what it means in the present context. This template walks through one answer.
- Never write or paraphrase the quote yourself. Pick its id; the app inserts the exact words.
- meaning: faithful to the line. today: how it applies to a public servant now. example: only from the source.
- structure: 3 or 4 short lines, the skeleton of a 150-word answer.
