# Answer skeleton

Schema: `upsc-pods/answer-skeleton@1` · Folder: `content/answer-skeleton/` · Family: Practise · Engine: steps

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-gs1, mains-gs2, mains-gs3 · formats: mains-answer, mains-directive

**Sound:** A page turn per beat; an optional soundscape under stories; a chime at the end.

**Motion:** Each beat slides in from the right in 300 ms; choices fold open in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

The skeleton of a 150 or 250-word Mains answer, built up step by step: question, intro, body points, conclusion, keywords.
- directive: the directive word, and the structure follows it.
- intro: 40 words or fewer, with a fact from the source.
- body: 3 to 5 points, 22 words or fewer each, each a separate dimension.
- conclusion: 36 words or fewer, balanced and forward-looking.
