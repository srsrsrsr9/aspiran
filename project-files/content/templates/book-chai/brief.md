# Book Chai (weekly)

Schema: `upsc-pods/book-chai@1` · Folder: `content/book-chai/` · Family: Read · Engine: choose

**Exam:** revision · papers: none (study skill) · formats: none

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

One idea from one book, once a week. The member picks which of three situations fits the idea, then gets one way to use it this week and a free route to the book.
- idea: one idea only, faithful to the source. No quotes unless the source gives the exact words.
- situations: three short, realistic aspirant situations. Exactly one fits.
- readIt: only routes the source names (a library, a free course, a public-domain text). Never sell the book as a shortcut to selection.
