# Story spine (Essay and GS4)

Schema: `upsc-pods/story-spine@1` · Folder: `content/story-spine/` · Family: Practise · Engine: steps

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-essay, mains-gs4 · formats: mains-essay, qual-essay

**Sound:** A page turn per beat and a soft chime at the end. Silent while the member writes.

**Motion:** Each beat slides in from the right in 300 ms; choices fold open in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A news item retold as a five-line story using Pixar's story spine, then the member writes their own for an essay topic. Essay and GS4 case answers reward a short, concrete story at the start.
- spine: exactly five lines, starting in order with "Every day", "One day", "Because of that", "Until finally", "And ever since" (Hindi: "हर दिन", "एक दिन", "इसी वजह से", "आख़िरकार", "और तब से"). Each line 26 words or fewer, every fact from the source.
- No invented people, dialogue or feelings. The story is about what happened, told in order.
- topic: a real-sounding essay topic the same shape of story would fit. starter: a hint, never a model answer.
