# Stamp trail (process journey)

Schema: `upsc-pods/stamp-trail@1` · Folder: `content/journeys/` · Family: Play

**Exam:** prelims, mains · papers: prelims-gs1, mains-gs2 · formats: pre-order, mains-diagram

**Sound:** A paper whoosh as the item moves, a rubber-stamp thud at each station, the chai motif at the end.

**Motion:** The item slides to the next station in 350 ms, the stamp lands with a 200 ms squash, and ink settles.

## How to fill it

A process told as a journey: an item (a bill, an FIR, a petition) is stamped at each station in order. Members tap to move it on.
- item: what travels, 2 words or fewer.
- stations: 3 to 6 in the real order. name is 4 words or fewer. stamp is the 1 or 2 words inked on the item. rule is 28 words or fewer and names the article or rule from the source.
- takeaway: one exception or twist, 24 words or fewer (for example how a Money Bill differs).
Only include steps the source describes.
