# Sort into buckets

Schema: `upsc-pods/bucket-sort@1` · Folder: `content/bucket-sort/` · Family: Play · Engine: sort

**Exam:** prelims, revision · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-select-items

**Sound:** A whoosh as a card flies to its bucket, a snap when it lands, a nudge if it bounces back.

**Motion:** Cards fly to the bucket in 300 ms; a wrong one bounces back with a 400 ms wobble.

## How to fill it

Cards to sort into two or three buckets.
- buckets: 2 or 3 names, 4 words or fewer.
- items: 4 to 10 cards, 8 words or fewer, each belonging to exactly one bucket. Spread them roughly evenly.
- why: 18 words or fewer, the reason it belongs there, with the article or rule if the source gives one.
- memoryHook: optional, 18 words or fewer. Only if the list is worth memorising: an acronym, a picture or a tiny story built from the items. Write the Hindi hook natively in Hindi (it may differ from the English). Use null if nothing honest fits; a hook never replaces understanding.
