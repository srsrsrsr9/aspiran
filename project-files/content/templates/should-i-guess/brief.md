# Should I guess? (negative marking)

Schema: `upsc-pods/should-i-guess@1` · Folder: `content/should-i-guess/` · Family: Practise · Engine: choose

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-direct

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

Sun Tzu's "choose your battles", made literal for Prelims. The member reads a situation and decides whether to guess. The app works out the expected marks from the marking scheme in config, so you only write the situations.
- rounds: 3 or 4, with optionsLeft covering 2, 3 and 4 at least once. Each situation says what the member has ruled out and how.
- Never say "always guess" or "never guess". The point is: eliminate first, then decide.
- rule: one line, from the source.
