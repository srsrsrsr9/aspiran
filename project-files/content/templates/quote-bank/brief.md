# Quote bank

File: `content/quotes/quotes.json` · Schema: `upsc-pods/quote-bank@1` · Kept by editors, never written by a model.

Every entry is unverified until an editor checks the exact words against the named edition and fills verifiedBy. The app shows only verified quotes, at most one per screen, never on TRAGIC content or in a distress flow. Hindi translations are done by the Hindi editor and labelled as translations. Portraits are our own illustrations; Sun Tzu and Kautilya are labelled as an artist's impression. No web photos, no AI voices imitating real people. Entries with an "original" carry the line in its own language and script; the app shows it above the English where the screen allows.

Templates that show a quote (kautilya-lens, strategy-corner) only pick a quote id. The assembler copies the exact words, citation, Hindi translation and verified flag from this file, so a model can never misquote.
