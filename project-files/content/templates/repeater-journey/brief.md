# Repeater story (decisions, not outcomes)

Schema: `upsc-pods/repeater-journey@1` · Folder: `content/repeater-journey/` · Family: Listen · Engine: steps

**Exam:** revision · papers: none (study skill) · formats: none

**Sound:** A page turn per stage; no soundscape; a soft chime at the end.

**Motion:** Each beat slides in from the right in 300 ms; choices fold open in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A repeater's story in the hero's-journey order (the ordinary world, the call, the ordeal, what they brought back), with the decision questions in the middle. It judges the decision by what was known at the time, not by the result.
- Only what the source says. No invented feelings, quotes, ranks or names.
- returned: who they became. A new path (another service, teaching, a different career) is a full ending, not a consolation.
- No winners-and-losers language, and no "never give up" pressure. Choosing to stop can be a good decision.
- question: about the reader's next decision, gentle, never "will you make it?".
