# Pair up

Schema: `upsc-pods/pair-up@1` · Folder: `content/pair-up/` · Family: Play · Engine: match

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-list-match

**Sound:** Tick on a match, a nudge on a miss.

**Motion:** Matched pairs slide together and fade to the done colour in 250 ms.

## How to fill it

A general matching game: anything on the left with its partner on the right.
- heads: the two column headings, 3 words or fewer.
- pairs: 4 to 10, each left item with exactly one right item. No right item should fit two left items.
- memoryHook: optional, 18 words or fewer. Only if the list is worth memorising: an acronym, a picture or a tiny story built from the items. Write the Hindi hook natively in Hindi (it may differ from the English). Use null if nothing honest fits; a hook never replaces understanding.
