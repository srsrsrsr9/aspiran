# Trap spotter (MCQ with named traps)

Schema: `upsc-pods/trap-spotter@1` · Folder: `content/trap-spotter/` · Family: Play · Engine: choose

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-direct, pre-negative

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A Prelims-style MCQ where every wrong option is labelled with the trap it sets. After answering, the member sees the trap named, which trains them to slow down.
- options: exactly four, similar in length. answerIndex is 0 to 3, the same in both languages.
- traps: one per option, in option order. The right option is "none". Each wrong option gets one of: extreme-word (all, only, never), half-true, right-fact-wrong-reason, recent-news-bias, sounds-familiar.
- why: 32 words or fewer. Name the trap the member most likely fell for, then the right answer from the source.
