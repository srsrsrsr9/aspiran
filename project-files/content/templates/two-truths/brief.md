# Two truths and a lie

Schema: `upsc-pods/two-truths@1` · Folder: `content/two-truths/` · Family: Play · Engine: choose

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-negative, pre-statement-code

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

Three statements, one false. The member finds the lie; the card then shows it corrected.
- statements: exactly three, 20 words or fewer. The lie is a common misconception the source corrects, not a silly one.
- fix: the false statement rewritten so it is true, 26 words or fewer.
- Vary the lie's position across rounds.
