# Fill the gap

Schema: `upsc-pods/fill-the-gap@1` · Folder: `content/fill-the-gap/` · Family: Revise · Engine: cloze

**Exam:** prelims, revision · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-direct

**Sound:** A snap when a word drops into a gap, a nudge if it doesn't fit.

**Motion:** Words fly into gaps in 250 ms; a wrong one shakes and returns.

## How to fill it

A short paragraph with 2 to 5 gaps and a word bank that includes a few tempting wrong words.
- text: 70 words or fewer, with gaps written as [[1]], [[2]] ... in order.
- bank: 3 to 8 words or numbers, including every right answer and at least one plausible distractor (for example the 74th Amendment's number where the 73rd's belongs).
- answers: for each gap in order, the index of its word in bank. Keep the bank in the same order in both languages so the indexes match.
- why: 26 words or fewer, on the distractor most likely to trip someone.
