# Statement I, II and III

Schema: `upsc-pods/three-statements@1` · Folder: `content/three-statements/` · Family: Play · Engine: choose

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-statement-i-ii-iii

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

The newest statement format: Statement I is a claim; Statements II and III may or may not be true and may or may not explain it. Statement I is always true.
- Build it from cause and effect in the source. An explaining statement must be a real cause, not just a related true fact.
- key: pick a, b, c or d to match what you wrote. Vary keys across a pack.
- why: say for II and for III whether it is true and whether it explains I.
