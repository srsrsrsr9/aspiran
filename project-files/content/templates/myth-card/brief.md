# Forward buster (myth or fact)

Schema: `upsc-pods/myth-card@1` · Folder: `content/myths/` · Family: Play

**Exam:** prelims, revision · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-statement-code

**Sound:** Stamp thud when the verdict lands.

**Motion:** Verdict stamp scales in over 200 ms.

## How to fill it

Claims of the kind that circulate on WhatsApp, each with a verdict and a kind correction.
- claim: written the way people actually say it, 20 words or fewer.
- verdict: MYTH, FACT or HALF. Mix them; do not make every claim a myth.
- correction: 45 words or fewer. Start with "Right." for FACT and "Half right." for HALF. Give the exact provision or date from the source.
Never mock the person who believed the claim.
