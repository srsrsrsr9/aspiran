# Chai cards (daily pack)

Schema: `upsc-pods/chai-pack@1` · Folder: `content/cards/` · Family: Read

**Exam:** prelims, mains · papers: prelims-gs1, mains-gs1, mains-gs2, mains-gs3 · formats: pre-current-static, pre-direct

**Sound:** Optional tick on a right answer.

**Motion:** Card entry by tone: LIGHT 300 ms, NEUTRAL 200 ms, SERIOUS 150 ms, TRAGIC none.

## How to fill it

One card per story. Each card teaches the static concept under the news, not the news alone.
- headline: starts with a verb ("Learn why...", "See how..."), 12 words or fewer.
- summary: 50 to 95 words. Lead with the concept, then the news hook, then one concrete detail from the source.
- lensChips: 2 to 4 short tags. First chip is the GS paper (GS1 to GS4), then the topic, then "Prelims" or "Mains" if it fits.
- whyItMatters: 2 or 3 bullets of 12 words or fewer, each a reason an aspirant should care.
- examCorner: one MCQ with four options and one right answer, all from the source. Feedback explains, never scolds.
- chotuJoke: LIGHT cards only, gentle, about the concept, never about people or tragedy. Use null on every other tone.
- takeaway: one line to remember, 16 words or fewer.
- Do not invent a previous-year question. The app shows "No direct PYQ" unless an editor adds one.
