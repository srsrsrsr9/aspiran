# Scratch and reveal

Schema: `upsc-pods/scratch-card@1` · Folder: `content/scratch/` · Family: Play

**Exam:** prelims, revision · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-direct, pre-current-static

**Sound:** A paper scrape that follows the speed of the finger, then a santoor chime when the answer shows.

**Motion:** Scratching paints away a chai-stain layer; at 55% cleared the rest fades in 300 ms. Reduced motion: one tap reveals.

## How to fill it

The member reads a question, guesses in their head, then scratches a chai stain off the card to see the answer.
- tease: a question of 14 words or fewer that has one clear answer in the source.
- answer: 8 words or fewer. It is read at a glance, so no clauses.
- detail: 30 words or fewer of why, with one fact from the source.
Good tease: "Which river is called the Dakshin Ganga?" Bad tease: "Tell me about rivers of India."
