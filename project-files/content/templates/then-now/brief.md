# Then and now slider

Schema: `upsc-pods/then-now@1` · Folder: `content/thennow/` · Family: Explore

**Exam:** prelims, mains · papers: prelims-gs1, mains-gs3 · formats: pre-data

**Sound:** A light tick at each tenth of the slider, a chime at either end.

**Motion:** Numbers count and bars grow as the member drags; the gap between two bars is shaded.

## How to fill it

Two points in time and a slider between them. The member drags from the earlier year to the later one and watches the numbers move.
- from.year and to.year: from the source.
- metrics: 1 to 3, each with the exact earlier and later values from the source, a label of 4 words or fewer and a unit.
- takeaway: 26 words or fewer. Point out what changed and what did not (a gap that stayed, a rank that held).
- note: any change in method between the two years (for example a different age group). If there is none, write a short dash.
