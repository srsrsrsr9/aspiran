# Acronym unpacker

Schema: `upsc-pods/acronym-unpack@1` · Folder: `content/acronym-unpack/` · Family: Revise · Engine: reveal

**Exam:** revision · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-direct

**Sound:** A page turn on each flip; a chime on the last card.

**Motion:** Cards flip in 400 ms (a swap with reduced motion); lines on the back appear one by one.

## How to fill it

An acronym whose letters expand one by one, then one line on what it is and one fact.
- letters: one per letter of the acronym, in order. letter is the letter; word is what it stands for.
- In Hindi, keep the Latin letters and give the Hindi words.
- what: 26 words or fewer. fact: 26 words or fewer, the most-asked fact from the source.
