# Domino effect (cause and effect)

Schema: `upsc-pods/chain@1` · Folder: `content/chains/` · Family: Think

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-gs2, mains-gs3 · formats: mains-answer, mains-diagram

**Sound:** A wooden click per domino, rising in pitch down the line, and a chime at the end.

**Motion:** Each domino tips over in 260 ms and knocks the next; its effect slides out underneath.

## How to fill it

A chain of causes and effects, shown as dominoes. Tapping the first one knocks the rest down, one link at a time.
- links: 4 to 7 links, each 18 words or fewer, each the direct cause of the next. Keep the source's hedges ("often", "can").
- breakIt.question: where could this chain be broken? 18 words or fewer.
- breakIt.answer: 34 words or fewer, naming which link a policy or natural factor weakens, from the source.
Do not claim certainty the source does not claim.
