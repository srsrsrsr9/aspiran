**Sound:** A page turn per part; no right or wrong sounds.

**Motion:** Each beat slides in from the right in 300 ms; choices fold open in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A full GS4 Section B case study, worked the way examiners expect: stakeholders, ethical issues, options with merits and demerits, and a course of action.
- scenario: from the source; you may tighten it but not add facts.
- options: 3 or 4 realistic options, each with honest merits and demerits. Do not make a straw man.
- course: one defensible course of action that names the values it serves. There can be more than one good answer; say so if the source does.
- values: 3 to 6 single values or short phrases (integrity, impartiality, compassion ...).
