## How to fill it

A "Kautilya says" card for governance, tax and administration topics: one line from the Arthashastra, what it means, where it sits in the syllabus, and how to use it in an answer.
- Never write or paraphrase the quote yourself. Pick its id; the app inserts the exact words, the citation and, once an editor has checked it, the Hindi translation.
- No battle language aimed at people: the paper is a puzzle, other aspirants are not the enemy.
- meaning: plain words, faithful to the line, no modern spin the source does not support.
- syllabus and useIt: only from the source. The example sentence should sound like a real answer, not a slogan.
