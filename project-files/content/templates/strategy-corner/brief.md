# Strategy corner (monthly)

Schema: `upsc-pods/strategy-corner@1` · Folder: `content/strategy-corner/` · Family: Think · Engine: reveal

**Exam:** revision · papers: none (study skill) · formats: none

**Sound:** A page turn on each flip; a chime on the last card.

**Motion:** Cards flip in 400 ms (a swap with reduced motion); lines on the back appear one by one.

## How to fill it

Once a month: one sourced line from Sun Tzu, Kautilya or the Gita, turned into one honest question about the member's own preparation.
- Never write or paraphrase the quote yourself. Pick its id; the app inserts the exact words, the citation and, once an editor has checked it, the Hindi translation.
- No battle language aimed at people: the paper is a puzzle, other aspirants are not the enemy.
- idea: what the line means for study, not for fighting anyone.
- question: specific and answerable ("Which GS paper have you avoided this month?"), never shaming.
- tryThis: one small, concrete step that fits in a week.
