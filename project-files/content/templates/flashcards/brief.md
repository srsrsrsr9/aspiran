# Flashcards

Schema: `upsc-pods/flashcards@1` · Folder: `content/flashcards/` · Family: Revise · Engine: reveal

**Exam:** revision · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: none

**Sound:** A page turn on each flip; a chime on the last card.

**Motion:** Cards flip in 400 ms (a swap with reduced motion); lines on the back appear one by one.

## How to fill it

Plain flashcards for revision. After each flip the member taps "knew it" or "not yet"; "not yet" cards come back at the end. No score is shown.
- front: a question or cue, 14 words or fewer.
- back: the answer and one supporting detail, 30 words or fewer.
- One fact per card.
