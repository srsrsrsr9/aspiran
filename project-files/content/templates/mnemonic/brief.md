# Mnemonic card

Schema: `upsc-pods/mnemonic@1` · Folder: `content/mnemonic/` · Family: Revise · Engine: reveal

**Exam:** revision · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: none

**Sound:** A page turn on each flip; a chime on the last card.

**Motion:** Cards flip in 400 ms (a swap with reduced motion); lines on the back appear one by one.

## How to fill it

A memory phrase whose letters unlock a list. Each letter lights up in turn to show what it stands for.
- mnemonic: the phrase from the source. Do not invent a new mnemonic; the editor supplies it.
- letters: one per item in the list, in order. key is the letter or syllable; stands is what it stands for.
- In Hindi, keep the same keys and order, and translate stands.
- note: 20 words or fewer, one fact about the list.
