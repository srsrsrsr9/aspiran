# How many of the above?

Schema: `upsc-pods/how-many-correct@1` · Folder: `content/how-many-correct/` · Family: Play · Engine: choose

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-how-many-statements

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

The newer UPSC format: two to four statements, and the member picks how many are correct. The app builds the options (only one, only two, ... none) and the answer from truth.
- statements: 2 to 4, each 26 words or fewer, each clearly true or false from the source.
- truth: one true or false per statement, in the same order. Avoid all true or all false more than once in a pack.
- why: 42 words or fewer, naming each false statement and correcting it.
