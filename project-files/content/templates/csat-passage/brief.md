# Reading comprehension (CSAT)

Schema: `upsc-pods/csat-passage@1` · Folder: `content/csat-passage/` · Family: Practise · Engine: choose

**Exam:** prelims, mains · papers: prelims-csat, mains-qualifying · formats: csat-rc, qual-comprehension

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A CSAT passage with two to four questions of the kinds UPSC asks: the main idea, what can be inferred, the assumption the author makes, the most logical corollary, or the tone.
- passage.en: copy the source passage exactly. passage.hi: a faithful translation.
- Answers must follow from the passage alone, never from outside knowledge. The best wrong option is one that is true in the world but not supported by the passage.
- Use at least two different kinds across the questions.
