# Calm now (breathing and grounding)

Schema: `upsc-pods/calm-script@1` · Folder: `content/calm-script/` · Family: Calm · Engine: steps

**Exam:** wellbeing · papers: none (study skill) · formats: none

**Sound:** No effects. The member may add a calm-tones or soundscape bed, off by default.

**Motion:** A circle grows on the inhale and shrinks on the exhale; with reduced motion it is a filling bar instead.

## How to fill it

A short guided exercise for a stressed moment: slow breathing, the double-inhale sigh, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding or a body scan. A human narrator records it later in English and Hindi. It sits beside "Need to talk?", never in place of it, and the app shows the support line from config on every script.
- No health claims. Say what the exercise is and let people judge how it feels. Never "cures", "heals", "treats", "therapy", "proven", "brainwaves", "anxiety disorder" or anything clinical.
- beats: short instructions, one action each. The seconds add up to about the minutes given (within a fifth).
- pace: for slow-breath, inhale + hold + exhale should come to 8 to 12 seconds (5 to 7 breaths a minute). For sigh, a short second inhale is the hold.
- Kind, plain words. No "relax!" commands, no "you should feel". Offer, don't instruct: "if it feels right, close your eyes".
- No sound effects. The optional bed (calm tones or a soundscape) is picked by the member, off by default.
