# Landmark case in 60 seconds

Schema: `upsc-pods/landmark-case@1` · Folder: `content/landmark-case/` · Family: Learn · Engine: reveal

**Exam:** prelims, mains · papers: prelims-gs1, mains-gs2 · formats: pre-direct, mains-answer

**Sound:** A page turn on each flip; a chime on the last card.

**Motion:** Cards flip in 400 ms (a swap with reduced motion); lines on the back appear one by one.

## How to fill it

One judgment on a three-panel card: the question before the court, what it held, and why it matters for the exam.
- case: the name as aspirants cite it. year: from the source.
- question: 24 words or fewer. held: 36 words or fewer, including the bench size or vote only if the source gives it.
- matters: 26 words or fewer, linking to the syllabus.
