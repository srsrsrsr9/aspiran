# Union, State or Concurrent?

Schema: `upsc-pods/union-state-list@1` · Folder: `content/union-state-list/` · Family: Play · Engine: sort

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1, mains-gs2 · formats: pre-select-items

**Sound:** A whoosh as a card flies to its bucket, a snap when it lands, a nudge if it bounces back.

**Motion:** Cards fly to the bucket in 300 ms; a wrong one bounces back with a 400 ms wobble.

## How to fill it

Subjects to sort into the three lists of the Seventh Schedule. The app supplies the bucket names.
- items: 4 to 10 subjects, 6 words or fewer, as the source names them.
- list: union, state or concurrent, exactly as the source says.
- note: 18 words or fewer. Mention any move between lists (for example by the 42nd Amendment) if the source does.
- memoryHook: optional, 18 words or fewer. Only if the list is worth memorising: an acronym, a picture or a tiny story built from the items. Write the Hindi hook natively in Hindi (it may differ from the English). Use null if nothing honest fits; a hook never replaces understanding.
