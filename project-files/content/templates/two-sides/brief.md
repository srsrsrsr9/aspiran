# Two sides (debate)

Schema: `upsc-pods/two-sides@1` · Folder: `content/debates/` · Family: Think

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-gs2, mains-gs3, mains-essay · formats: mains-both-sides, mains-answer

**Sound:** Page turn when a point opens.

**Motion:** Points unfold in 200 ms; the outline appears after all six are open.

## How to fill it

A balanced debate for Mains practice. Equal weight to both sides, no verdict.
- question: a yes-or-no policy question, 16 words or fewer.
- for / against: exactly three points each, 22 words or fewer, each a distinct argument from the source.
- outline: three steps for a 150-word answer: introduce, weigh, conclude with a balanced way forward.
Never say which side is right. Name committees or articles only if the source does.
