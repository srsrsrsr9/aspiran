## How to fill it

Translation practice for Paper A (Hindi): English sentences from the source and a natural Hindi translation for each.
- en: copy each sentence from the source.
- hi: natural, idiomatic Hindi as aspirants write it, not word-for-word. Use standard official terms (अनुच्छेद, संशोधन, लोकसभा).
- note: the one thing that is easy to get wrong in that sentence, in both languages.
