# Committee match

Schema: `upsc-pods/committee-match@1` · Folder: `content/committee-match/` · Family: Play · Engine: match

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-list-match

**Sound:** Tick on a match, a nudge on a miss.

**Motion:** Matched pairs slide together and fade to the done colour in 250 ms.

## How to fill it

Match committees and commissions to what they looked into.
- pairs: 4 to 8. committee is the name as aspirants know it ("Sarkaria Commission"). subject is 8 words or fewer.
- year: as the source gives it, or null. Say in the source whether it is the year set up or the year of the report; do not guess.
- No two committees with the same subject in one pack.
- memoryHook: optional, 18 words or fewer. Only if the list is worth memorising: an acronym, a picture or a tiny story built from the items. Write the Hindi hook natively in Hindi (it may differ from the English). Use null if nothing honest fits; a hook never replaces understanding.
