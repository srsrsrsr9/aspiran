## How to fill it

A sectional Prelims paper in the exam's real formats, for practice under exam conditions but without exam pressure. The member can switch on a timer that counts up; there is never a countdown. Results are shown as a score with its range across the member's recent mocks, never a rank.
- Use the exam's formats in a realistic mix: direct and negative questions, statements with codes, "how many of the above" for statements and pairs, Statement I and II, Statement I, II and III, and List I with List II. Set format to match.
- Every fact comes from the source. Questions are new, written for this mock; never copy or reconstruct a previous-year question.
- statements: the numbered lines for statement, pair and list formats; an empty list for direct and negative questions. For list-match, put List I items first, then List II items, each prefixed "A." / "1." as UPSC prints them.
- options: exactly four. For "how many" formats use "Only one", "Only two", "Only three" (or "All three") and "All four" (or "None") as UPSC does, matching the number of statements.
- Spread the right answers across (a) to (d), roughly evenly, and never more than three of the same letter in a row.
- why: 45 words or fewer, from the source. Name the trap in the most tempting wrong option.
- difficulty: honest. About a third each.
