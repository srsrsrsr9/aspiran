# Quiz night

Schema: `upsc-pods/quiz-pack@1` · Folder: `content/quizzes/` · Family: Play

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-direct, pre-negative

**Sound:** Tick on reveal.

**Motion:** Each question rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A short group quiz. Members discuss, then tap reveal.
- question: 30 words or fewer, answerable from the source alone.
- options: exactly four, similar in length, one clearly right.
- answerIndex: 0 to 3, the same in en and hi.
- explanation: 25 words or fewer, says why the answer is right.
Never write "this week's card said" unless the source says so.
