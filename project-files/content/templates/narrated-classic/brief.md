# Narrated classic (two minutes)

Schema: `upsc-pods/narrated-classic@1` · Folder: `content/narrated-classic/` · Family: Listen · Engine: steps

**Exam:** revision · papers: mains-gs4 · formats: mains-gs4-concepts

**Sound:** No effects over the voice. A soft chime only after the reading ends.

**Motion:** Text follows the reading line by line when audio exists; otherwise it shows in full.

## How to fill it

A two-minute reading of a public-domain passage, read by a human narrator in English and Hindi and labelled "Read by ...". You prepare the text around the reading; the recording is made later.
- passage.en: copy the passage exactly from the source. About 120 to 300 words. passage.hi: null.
- intro: who wrote it and when, from the source. Mention the translator.
- after: about the member's own study, not about war. No battle language aimed at people.
