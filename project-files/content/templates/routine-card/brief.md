# How they worked (routine library)

**Sound:** A page turn per beat; a soft chime at the end.

**Motion:** Each beat slides in from the right in 300 ms; choices fold open in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

One real person's daily routine from a public source, then what an aspirant could borrow and what not to copy. The point, after Mason Currey's Daily Rituals, is that routines vary wildly and there is no one right timetable, which is a good answer to "topper's timetable" anxiety.
- blocks: only times and activities the source gives, in the source's order. Do not fill gaps or round times.
- No quotes unless the source gives the exact words, and then copy them exactly.
- borrow: small and practical (a fixed start, a walk between blocks, a review at night). Never extreme hours, all-nighters or stimulants.
- dontCopy: honest about survivorship bias. Their routine is not why they succeeded.
- question: gentle and specific, never "are you working hard enough?".
