# Spot the better answer

Schema: `upsc-pods/better-answer@1` · Folder: `content/better-answer/` · Family: Learn · Engine: choose

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-gs1, mains-gs2, mains-gs3, mains-gs4 · formats: mains-answer

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

Two Mains answer openings (or conclusions) side by side. The member taps the better one and sees one line on why. Seeing good and weak work side by side builds judgment faster than rules.
- Both answers must be plausible. The weaker one should be generic, vague, or off the question's directive, not absurd.
- The better one uses a specific fact from the source (an article, a year, a number) and answers the directive word.
- Vary which side is better across pairs. better is 0 for A and 1 for B, the same in both languages.
- Write the Hindi answers as a Hindi-medium topper would, not as a word-for-word translation.
