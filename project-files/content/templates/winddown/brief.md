# Close the day (wind-down)

Schema: `upsc-pods/winddown@1` · Folder: `content/winddown/` · Family: Rest

**Exam:** revision · papers: none (study skill) · formats: none

**Sound:** Tanpura drone, only if sound is on.

**Motion:** The lamp dims over the full minute; nothing flashes.

## How to fill it

A one-minute wind-down at the end of a study day. No study content and no numbers.
- start: an invitation to breathe and put the books away, 14 words or fewer.
- cue: what to do while the lamp dims, 12 words or fewer.
- button: 3 words or fewer. end: a warm good night, 6 words or fewer.
