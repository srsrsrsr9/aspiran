# The Hidden Why

Schema: `upsc-pods/hidden-why@1` · Folder: `content/hidden-why/` · Family: Watch · Engine: picture

**Exam:** mains · papers: interview, mains-gs2, mains-gs4, mains-essay · formats: interview-talk, mains-answer

**Sound:** A page turn per frame and a chime on the frame that names the idea. Off until the member turns sound on.

**Motion:** The camera pans across one illustration to each frame's focus point (1.3 s). With motion off, each frame is a still crop.

## How to fill it

A Deepstash-style picture story with a Freakonomics question: why do people do what they do? One idea per story, told in 5 to 8 captions over one tall illustration, then three cards.
- frames: the first caption is a hook that sounds wrong ("Pay people to kill rats, and you can end up with more rats"). Each caption is 16 words or fewer and moves the story one step. The last caption names the idea ("Economists call this the Cobra Effect").
- focus: [x, y, zoom] on the 360x640 illustration for each frame. Use [180, 320, 1] for the whole picture; zoom no more than 2.5.
- named: the idea's name, with a short gloss in brackets if the name alone is opaque.
- sayItSmart: about 40 words the aspirant could say to an interview board or use to open an answer. It carries the evidence (who, where, when) and ends with what they would do with the idea.
- careful: what is contested or unproven. If the popular version of the story has no record, say so and point to the documented one.
- followUp: the question a board member would ask next, and a short hint on how to answer it honestly.
- useItIn: 1 to 3 papers, each with the kind of answer it fits.
- sources: the study, record or law the story stands on, with verify true until a person has checked it. Every number must be in the source text.
- Prefer Indian evidence. No invented dialogue or quotes. No result claims.
