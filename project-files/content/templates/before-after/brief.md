# Before and after

Schema: `upsc-pods/before-after@1` · Folder: `content/before-after/` · Family: Learn · Engine: compare

**Exam:** prelims, mains · papers: prelims-gs1, mains-gs2 · formats: pre-direct, mains-answer

**Sound:** A tick as each row opens.

**Motion:** The 'before' panel slides aside in 300 ms to show 'after'; reduced motion shows both stacked.

## How to fill it

One change in the law, before and after.
- law: what changed (an article, a right). amendment: what changed it.
- before and after: 36 words or fewer each, in parallel wording so the difference stands out.
- why: 30 words or fewer, the reason from the source.
