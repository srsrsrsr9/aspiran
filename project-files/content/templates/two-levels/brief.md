# Explain it twice

Schema: `upsc-pods/two-levels@1` · Folder: `content/two-levels/` · Family: Learn · Engine: reveal

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-gs3 · formats: mains-answer

**Sound:** A page turn on each flip; a chime on the last card.

**Motion:** Cards flip in 400 ms (a swap with reduced motion); lines on the back appear one by one.

## How to fill it

One concept explained twice: simply, then in exam language. Tapping toggles between them.
- simple: 40 words or fewer, with an everyday comparison and no jargon.
- exam: 60 words or fewer, with the precise terms, bodies and numbers from the source.
- keywords: 3 to 5 terms an examiner looks for.
