# Chai in 60 reel

Schema: `upsc-pods/reel@1` · Folder: `content/reels/` · Family: Watch

**Exam:** revision · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-current-static

**Sound:** Tanpura bed at 0.55, motif on the outro.

**Motion:** Segments cut at fixed times; captions are burned in for export.

## How to fill it

A 60-second vertical reel built from the day's Chai cards. The cards fill the middle segments automatically. You write the intro and the outro.
- intro: exactly three lines, 5 words or fewer each: the date, what this is, how many cards.
- outro: two or three lines, 8 words or fewer each, ending on an invitation to read the full cards in the app.
- No urgency, no hype, no "don't miss".
