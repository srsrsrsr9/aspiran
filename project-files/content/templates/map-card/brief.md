# Map card

Schema: `upsc-pods/map-card@1` · Folder: `content/maps/` · Family: Explore

**Exam:** prelims, mains · papers: prelims-gs1, mains-gs1 · formats: pre-map, mains-diagram

**Sound:** Tick when a pin opens or the place is found.

**Motion:** Pins pulse once; no looping animation.

## How to fill it

A schematic map with pins and a "find it" moment. You never write coordinates; you pick anchor ids from the settings.
- lead: 30 words or fewer on why the place matters.
- pins: 2 to 6, each an anchor id from the settings, with a 3-word label and a fact of 20 words or fewer.
- find: a prompt, a hint for a wrong tap, and the line shown when found. targetAnchor is one of the anchor ids.
Never describe borders or disputed territory. The base map draws none.
