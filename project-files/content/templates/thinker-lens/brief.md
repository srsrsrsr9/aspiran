## How to fill it

A general version of the Kautilya lens for any thinker in the quote bank: one line, what it means, how to use it in a GS4 or Essay answer, and a sample sentence for an introduction or conclusion. GS4 asks for the "contributions of moral thinkers and philosophers from India and the world", so this is syllabus, not decoration.
- Never write or paraphrase the quote yourself. Pick its id; the app inserts the exact words and, where the bank has it, the line in its original language and script.
- Prefer the older, classical voices (Kautilya, Thiruvalluvar, the Buddha, Patanjali, the Bhakti and Sharana saints). A thinker is a thinker, never a mascot for a party, a faith or a region. Religious texts are read as philosophy and literature, never as instructions on how to worship.
- meaning and useIt: only from the source. introLine should sound like a real answer, not a slogan, and must not quote the line again.
- If the bank marks the entry as a paraphrase, refer to it as an idea ("Sen argues that..."), never in quote marks.
