# Fact or opinion?

Schema: `upsc-pods/fact-or-opinion@1` · Folder: `content/fact-or-opinion/` · Family: Think · Engine: sort

**Exam:** prelims, mains · papers: prelims-csat, mains-gs2 · formats: csat-rc, mains-answer

**Sound:** A whoosh as a card flies to its bucket, a snap when it lands, a nudge if it bounces back.

**Motion:** Cards fly to the bucket in 300 ms; a wrong one bounces back with a 400 ms wobble.

## How to fill it

Statements to sort into fact (checkable) and opinion (a judgment). Trains the habit Mains examiners reward: back opinions with facts.
- items: 4 to 8 statements, 20 words or fewer, about half facts and half opinions, all on one topic.
- Facts must be true and from the source. Opinions should sound reasonable, the kind found in editorials.
- why: 16 words or fewer. For a fact, how you could check it; for an opinion, what makes it a judgment.
