# Number sense

Schema: `upsc-pods/number-card@1` · Folder: `content/numbers/` · Family: Explore

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-data

**Sound:** None, so the number lands in silence.

**Motion:** Bars grow in 400 ms from a printed axis start.

## How to fill it

One number, made meaningful by 2 to 5 comparisons from the same source.
- value: the hero number, copied exactly. unit: 5 words or fewer.
- lead: completes the sentence after the number, 14 words or fewer.
- chart.bars: 2 to 5 bars from the source, one of them the hero value. Never mix sources with different methods in one chart.
- reference: a meaningful line (1000 for sex ratio parity, 100 for full coverage), or null.
- whatItMeans: 1 to 3 lines. If two sources disagree, say why (survey versus census) instead of picking one.
