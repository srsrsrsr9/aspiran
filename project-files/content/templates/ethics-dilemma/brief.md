## How to fill it

A GS4 case: a short scenario, three possible actions, and what each one leads to. There is no score and no single right answer; the member sees the values each choice serves.
- scenario: 80 words or fewer, second person ("You are the district collector..."). No real names.
- options: exactly three realistic actions. outcome: what likely follows, 30 words or fewer. values: 8 words or fewer, e.g. "impartiality over speed".
- reflect: a closing question, 26 words or fewer.
- Do not call any option right or wrong.
