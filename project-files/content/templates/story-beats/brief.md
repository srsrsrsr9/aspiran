# Story in four beats

Schema: `upsc-pods/story-beats@1` · Folder: `content/story-beats/` · Family: Listen · Engine: steps

**Exam:** revision · papers: mains-gs1, mains-essay · formats: mains-essay

**Sound:** A page turn per beat, an optional soundscape under the story, and a chime at the end.

**Motion:** Each beat slides in from the right in 300 ms; choices fold open in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A history or geography story told in 3 to 5 beats, each a short scene, with an optional soundscape the member can turn on.
- head: 6 words or fewer, like a chapter title. text: 45 words or fewer, vivid but exact; every date and number from the source.
- No invented dialogue, thoughts or quotes.
- scape: a soundscape that fits, or none.
- takeaway: why it matters for the exam, 22 words or fewer.
