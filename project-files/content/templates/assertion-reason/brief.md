# Assertion and reason

Schema: `upsc-pods/assertion-reason@1` · Folder: `content/assertion-reason/` · Family: Play · Engine: choose

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-statement-i-ii

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

The UPSC assertion-reason format: an Assertion (A), a Reason (R), and the four standard options, which the app supplies.
- key a: both true and R explains A. b: both true, but R does not explain A. c: A true, R false. d: A false, R true.
- Use at least two different keys across the items. Option b is the hardest; include one if the source allows.
- why: 36 words or fewer, says for each of A and R whether it is true, and whether R explains A.
