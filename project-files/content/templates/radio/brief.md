# Chai stall radio

Schema: `upsc-pods/radio@1` · Folder: `content/radio/` · Family: Listen

**Exam:** revision · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-current-static

**Sound:** Radio static between stations, a tuning sweep as the dial moves, the chai motif when the last station is heard.

**Motion:** The needle follows the finger; each bulletin types in at reading speed.

## How to fill it

A pretend transistor radio at a chai stall. The member drags the dial between 2 to 4 stations; each plays a short bulletin as captions.
- desk: the show's name, 4 words or fewer.
- stations: each has a callsign (3 words or fewer) and 2 or 3 lines of 16 words or fewer, written to be read aloud. One idea per station.
- signoff: a warm closing line, 12 words or fewer.
Use hedges the source uses ("often", "usually"). Never present a forecast as certain.
