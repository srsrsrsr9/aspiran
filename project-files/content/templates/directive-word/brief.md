# Directive words decoded

Schema: `upsc-pods/directive-word@1` · Folder: `content/directive-word/` · Family: Learn · Engine: reveal

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-gs1, mains-gs2, mains-gs3, mains-gs4 · formats: mains-directive

**Sound:** A page turn on each flip; a chime on the last card.

**Motion:** Cards flip in 400 ms (a swap with reduced motion); lines on the back appear one by one.

## How to fill it

What Mains directive words ask for: discuss, critically examine, analyse and so on.
- word: the directive as it appears in questions.
- means: 20 words or fewer. do: what the answer must contain, 18 words or fewer. avoid: the common mistake, 14 words or fewer.
- In Hindi, use the Hindi form UPSC prints (for example चर्चा कीजिए).
