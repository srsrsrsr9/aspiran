# Match the pairs

Schema: `upsc-pods/match-game@1` · Folder: `content/games/` · Family: Play

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-list-match

**Sound:** Santoor tick on a match; a soft nudge, never a buzzer, on a miss.

**Motion:** Wrong picks wobble for 400 ms.

## How to fill it

Pairs to match. Each round draws 6 at random, so every pair must stand alone.
- article: the short left item (an article number, a schedule, a year). 6 characters or fewer.
- en / hi: what it is, 8 words or fewer. No two pairs should be easy to confuse unless the source makes the distinction.
