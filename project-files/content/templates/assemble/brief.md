# Snap together (put in order)

Schema: `upsc-pods/assemble@1` · Folder: `content/assemble/` · Family: Play

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-order

**Sound:** A tap when a tile is picked, a wooden snap when it locks into place, a soft nudge for the wrong slot, a chime when the whole line is built.

**Motion:** Tiles spring into slots (300 ms, slight overshoot). A wrong tile wobbles and returns. The finished line glows once.

## How to fill it

The member rebuilds a sequence by tapping tiles into slots: the words of the Preamble, the stages of a process, the order of events.
- pieces: 3 to 8 tiles, in the CORRECT order. The app shuffles them. Each tile is 4 words or fewer.
- note: 16 words or fewer, shown as that tile locks in. Use it for the fact that makes the order make sense.
- explain: 40 words or fewer, shown when the line is complete.
The order must be a fact from the source, not a matter of opinion.
