# Timeline

Schema: `upsc-pods/timeline@1` · Folder: `content/timelines/` · Family: Explore

**Exam:** prelims, revision · papers: prelims-gs1, mains-gs1 · formats: pre-order

**Sound:** Santoor tick when a dot opens.

**Motion:** Dots spaced evenly; the detail card rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A tappable timeline of 4 to 9 dated events, oldest first.
- date: YYYY-MM-DD exactly as in the source. If the source gives only a month or year, leave the event out.
- key: true for the two or three dates most worth remembering.
- title: 12 words or fewer. body: 24 words or fewer, saying why the event mattered.
