# Who am I? (clue by clue)

Schema: `upsc-pods/who-am-i@1` · Folder: `content/who-am-i/` · Family: Play · Engine: choose

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-direct

**Sound:** A page turn per clue, a chime when the guess is right, a nudge when it is wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

Clues appear one at a time, hardest first. The member can guess at any point from four options.
- clues: 3 or 4, 16 words or fewer, written in the first person ("I chaired..."). Each clue alone should narrow the field; the last makes it easy.
- options: four names of the same kind (four people, four places), one right. answer is its index.
- reveal: 28 words or fewer.
- Only use facts about the person that the source states.
