# Essay planner

Schema: `upsc-pods/essay-planner@1` · Folder: `content/essay-planner/` · Family: Practise · Engine: steps

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-essay, mains-qualifying · formats: mains-essay, qual-essay

**Sound:** A page turn per beat; an optional soundscape under stories; a chime at the end.

**Motion:** Each beat slides in from the right in 300 ms; choices fold open in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A plan for a 1,000 to 1,200 word essay: what the topic asks, the dimensions to cover, an opening, the counter-view and the close. Also works for the qualifying-paper essay.
- reading: name the narrow reading to avoid (for example, treating "failure" only as exam failure).
- dimensions: 4 to 8, each a different lens, every point from the source.
- opening: a concrete story or example from the source, not a dictionary definition or a quote.
- counter: a fair, strong counter-view. close: returns to the theme without slogans.
