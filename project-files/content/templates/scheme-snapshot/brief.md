# Scheme snapshot

Schema: `upsc-pods/scheme-snapshot@1` · Folder: `content/scheme-snapshot/` · Family: Learn · Engine: reveal

**Exam:** prelims, mains · papers: prelims-gs1, mains-gs2, mains-gs3 · formats: pre-current-static

**Sound:** A page turn on each flip; a chime on the last card.

**Motion:** Cards flip in 400 ms (a swap with reduced motion); lines on the back appear one by one.

## How to fill it

A government scheme on one card: who runs it, for whom, the number to remember, and the exam angle.
- launched: the year from the source, or null.
- keyNumber: the benefit amount or target, exactly as the source gives it.
- examAngle: 20 words or fewer, the GS paper or issue it links to.
- Present the scheme's design neutrally. No praise, no criticism.
