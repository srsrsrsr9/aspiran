# How many pairs are correctly matched?

Schema: `upsc-pods/pairs-count@1` · Folder: `content/pairs-count/` · Family: Play · Engine: choose

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-how-many-pairs

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

The newer Prelims format: three or four pairs, and the member counts how many are correctly matched. The app builds the options (only one, only two, all, none) and the answer from correct.
- Mix right and wrong pairs. A wrong pair should be a plausible swap (a park in the neighbouring state), never absurd.
- fix: for a wrong pair, the right match from the source.
