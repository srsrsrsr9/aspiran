# Shabd: bilingual terms

Schema: `upsc-pods/word-bank@1` · Folder: `content/word-bank/` · Family: Revise · Engine: reveal

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-qualifying, mains-gs4 · formats: qual-usage, qual-translation, mains-gs4-concepts

**Sound:** A page turn on each flip; a chime on the last card.

**Motion:** Cards flip in 400 ms (a swap with reduced motion); lines on the back appear one by one.

## How to fill it

Official English and Hindi terms side by side, for Hindi-medium and bilingual aspirants. The card shows the English term; flipping shows the Hindi term, and each language's meaning.
- term: the official term in each language (for example Ordinance and अध्यादेश). Use the standard Hindi term.
- meaning: 18 words or fewer, with the article if the source gives it.
- example: a natural sentence, 20 words or fewer.
