# Odd one out

Schema: `upsc-pods/odd-one-out@1` · Folder: `content/odd-one-out/` · Family: Play · Engine: choose

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-negative

**Sound:** Tick on a pick, a santoor chime when right, a soft nudge (never a buzzer) when wrong.

**Motion:** The picked option settles in 150 ms; the explanation rises in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

Four items; one does not belong. The member taps it, then sees the rule.
- items: exactly four, 5 words or fewer each. Exactly one breaks a rule the other three share.
- The rule must be a fact from the source, not taste. Avoid sets where two items could be the odd one.
- why: states the shared rule first, then why the odd one breaks it.
