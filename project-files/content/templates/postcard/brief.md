# Postcard from a place

Schema: `upsc-pods/postcard@1` · Folder: `content/postcards/` · Family: Listen

**Exam:** revision · papers: prelims-gs1, mains-gs1 · formats: pre-map

**Sound:** A soundscape for the place (waves, rain, wind, a market), a card flip, and a postmark thud.

**Motion:** The card flips in 400 ms; the postmark lands in 200 ms. Reduced motion swaps sides without the flip.

## How to fill it

A postcard from a place in the syllabus: a national park, a strait, a heritage site. The front sets a mood with sound; the back holds three facts.
- place: 4 words or fewer. greeting: the front of the card, 12 words or fewer.
- stamp: one word for the postage stamp, e.g. "Mangrove".
- scape: the soundscape that fits (waves, rain, wind, market, forest).
- facts: exactly three, 20 words or fewer each, written warmly like a note to a friend but exact like an exam answer.
