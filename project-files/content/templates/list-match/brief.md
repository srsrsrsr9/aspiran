# Match List I with List II

Schema: `upsc-pods/list-match@1` · Folder: `content/list-match/` · Family: Play · Engine: match

**Exam:** prelims · papers: prelims-gs1 · formats: pre-list-match

**Sound:** Tick on a match, a nudge on a miss.

**Motion:** Matched pairs slide together and fade to the done colour in 250 ms.

## How to fill it

The Match List I with List II format, played as matching. Exactly four pairs, each one-to-one, all from the source.
- listI and listII: two or three words naming what each list holds.
- Avoid pairs where two List I items could share a List II answer.
