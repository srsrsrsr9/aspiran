# Précis (qualifying paper)

Schema: `upsc-pods/precis@1` · Folder: `content/precis/` · Family: Practise · Engine: steps

**Exam:** mains · papers: mains-qualifying · formats: qual-precis

**Sound:** Silent while the member writes; a soft chime at the end.

**Motion:** Each beat slides in from the right in 300 ms; choices fold open in 200 ms.

## How to fill it

A précis for the qualifying papers: reduce the passage to about one-third, in your own words, with a title (precisTitle). The passage and the précis are in one language (English for Paper B, Hindi for Paper A); the coaching around them is in both.
- passage: copy the source passage exactly.
- precis: about one-third of the passage's word count, in your own words, keeping the main argument and the order of ideas. No new ideas, no examples the passage does not have.
- steps: what you kept, what you cut and why.
