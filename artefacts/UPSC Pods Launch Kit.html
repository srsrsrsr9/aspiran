Channel 3 · Play Store search

Play Store listing

Copy for the store page, checked against Google Play's limits: 30 characters for the name, 80 for the short description and 4,000 for the full description. The Hindi listing is written in Hindi, not translated word for word.

Keywords this copy is written to rank for: UPSC study group, UPSC current affairs Hindi, UPSC daily quiz, IAS preparation, करेंट अफेयर्स हिंदी. The name "UPSC Pods" still needs the legal check flagged in the plan.

Channel 3 · Play Store search

Store screenshots

Five screenshots, in the order people swipe through them. Each one shows how the app feels to use, not a list of features, and none shows a streak, a countdown or a rank.

Names and messages in these screenshots are sample data and will be marked that way on the store. Built as layouts at 9:16. The final files are exported at 1080 × 1920 from real app screens once the closed test ends. Scroll sideways to see all five.

Channel 1 · Product loops

Share cards

These are the cards members choose to post to WhatsApp status or Instagram stories. Each one carries the app name and a Play link. Members only ever share them by choice, and no card shows a mood, a pod message or a rank. The calculator card on the right is live, so try your own numbers.

Try the calculator

Rules used: General 6 attempts to age 32, OBC 9 to 35, SC/ST unlimited to 37, age counted on 1 August of the exam year. Without a birth month it takes the cautious case, so someone born after August may have one more year. These come from the founding brief and must be checked against the official CSE 2027 notice before launch. Disability and other relaxations are not included yet.

Channel 1 · Product loops

Parent page

A one-page note a member can send home in one tap. It explains the year in plain words, gives families a few things to say and a few to avoid, and lists where to find help. It never shows the member's own progress or mood.

Channels 2 and 4 · Video and communities

Week-1 posts

The first posts for launch week, one per channel. They are written to be posted by a person, the founder or a pod lead, and they follow each platform's habits. Check r/UPSC's self-promotion rules before the Reddit post goes up.

Partnerships · draft only

Author outreach

A short email to the living authors whose work shaped the app: Barbara Oakley, Kathy Sierra, BJ Fogg, Shiv Khera and Yandamuri Veerendranath. It asks for a 30-second message to aspirants and nothing else, and it has not been sent. Any yes becomes a "Hear it from them" item in the app. We never use their photos or voices without written permission, and never an AI voice.

Email · send only after your review

Subject: A 30-second message for young people preparing for India's civil services exam? Dear [Name], I'm [your name], and I'm building UPSC Pods, a free study app for people preparing for India's Civil Services exam. About 10 lakh (1 million) people apply each year and only around a thousand are selected, so most of them spend years preparing, often alone and far from home. Your work shaped how the app works. [One specific line, e.g. for Barbara Oakley: "Close the Day asks for one thing you can recall from memory, which comes straight from your writing on retrieval practice."] We credit you by name inside the app, with a link to your book and to free ways to read it. Would you be willing to record a 30-second message to these aspirants, in your own words, about learning or keeping going? A phone recording is perfect. We would play it in the app with your name and a link to your work. To be clear, this is not a request to endorse the app, and we won't present it as one. If you'd rather not, no reply is needed, and we'll keep crediting your work either way. With thanks, [Your name] [Role, UPSC Pods · email · phone]

Before sending: fill in the one line on what came from each author's work, and use each author's official contact page rather than a personal address.
