## Main store listing
**App name** (max 30): `Aspiran: UPSC prep, together`

**Short description** (max 80):
`UPSC prep in a small pod: daily current affairs, practice, mocks, an AI buddy.`

**Full description:**
```text
Never prep alone.

Aspiran is a study app for UPSC Civil Services aspirants. You join a small pod of aspirants at your stage, in your language, and prepare side by side.

What's inside
• Your pod: a small group at the same stage and in the same language, with a quiet chat and an Answer hour where you compare answers in pairs.
• Daily Chai: short current-affairs cards each morning, each with an exam angle and a question.
• Practice: Prelims questions, mock papers, CSAT sets, flashcards, timelines and matching games, Mains answer skeletons and GS4 case studies.
• Mains writing: write an answer, mark it yourself first, then get a second opinion from AI, and rewrite once.
• Your syllabus map: every topic, marked Not started, Met, Can recall or Can use. No percentages, no ranks.
• Review that comes back just before you'd forget.
• Chotu, your AI study buddy. Always labelled AI, quiet at night, and never comments on your feelings, ranks or results.
• Your room: a private journal, notes, sleep log and to-dos that stay on your phone.

How it feels
No streaks, no timers, no leaderboards. Sound is off by default. Every session has an end. You can pause the app for a week or a month, and result-day mode keeps things quiet for 72 hours.

English and Hindi.

Aspiran is free to use. Aspiran Plus adds more AI marking and more of the deeper tools.

Aspiran is an independent app run by SnapSkill LLP. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Union Public Service Commission or any government body. Past UPSC papers in the app come from upsc.gov.in.

Privacy policy: https://upsc-pods-production.chotu-aec.workers.dev/legal/privacy
```
