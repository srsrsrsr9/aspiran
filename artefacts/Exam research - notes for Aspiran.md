# Exam research: notes for Aspiran

The main research (`reports/Exam success tools and methods.md` and the parts that follow) is written
from a neutral test-prep point of view, for all exams. This file keeps the Aspiran-specific takeaways
separately. Date: 1 November 2026.

## Where Aspiran's rules already match what the research shows
- **Public study-hour rankings cause harm.** YPT (Korea) is the best-known small-group study app, and a
  Singapore student paper describes the pressure its hours competition creates. Aspiran's "no public
  scores" rule avoids this.
- **Streaks and countdown timers** are built into Forest, Habitica, Streaks, Pomodoro apps and most habit
  books. Aspiran's rules rule them out, but habit stacking, environment design and focus blocks are fine.
- **Regulators go after rankings, pass guarantees and answer-lookup apps.** China (Double Reduction,
  photo-search suspension) and India (CCPA fines on Vision IAS, Drishti IAS and 26 others) both did.
  Aspiran's honest-claims rule is on the right side of this.

## Openings
- **No bilingual (English and Hindi) spaced-repetition product clearly leads for UPSC.** QuizCherry (free,
  English and Hindi, daily quizzes with "spaced revision" for UPSC, SSC, banking) is the closest found.
- **No one has checked AI Mains-answer graders against real examiners.** SuperKalam, PadhAI, UPSC AI, Vaidra,
  PrepAiro, AnswerWriting.com, PW OnlyIAS all make unchecked claims.
- **No Indian book applies learning science to UPSC.** Topper habits (revision cycles, PYQs, mocks,
  daily answer writing) are spacing, retrieval and feedback under other names. The only Indian topper
  strategy books an AI tool offered turned out to be invented.
- **Peer study happens in unmoderated places**: Telegram groups (piracy, scams), paid reading rooms,
  coaching hubs (the 2024 Rau's IAS basement deaths).

## Product decisions this supports
- Use the open FSRS scheduler (there is a TypeScript version, ts-fsrs) rather than inventing a "smart"
  algorithm.
- Chotu can't win on being a tutor alone: ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude all have free study modes since 2025.
- Borrow East Asian habits that travel well: mistake notebooks (错题本 / 오답노트), quiet shared study time,
  honest mocks without national rankings.
