# Marketing content task list, 12 Nov 2026

Built for the new funnel. Looks, colours, fonts and voice are in `artefacts/marketing-prompt-2026-11-12.md` (fresh, 12 Nov); use that
brief with this list when you make the pieces.

## The one rule: every piece ends at the same door

| Step | Where | What we count |
|---|---|---|
| 1. See | A post, a short, a Telegram message, a search result | Views, saves, shares |
| 2. Try | **"Try one topic free, no sign-up"** (the Federalism demo), or a public topic page | Demo starts, demo finishes |
| 3. Join | Sign in, land in a starter room, get tomorrow's Chai | Sign-ups, day-2 and day-7 return |
| 4. Pay | Plus, after a marked answer or a mock | Paid |

So: **one link everywhere** (the demo, with a tag saying where the click came from, e.g. `?utm_source=telegram`).
No post sends people to a waiting list. No post sells Plus to a stranger.

## First, a line on "UGC"

You asked for content "posted by random people with no association with Aspiran". Two different things hide in that:

- **Real user content:** people who use Aspiran post about it because they want to. This is the best marketing there
  is, and the tasks in Part C are about earning it.
- **Our content, posted by others as if they'd found us themselves:** I won't plan this. It is misleading under
  India's Consumer Protection Act and the ASCI influencer rules, breaks Instagram, YouTube and Reddit's own rules, and
  UPSC communities are small and sharp: one exposed fake review would undo the "anti-ed-tech" position the brand
  stands on.

The honest middle is Part B: creators and ambassadors who are paid or given Plus, **and say so** ("#ad", "paid
partnership", "I'm an Aspiran ambassador"). It works nearly as well and can't blow up.

## Part C. Real user content (earned, not written)

You can't write this. You can make it easy and worth sharing.

| # | Task | What it produces |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Share cards inside the app** (opt-in, never automatic): "My garden after 30 days", "Sure and right: 87 in 100", "My self-marks are now 1 point off". No ranks, no scores against others | Posts users want to show |
| C2 | A weekly public prompt on Telegram and Instagram: "Post your answer skeleton for this question", "Post your desk". Reshare the best with credit | A steady stream of real posts |
| C3 | "Pod of the month": a pod tells its own story, in its own words, if it wants to | Stories no brand post can match |
| C4 | Ask at the right moment: after a user's third marked answer, one line: "Did this help? Tell a friend who's preparing" with a ready link | Referrals |
| C5 | A referral that gives, not bribes: both get a month of Plus. Said plainly | Word of mouth |
| C6 | Collect what users say (with a yes) on one page: the good and the critical | The proof section of the website |
| C7 | Reply to every public mention within a day, including the harsh ones | Trust |

**Not on this list, on purpose:** fake reviews, sock-puppet Telegram or Reddit accounts, posts in aspirant groups
pretending to be a user, paid posts without a label, and "toppers recommend" lines without a named, consenting topper.
