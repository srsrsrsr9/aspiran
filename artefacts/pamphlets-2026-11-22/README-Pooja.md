# Aspiran pamphlets (22 Nov 2026)

Two pamphlets, each two-sided: **English on the front, Hindi on the back.** Each comes in colour and in black and white.
The print files are in `pdf/`.

| File | What | Print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Aspiran-students-A5-colour.pdf` | Students, A5 | A5 paper, double-sided, flip on long edge |
| `Aspiran-students-A5-mono.pdf` | Students, A5, black and white | same, or photocopy |
| `Aspiran-students-2up-on-A4-colour.pdf` | Students, two per A4 sheet | A4 landscape, double-sided, flip on **short** edge, cut down the dotted middle line |
| `Aspiran-students-2up-on-A4-mono.pdf` | same, black and white | cheapest: photocopy, then cut |
| `Aspiran-centres-A4-colour.pdf` | Coaching centres and study libraries, A4 | A4, double-sided, flip on long edge |
| `Aspiran-centres-A4-mono.pdf` | same, black and white | same, or photocopy |

**At the print shop:** say "actual size / 100%, no fit-to-page". Ask for one test copy first and scan both QR codes
with your phone before printing the rest. 100 to 130 gsm paper is fine for students; 130 to 170 gsm looks better for centres.

**Where the QR codes go**
- Students: straight into the app's free sign-up (Hindi side opens it in Hindi).
- Centres: aspiran.in/for-coaching, where they can see what students see and book a 15-minute call.
Each QR is tagged with the pamphlet, the print run, the language and colour or black-and-white, so we can see which version brings people in.

**For centres:** the white box "Your Aspiran contact" is for you. Write your name and phone number (or stamp it) before
you hand it over. If a centre asks about price: the first 30 days are free with one or two batches, and the price after
that depends on their size; the owner will quote. Don't promise a price, discount or "your own app".

**Don't say** anything the pamphlet doesn't: no ranks, toppers or selection chances, and don't say a person checks
the answers (they are marked in the app).

To change wording: edit `build.py`, then run it and `render.sh` (Claude can do this).
