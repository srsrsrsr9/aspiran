What your centre gets

Your name, your colour

Students join with your code; from then on the app shows your logo and colour, with “with Aspiran” under it.

Every answer, marked in minutes

Students write answers daily and get marks on five parts, with a fix for each. A teacher view to confirm marks is being built with our first centres.

Who has gone quiet

Weak topics per batch, and the students who haven’t turned up for a few days, so you can talk to them before they drop out.

Batch chat and announcements

Each batch has its own chat, with announcements pinned at the top. Teachers can hide messages and see what was reported.

Doubts and study plans

कौन चुप हो गया

बैच चैट और घोषणाएँ

हर बैच का अपना चैट, ऊपर पिन की हुई घोषणाएँ। शिक्षक संदेश छिपा सकते हैं और रिपोर्ट देख सकते हैं।

डाउट और स्टडी प्लान
