# 100 explainer videos (numbers 201–300), 25 Nov 2026

Three formats, kept from the 25 Nov test (owner liked all three):
- **Calm**: real nature or place footage plus drawn diagrams, soft fades, captions low on screen, voice only.
- **Fast**: mission or tech footage (or quick diagrams), hook first, hard cuts, big centre words, whooshes and a soft pulse.
- **Photo**: photos of places, buildings and documents with slow pan and zoom, numbers that count up, captions in a box, a soft drone.

Voice: ElevenLabs Sumeet while credits last, then Fish Shilpa. English first; 45–75 seconds each.
**Claude makes 201–250. The owner's other Claude makes 251–300** with `artefacts/content-assignment-2026-11-25-explainers.md`.
Packs: `content/motion/shorts/2001-20` … `2001-24` (Claude), `2001-25` … `2001-29` (other Claude), 10 per pack.
Existing shorts 1–100 cover some neighbouring topics; these were picked not to repeat them.

Picture ideas are starting points; every source must pass the licence rules (see the assignment).
