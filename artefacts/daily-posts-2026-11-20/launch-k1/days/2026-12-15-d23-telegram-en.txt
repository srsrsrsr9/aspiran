How is the CAG removed from office?
(a) Like a Supreme Court judge
(b) By the Prime Minister's order
(c) By a simple vote of the Lok Sabha
(d) By the Public Accounts Committee
Correct option: (a)
Explanation: Article 148(1): removal in like manner and on like grounds as a Judge of the Supreme Court.
