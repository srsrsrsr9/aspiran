On which date was Purna Swaraj Day observed in 1930?
(a) 26 January
(b) 26 November
(c) 15 August
(d) 2 October
Correct option: (a)
Explanation: After the Lahore session of December 1929, the Congress asked people to observe 26 January 1930 as Independence Day.
