*How to write a 150-word Mains answer.*

1. Read the directive word. 'Discuss' wants both sides; 'critically examine' wants a judgement with reasons.
2. Intro: 1 or 2 lines of context.
3. Body: 3 or 4 short points, one idea each. Draw a sketch where it saves words.
4. Conclusion: 1 line that answers the question.

A 250-word answer keeps the same shape, with more points and an example.
