Studying alone? Revising doesn't have to be.

Self-study can mean whole days without saying a topic out loud to anyone. In Aspiran you can study in a pod: a small study group of aspirants. Pods also have pod friends. They are study helpers, not members, so every member is an aspirant like you. Like the men around the elephant, each of you holds one part. Put the parts together.
