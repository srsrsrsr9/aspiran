---- Caption ----
Did you know? Konark's tallest part is no longer there.

The Sun temple at Konark was built as the Sun god's chariot, on 12 pairs of carved wheels. Its great tower, said to reach about 70 m, proved too heavy for its site and fell in the 19th century.
