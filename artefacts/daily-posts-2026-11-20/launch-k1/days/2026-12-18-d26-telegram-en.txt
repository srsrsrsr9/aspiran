Did you know?

Operation Flood grew out of a strike. Farmers in Kaira, Gujarat, stopped selling milk for 15 days, and their demand for their own co-operative was accepted. Phase I later linked milksheds to Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai. Why it matters: GS3 asks about co-operatives and dairy, and this is the Indian example to use.
