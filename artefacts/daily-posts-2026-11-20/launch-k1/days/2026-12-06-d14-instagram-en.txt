Did you know? England once banned printed cotton cloth.

A 1720 law, the Calico Act, banned printed cotton textiles (chintz) in England. By the 1880s the tide had turned: two-thirds of the cotton clothes Indians wore were made of cloth from Britain.

Why it matters: the decline of Indian crafts under colonial rule is a recurring GS1 theme.
