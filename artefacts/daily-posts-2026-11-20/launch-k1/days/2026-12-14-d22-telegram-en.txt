Did you know?

India's first satellite, Aryabhata, was designed and built in India, but it went up on someone else's rocket. It was launched on 19 April 1975 from the Soviet Union. Why it matters: Prelims often asks about "firsts" in space, and the detail it tests is the launcher and the place.
