Did you know? There is a temple in Gujarat you walk down into.

Rani ki Vav in Patan is a stepwell designed as an inverted temple. Each level takes you down towards sacred water. UNESCO put it on the World Heritage List in 2014.

Why it matters: Prelims asks what makes each heritage site special.
