RBI keeps the repo rate unchanged आरबीआई ने रेपो दर नहीं बदली The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee kept the policy repo rate unchanged. The repo rate is the rate at which the RBI lends to banks for short periods; changing it is the main tool of monetary policy. The committee has six members, three from the RBI.
