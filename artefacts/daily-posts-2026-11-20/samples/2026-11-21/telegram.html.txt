<b>1. RBI keeps the repo rate unchanged</b>
<i>आरबीआई ने रेपो दर नहीं बदली</i>
The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee kept the policy repo rate unchanged. The repo rate is the rate at which the RBI lends to banks for short periods; changing it is the main tool of monetary policy. The committee has six members, three from the RBI.
