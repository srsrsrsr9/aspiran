📖 The Farmer Who Brought Gandhi to Champaran

A farmer wanted one thing. A busy man's single day. Gandhi tells it in his autobiography. Lucknow, nineteen sixteen. An indigo farmer, Rajkumar Shukla, catches hold of him.

Rajkumar Shukla follows Gandhi from city to city until he comes to see the indigo fields, 1917.
