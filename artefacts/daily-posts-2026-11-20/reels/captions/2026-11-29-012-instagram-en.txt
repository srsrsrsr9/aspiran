📌 Konark: A Temple Built as a Chariot

A temple built as a chariot. The wheels are still there. On the shore of the Bay of Bengal, in Odisha, lie the ruins of Konark's Sun temple.

Twenty-four wheels, seven horses, the sun god's chariot in stone, and the wheels as sundials.
