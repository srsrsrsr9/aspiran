📌 Why Deserts Sit Near Thirty Degrees

Why do the hot deserts sit in a belt? Blame the air. At the equator the sun is strong. Air heats and rises by convection, leaving a low pressure, the Inter Tropical Convergence Zone.

Rising air at the equator, sinking air further out: the circulation that makes the Thar and the Sahara.
