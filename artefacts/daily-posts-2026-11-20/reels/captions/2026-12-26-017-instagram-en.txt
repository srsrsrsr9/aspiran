📖 Hampi's Market, Seen by a Traveller

A city where some fair was on every single day. Around fifteen twenty, a Portuguese traveller, Domingos Paes, visited Vijayanagara, which he called Bisnaga, in the reign of Krishnadevaraya.

A Portuguese visitor walks Vijayanagara's bazaars and is astonished.
