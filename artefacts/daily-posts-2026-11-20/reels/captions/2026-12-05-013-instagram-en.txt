🗺️ The Cloud Messenger's Route

A letter with no postman. So the sender asks a cloud. In Kalidasa's poem, the Meghaduta, a yaksha, a servant of Kubera, is exiled for a year at Ramagiri.

Kalidasa's exiled yaksha sends a monsoon cloud north from Ramagiri to Alaka; the rivers and towns on the way.
