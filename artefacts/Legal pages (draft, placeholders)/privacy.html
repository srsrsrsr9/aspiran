Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]. Text in yellow is filled in before launch.

UPSC Pods is a study companion for UPSC aspirants. This page says what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. [Legal entity name] ("we") is the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What stays on your device

Your journal, mood check-ins, Close the Day notes, profile details beyond the four matching fields, your roadmap and goal answers, and your focus logs stay on your phone or in your browser. We never receive them.

What we keep on our servers

  • Your account. Sign-in is with Google only. We keep your Google account id and email address, to sign you in and to help if you contact us.
  • Pod matching. A random member id and four fields (stage, attempt band, language, time band), so we can place you in a pod.
  • Pod messages. What you post in your pod, so the pod can read it, and reports and moderation notes when something is reported.
  • Usage counts. Which features were used and when (for example "joined a pod" or "built a roadmap"), one-tap ratings and the day-7 check-in answers. Never the text of your mood, journal or chats. We use no third-party trackers or advertising SDKs.
  • Payments. For Plus: the order id, amount, status and Cashfree's payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details go to Cashfree, not to us. The mobile number Cashfree asks for is passed to Cashfree and not stored by us.
  • Consent records. What you agreed to, and when.

Chotu and the pod friends

When you ask Chotu online, the messages in that chat are sent to an AI provider to write a reply. Chotu never sees your journal, mood, nickname or stage. Messages that look like distress are not sent; the app shows help instead. Chotu and the pod friends (Meera, Arjun, Pooja and Kabir) are AI, and are always labelled "AI".

Who we share with

Only the services that run the app: Google Firebase (sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting and storage), the AI provider for Chotu's online replies, and Cashfree (payments). We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your rights

You can export or delete your data at any time in the app: Me → Privacy. Deleting removes your server data, your pod membership and your sign-in account. You can withdraw consent the same way. For anything else, or to raise a grievance, write to our Grievance Officer, [Grievance officer name], at [Support email]. We reply within 7 days.

Age

UPSC Pods is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change applies.
