Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: [date]. Text in yellow is filled in before launch.

Plus is a one-time yearly payment

You pay once for a year of Plus. Nothing renews automatically, so there is nothing to cancel.

7-day refund

If Plus isn't right for you, ask for a full refund within 7 days of paying. Write to [Support email] with your receipt number (Me → Plus → My receipts). No questions asked.

After 7 days

We don't normally refund after 7 days. If something went wrong on our side (for example you were charged twice, or Plus never switched on), write to us and we'll put it right.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the way you paid, through Cashfree. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show them. When a refund is made, Plus ends on that account.

Failed payments

If money left your account but Plus didn't switch on, wait a few minutes and open Me → Plus. If it still isn't on, write to us with the time and the amount. Failed payments are returned by your bank or by Cashfree automatically.
