## Costs and limits found
- Voices: Fish is pay-as-you-go; ElevenLabs Starter gives 40,000 characters a month (one 60 s video ≈ 900).
- Licensed footage is thin for India-specific topics: ISRO (GODL-India) and Commons cover space and monuments well;
  rain, rivers and daily life need Pexels/Pixabay or our own drawings. NASA globe footage shows national borders, so
  it's out.
- CC BY-SA pictures pass their licence on to the video; prefer public-domain and GODL-India.
