# Aspiran trailer, "Page 42" (13 Nov 2026)

## What's here
- `aspiran-trailer-1080p.mp4`: the master. 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC audio. Runs **1:39.9**.
- `aspiran-trailer-vertical.mp4`: 1080×1920 for Reels, Shorts and Status. Every scene is laid out again for the tall
  frame (no black bars, no letterbox). Same sound.
- `script.md`: the final script with exact times, the cast and voices, the shot list, and the lines that were cut.
- `scenes/`: the picture. Each scene is drawn in HTML, SVG and canvas (`film.js`, with the characters in `chars.js`).
  Open `scenes/index.html?t=42` in Chrome to see any moment.
- `audio/`: what each character says (`lines.json`), the chosen voices (`voices.json`), the sound effects list
  (`sfx.json`), and the voice and effect recordings.
- `build/`: the scripts that make everything (listed under "How to make it again").

Captions are burned into the picture throughout. Hindi lines carry an English line under them.

## The story
In October an aspirant highlights page 42 of her polity book for the sixth time, and by March she can't answer
it. Jeetu, a mentor who has seen it all, shows her a better method: answer first, review just before you forget,
write and mark answers, and work in a pod. Chotu, the chai cup, interrupts every serious moment. By March she answers
in three short phrases, and the film ends on "Try one topic free · aspiran.in".

## Fallbacks (where the result differs from the brief)
1. **Narrator voice.** The free ElevenLabs plan refuses library voices, Vanishree included (error 402). The narrator
   is Aadya, a voice already on this account, which speaks Indian English and Hindi. The aspirant is "Student",
   also an account voice.
2. **Chotu and Jeetu voices.** Voice design is refused on the free plan (error 403), and so are the library's Indian
   voices. Chotu is the stock voice Liam and Jeetu is the stock voice George. Both are on eleven_v4, told in each line
   to speak as Indian men, with emotion directions (dry, whispering, proud and so on). Chotu is pitched up slightly
   afterwards. They sound Indian-accented, but they are not native Indian voices.
3. **Music.** The ElevenLabs music feature is refused on the free plan (error 402). The score is original and is
   generated by code (`build/mix.py`): felt piano, pads, plucks, a cello-like line, drums and risers, in D minor
   lifting to F major. It ducks under every spoken line. It is clean and in time with the cuts, but simpler than a
   composed score.
4. **Sound effects** come from ElevenLabs (27 effects) as requested, at 128 kbps. The higher-quality format is refused
   on the free plan.
5. **App screens.** Four screens are real captures from app.aspiran.in: the first question, the same question after
   answering, the Hindi first question, and the Indian Polity page. The "How sure are you?" card, the second-opinion
   panel, the Chai cards and the pod ring are drawn to look like the app, but they are not captures.
6. **Takes were checked by machine, not by ear.** I can't listen. Each line was transcribed back with ElevenLabs
   speech-to-text and redone until the words matched, names included ("Jeetu bhaiya" and "Aspiran" needed
   respelling). Delivery (whether a joke lands) still needs a human ear.
7. **Lines cut for time.** 11 recorded lines were cut to stay under 100 seconds. They're listed in `script.md` and can be
   put back.

## How to make it again
Run in macOS Terminal (zsh), from the `app` folder:

```
zsh artefacts/trailer-2026-11-13/build/make.sh
```

It needs Node 22+, Python 3 with numpy and scipy, ffmpeg and Google Chrome. It uses no internet and no ElevenLabs
credits. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes and writes both MP4s into this folder.

Recordings: the voice takes (`audio/vo/*.mp3`) and effects (`audio/sfx/*.mp3`) are kept out of git, together with
the videos and `_work/`, so they exist only on this Mac. Back up the folder if you want to rebuild later without
spending credits. To re-record lines, you type the key into the command yourself (it's never saved), for example:

```
ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=... python3 artefacts/trailer-2026-11-13/build/vo.py c04 j10
```

Then run `make.sh` again. Line text is in `audio/lines.json` and the timing of the film is in `build/timeline.py`.
Effects are re-made with `build/sfx.py` in the same way. `build/capture-screens.mjs` re-captures the real app screens.
