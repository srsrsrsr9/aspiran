# Aspiran trailer: "Page 42" (final script, 13 Nov 2026)

Running time **1:39.9**. Master 1920×1080, 30 fps; vertical 1080×1920 re-framed from the same scenes.
Times below are exact (they come from `build/timeline.json`, which both the picture and the mix read).

**Logline.** An aspirant highlights page 42 of her polity book for the sixth time in October and can't answer it in
March. Jeetu, the mentor who has seen it all, walks in with a steel tumbler and turns her reading into remembering,
while Chotu, the cheeky chai cup, gets in the way of every serious moment.

**Cast.** The aspirant (illustrated young woman, teal kurta, pencil in her bun). Chotu (the clay chai cup, AI badge
on as always). Jeetu (illustrated older man, grey hair, round spectacles, maroon shawl, steel tumbler). Narrator.

## Shot list and lines

### 1. Cold open: October, page 42 (0:00 to 0:09.3)
Black. A desk-lamp click; warm light flickers on. Night room, a window with the moon, a door on the right. HUD types
on: OCTOBER · PAGE 42 · READING NO. 6. Slow push in on the open book ("Federalism", "Seventh Schedule"); her hand
highlights line after line in saffron until the whole page glows.
- 0:01.30 CHOTU: Page forty-two. Again?
- Rack focus to her (book and Chotu blur). 0:03.52 ASPIRANT (tired): Sixth time. It's sticking.
- Pan to Chotu, then a whip zoom to the page corner: even the "42" is highlighted. 0:06.45 CHOTU (deadpan): You've highlighted the page number.
- SFX: lamp click, highlighter squeaks, whoosh and a wooden "bonk" on the zoom. Music: felt-piano motif, then a two-note "ba-dum".

### 2. Montage: the grind (0:09.3 to 0:14.5)
Three 300-page monthly magazines (NOVEMBER, DECEMBER, JANUARY) slam onto the book, each with a camera shake. Phone
notifications drop in: "Day 47 streak! Don't break it now", "Only 2 seats left at this price · Offer ends in 09:59".
A pop-up springs open: YOUR SELECTION CHANCE 73% ✦ calculated by our advanced system ✦.
- 0:10.61 CHOTU (scoffs): 73%? Says who, your horoscope?
- One more "Day 48. Don't lose it!" and the room spins and blurs into black with a reverse-cymbal suck. Music: a
  132 bpm staccato ostinato that piles up and is cut dead.

### 3. The turn: the leak (0:14.5 to 0:27.3)
Night sky. "March" rises in big letters; an exam question card: "Which Schedule of the Constitution divides powers
between the Union and the States?" She appears at the edge, mouth open, a sweat drop.
- 0:15.07 NARRATOR: And then… March.
- The October page floats in the dark; its saffron highlights and words lift off as dust and blow away.
- 0:17.84 NARRATOR: What you read in October… is gone by March.
- Silence; a deep drone. A cyan forgetting curve draws itself and plunges. HUD: EBBINGHAUS · 1885 · THE FORGETTING CURVE.
- Warm light opens from a door on the right; a long shadow. 0:22.45 JEETU (off screen): You don't have a reading
  problem, beta. You have a forgetting problem.

### 4. Jeetu's entrance (0:27.3 to 0:32.0)
Back at the desk. Chotu's steam shoots bolt upright; he slides behind the book.
- 0:27.39 CHOTU (urgent whisper): Oh no. It's Jeetu bhaiya. Act normal.
- The door swings open, light floods in, a silhouette steps through. Push in as Jeetu is lit: spectacles glint
  (sparkle), one eyebrow rises. Title card: "Jeetu", THE MENTOR · HAS SEEN IT ALL. Steel tumbler clinks on the desk.
  Music: a warm cello phrase in F.

### 5. The method (0:32.0 to 1:03.2): screens floating in the night garden
**5a. Answer first (real screen).** Whoosh into the night garden. The real first-run screen from app.aspiran.in

the real answered screen: "You answered first, then looked… this one comes back in a week, just before you'd forget
it." HUD: ROEDIGER & KARPICKE · 2006 · RETRIEVAL PRACTICE.
- 0:32.37 JEETU: Answer first. Then look.

**5b. How sure?** A card: BEFORE YOU LOOK · How sure are you? [Sure] [Not sure]. Chotu wobbles between the buttons.
- 0:35.23 CHOTU: Sure… ish?
- The card falls back out of focus; the real Indian polity page slides up and its line "−⅓ for a wrong answer" is
  marked with a stamp. HUD: PRELIMS · NEGATIVE MARKING. 0:36.78 JEETU (dry): Prelims charges you for "ish".

**5c. The review.** The forgetting curve again, but now a review catches it at A DAY, A WEEK, MONTHS (saffron
