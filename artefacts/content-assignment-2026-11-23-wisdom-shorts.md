# Aspiran wisdom shorts: "One line for your answer" (overrides for this batch)

Everything in the prompts above still applies, except where this section changes it.

## A. The format: F10, "One line" · 30 to 45 s · 3 or 4 pictures

1. **0–3 s, hook.** The line itself on screen in its own script (Devanagari, Tamil or Kannada; for Pali and
   Prakrit use Devanagari with a small language label), appearing word by word. No voice yet, or a single soft note.
2. **3–12 s.** The voice reads the line once, slowly, in the original. On screen: Roman transliteration under it.
3. **12–22 s.** What it means, in one plain sentence (English video in English, Hindi video in everyday Hindi).
4. **22–30 s.** Where it comes from: the text and verse, and one sentence of context (who said it, or where we see
   it today, like a motto).
5. **30–40 s, the turn.** "Use it in GS4 when you write about…" or "A strong Essay opening for…": one theme, said
   plainly. On screen: a small card "GS4 · integrity" (or the right paper and theme).
6. **End card.** "Aspiran" and the source line. Nothing else: no "AI" labels, no "follow for more", no "part 2".

## B. The look

- **One palette for the series:** dusk pinks, soft violets, warm gold light, painted texture (like a watercolour or
  gouache book illustration). Every video should be recognisable as the same series at a glance.
- **Motion:** slow zoom and pan on each still (about 3 to 5% over the shot), a soft cross-fade between pictures.
  Nothing fast, no flashes, no shaking text. Respect reduced motion in the app version (stills with fades only).
- **Figures:** a sage, a student at a lamp, a crowd at a well, a king seen from behind, hands at work. **Never a real
  person's face**, living or dead, and never a god or saint drawn as a recognisable figure (it starts arguments in
  comments). Ashoka, Kautilya, Kabir, Basavanna, Thiruvalluvar: show their world (a pillar, a palm-leaf book, a loom,
  a temple courtyard), not their faces.
- **Text on screen:** large, centred, a serif for the line, a clean sans for the meaning. Captions burned in for the
  social cut, `.vtt` for the app.
- **Sound:** soft royalty-free instrumental (tanpura or flute bed), voice on top. Use only music you have a licence
  for, and put the licence in the ledger. In the app, sound stays off until tapped, as always.

## C. Accuracy (the most important part)

Misquoted Sanskrit, Tamil or Pali gets called out in comments within minutes.
- Copy every line from a printed or scholarly edition, not from a quotes website. Put the edition, page or verse
  number in the fact sheet.
- If an attribution is traditional rather than certain (Kabir's dohas, some vachanas), say "attributed to" in the
  video and the fact sheet.
- Give the meaning, not a loose motivational rewrite. If the line is often misread (the Gita 2.47 line is the usual
  one), don't use it.
- Even-handed: the 12 lines below come from many traditions on purpose. Don't add a line that takes a side in a
  current religious or political argument.
