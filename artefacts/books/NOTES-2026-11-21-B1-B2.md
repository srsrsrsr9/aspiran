# Workbooks B1 and B2: layout notes (23 Nov 2026)

Two workbooks from the 21 Nov content delivery are laid out as A4 PDFs in English and Hindi.

| Book | English | Hindi |
|---|---|---|
| Syllabus Companion (B1, whole syllabus, 8 parts and back matter) | `syllabus-companion/syllabus-companion-en.pdf` (99 pages) | `syllabus-companion-hi.pdf` (99 pages) |
| Polity workbook (B2, 12 chapters) | `polity-workbook/polity-workbook-en.pdf` (154 pages) | `polity-workbook-hi.pdf` (152 pages) |

## What was checked

These checks were run by script (`tools/check_workbook.py`) and by reading, not by a person:

- Every block has English and Hindi, and the lists in the two languages are the same length.
- No "AI", "UPSC Pods", "Chotu" or placeholder words. The PDF text was also searched; none were found.
- Every past question has a real year and number in the app's `content/pyq/pyq.json`, and its copied
  wording matches UPSC's. Recall answers match the official keys.
- Fill-in gaps run from 1 to n and match the answer key. Every exercise has an answer.
  Practice questions are never labelled as past questions.
- Each Polity chapter has an "as of" date and its sources.
- Syllabus lines match the syllabus the app uses.

## How to rebuild

From `artefacts/books/tools/` (needs Python with `segno` for the QR codes, and Google Chrome):

1. `python3 -I fix_2026_11_21.py <B1 workbook.json> <B2 workbook.json> ../../../content/pyq/pyq.json ..` rewrites the two `workbook.json`
   copies (only needed again if a new delivery comes in).
2. `python3 -I check_workbook.py ../polity-workbook/workbook.json ../../../content/pyq/pyq.json` (and the
   same for `../syllabus-companion/workbook.json`) should say 0 issues.
3. `python3 -I build_workbooks.py .. ../../../content/pyq/pyq.json` writes the four HTML files.
4. `zsh render.sh` prints the four PDFs. Fonts are local in `fonts/`, so no internet is needed.
