## Why it feels like less than it is

1. **No daily Chai.** The Chai is the reason to open the app every day. Without it, Today looks empty.
2. **Practice runs out fast.** 37 formats sound like a lot, but with 2 to 4 items each, a keen aspirant finishes them in a sitting.
3. **No Prelims past papers.** Aspirants expect every Prelims paper from 2011 onwards, sorted by topic.
4. **Long reads sat in one flat list.** Most already had a subject, but the screen didn't use it. Now the reads sit under Polity, Economy and the rest (correction: an earlier version of this audit said they had no subject; that was wrong).
