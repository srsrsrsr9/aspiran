Teaching plan

The final position after your comments and the research (full text: artefacts/pedagogy-response-2026-11-01.md), then how much of it is built.

The plan in short

TopicDecision
A big plan that feels smallToday shows one step at a time. The full plan is one tap away, never on the front screen.
Pause linesOur own lines, placed where the idea is used. No made-up quotes from real people.
Which tactic whereRetrieval and spacing everywhere. Mixing topics only for maths, CSAT, maps and "which of these" Prelims items, never essays.
Review scheduleFSRS, a tested open scheduler, replaces our fixed day rules.
AI-made contentIt's the source, like a textbook, never the last step. Every piece makes the aspirant produce something (answer from memory, a one-pager, their own card).
Your roomPrivate sleep log, notes, journal, to-dos and export. Never scored. Sleep gets the most care.
LanguagesHindi first for practice and evaluation. Telugu waits.
Away from the screenAudio walks, weekly printables, paper answers. No "wait for Wi-Fi"; a 12 MB day pack is the target.
Missed daysPlans bend: set steps after 1 missed day, 1 week and 1 month. Welcome-back scripts, no guilt.
WritingMark yourself, then AI, then rewrite. Paper by default, stylus allowed. Essays in three snaps: skeleton, outline, essay.
AI markingCalled "a second opinion next to your own marks" until 50 answers are compared with two experienced evaluators. Hindi marking waits for the same test.
ProgressOnly "you vs. you" and "ready by exam date". No cut-offs, no "toppers had X by now".
MistakesThe Sunday Fix turns each logged mistake into one change. A log alone isn't built.
Keep for MainsOne quiet nudge, only on cards an editor marked high value.
InterviewChotu plays the board members; the pod is the human board.
Sound and motionRelaxed. Tragic-tone rules and Chotu's quiet hours (23:30–07:00) stay.
TrackingTrack what helps learning, with one plain notice and four opt-ins (DPDP Act 2023, Play data safety).
