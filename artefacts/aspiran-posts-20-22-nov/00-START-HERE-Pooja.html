Before you post (10 minutes)

  1. Open https://app.aspiran.in/app/#try/path-pol-constitution on your phone. You should see the Constitution topic. That's where the workbook's QR codes go.
  2. Watch the end of videos 1, 2 and 3 in both languages. If an end card or a corner says "AI voice" or "made with AI", don't post that video; tell the owner. (Since 17 Nov we don't put AI labels anywhere.)
  3. If YouTube asks "Altered or synthetic content?", answer No: these are illustrations, not realistic footage of real people or events.

How to post each kind

  • Carousel: New post, pick frames 01 to the last in order, paste the caption.
  • Story: one image; add the Link sticker with the link in the day file.
  • Telegram quiz: Attach, Poll, switch on "Quiz mode", tick the ✔ option. On WhatsApp, post the question as plain text. You can schedule all three days in one sitting (long-press Send, Schedule).
  • Workbook DMs: when someone writes "WORKBOOK" on Instagram, send them the PDF with the reply line in the Day 2 file.
  • Links: each app has its own link (already in the text). Don't swap them; that's how we see which app brings people who actually finish a topic.
Don't post anything about ranks, toppers, selection chances, deaths or disasters; don't forward other people's PDFs or notes; and don't say a person checked something unless one did. If someone in comments sounds in distress, don't reply publicly: tell the owner the same day.
