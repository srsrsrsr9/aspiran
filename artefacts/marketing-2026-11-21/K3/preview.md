## How it works

**EN**

1. **Study the way you already do**  
   Keep your coaching, your YouTube teacher or your books. Add Aspiran's syllabus notes with audio, Daily Chai cards and topic paths where you need them.

2. **Recall it, just before it fades**  
   This is spaced recall. Short questions and flashcards come back after a gap, and the gap grows each time you get them right. Pulling an answer from memory makes it last longer than reading it again.

3. **Test it the way UPSC will**  
   Practise with questions, mocks and past papers with UPSC's keys. Write Mains answers, see them marked in the app, and rewrite. You see what has stuck and what needs another pass.

1. **जैसे पढ़ते हैं, वैसे ही पढ़िए**

2. **भूलने से पहले, याद करके दोहराइए**  
   यही अंतराल पर दोहराव (spaced recall) है। छोटे सवाल और फ़्लैशकार्ड कुछ अंतर के बाद लौटते हैं, और हर सही उत्तर के साथ यह अंतर बढ़ता जाता है। याद से उत्तर निकालना, दोबारा पढ़ने से ज़्यादा देर टिकता है।

## FAQs

**EN**

**What is Aspiran?**  
Aspiran is a study app for the UPSC Civil Services exam, in English and Hindi. It is the layer that makes your prep stick: it brings back what you study as short recall questions, gives you practice and past papers, and marks your Mains answers in the app.

**Does Aspiran replace my coaching?**  
No. Keep your coaching, your YouTube teacher or your books. Aspiran works beside them. You learn there; Aspiran helps you remember it, practise it and write it.

**Is Aspiran good for Hindi medium?**  
Yes. Aspiran is built in Hindi and English side by side. The Hindi is written for a Hindi-medium reader, using the terms UPSC uses in its own Hindi papers, not translated line by line.

**Is it free? What does Plus cost?**  
Joining is free, and no card is asked. Aspiran Plus is ₹3,999 a year, the same for everyone: one payment, no auto-renewal, refund within 7 days.

**What can I try in the free demo?**  
The free demo lets you try a sample of the app, including recall questions, before you decide on Plus. You can see how Aspiran fits beside your own prep. Try it at aspiran.in.

**How are my Mains answers marked?**  
You write your answer in Aspiran and it is marked in the app. Then you can rewrite the same answer and compare the two. Writing, seeing the marks and rewriting is how answers get better.

**Where do your facts come from?**  
From primary sources: the Constitution, Acts of Parliament, Supreme Court judgments, PIB, ministry pages, the Economic Survey and NCERT. Facts that change, like the Supreme Court's strength or the repo rate, are watched every day and updated when they change.

**What are pods and pod friends?**  
A pod is a small study group inside Aspiran, so you are not revising alone. Pod friends are study helpers built into the app. They are not other members, and they are not real people.

**Why does Aspiran keep asking me things I already studied?**  
That is the point. Recalling an answer from memory, after a gap, makes it last far longer than reading it again. This is spaced recall. A question that feels slightly hard to answer is doing its job.

**Will I be ranked against other aspirants?**  
No. Aspiran has no leaderboards, public scores or streaks. You see your own progress: what has stuck and what needs another pass. Your prep is not a race inside the app.

आप Aspiran में उत्तर लिखते हैं और उसकी जाँच ऐप में ही होती है। फिर आप वही उत्तर दोबारा लिखकर दोनों की तुलना कर सकते हैं। लिखना, अंक देखना और फिर लिखना, उत्तर ऐसे ही सुधरते हैं।

**आपके तथ्य कहाँ से आते हैं?**

**पॉड और पॉड फ़्रेंड क्या हैं?**  
पॉड Aspiran के अंदर एक छोटा अध्ययन समूह है, ताकि आप अकेले न दोहराएँ। पॉड फ़्रेंड ऐप में बने अध्ययन सहायक हैं। वे दूसरे सदस्य नहीं हैं, और न ही असली लोग।

**Aspiran बार-बार वही क्यों पूछता है जो मैं पढ़ चुका हूँ?**  
यही तो मक़सद है। कुछ अंतर के बाद याद से उत्तर निकालना, दोबारा पढ़ने से कहीं ज़्यादा टिकता है। इसे अंतराल पर दोहराव (spaced recall) कहते हैं। जो सवाल थोड़ा कठिन लगे, वह अपना काम कर रहा है।

**क्या मुझे दूसरे अभ्यर्थियों के मुक़ाबले रैंक मिलेगी?**  
नहीं। Aspiran में कोई लीडरबोर्ड, सार्वजनिक स्कोर या स्ट्रीक नहीं है। आप सिर्फ़ अपनी प्रगति देखते हैं: क्या टिक गया और किसे फिर दोहराना है। ऐप के अंदर आपकी तैयारी कोई दौड़ नहीं है।
