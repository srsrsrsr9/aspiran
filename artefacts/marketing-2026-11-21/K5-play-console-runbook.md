**You publish this; Claude doesn't sign in to Play Console.** About 15 minutes, in the browser on your Mac.

## Step 1. Open the listing
Go to https://play.google.com/console → **Aspiran** → left menu **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**.
- Worked: you see App name, Short description, Full description.
- If you don't see Aspiran: you're signed in to the wrong Google account. Switch account (top right).

## Step 2. Short description (English)
Select all in **Short description**, delete it, paste:

```text
UPSC prep that sticks: spaced recall in English and Hindi, beside your coaching
```
- Worked: the counter shows 79/80.

## Step 3. Full description (English)
Select all in **Full description**, delete it, paste:

```text
Aspiran is a study app for the UPSC Civil Services exam, in English and Hindi. It is the layer that makes any prep stick. Keep your coaching, your YouTube teacher or your books. Aspiran helps you remember what you learn there.

WHY IT WORKS
Reading a page again feels like revision, but most of it fades. Recalling an answer from memory, after a gap, makes it last. This is spaced recall. Aspiran brings back what you studied as short questions and flashcards, spaced out over days and weeks.

WHAT YOU GET
• Daily Chai: 3 current-affairs cards a day
• Syllabus notes with audio
• Flashcards and spaced recall questions
• Practice questions and mocks
• Past papers with UPSC's keys
• Mains answer writing: your answer is marked in the app, and you can rewrite it
• Guided walks and topic paths through the syllabus
• Interactives and illustrated shorts
• Pods: small study groups, with pod friends as study helpers
• Chotu, your study buddy
• Focus blocks for steady study time

FOR HINDI MEDIUM
Notes, questions and cards are written in Hindi for Hindi-medium readers, in UPSC's own terms. Not a line-by-line translation.

WHERE THE FACTS COME FROM
Primary sources: the Constitution, Acts of Parliament, Supreme Court judgments, PIB, ministry pages, the Economic Survey and NCERT.

WHAT ASPIRAN DOESN'T DO
• No promise of selection
• No ranks, leaderboards or public scores
• It doesn't replace your teacher

PRICE
Free to join, no card. Aspiran Plus is ₹3,999 a year.

Start free, then decide. More at aspiran.in

Aspiran is an independent app. It is not linked to UPSC or any government body.

Privacy policy: https://aspiran.in/privacy
```
- Worked: the counter shows about 1,639/4,000 and no red text.
- If Play warns about a word: tell Claude which one; don't reword it yourself in a hurry.

## Step 4. Leave the app name
Keep **App name** as it is (`Aspiran: UPSC prep, together`, 29 characters). Click **Save** (bottom right).

• छोटू, आपका पढ़ाई का साथी
• एकाग्र पढ़ाई के लिए फ़ोकस ब्लॉक

हिंदी माध्यम के लिए
नोट्स, प्रश्न और कार्ड हिंदी माध्यम के पाठक के लिए हिंदी में लिखे गए हैं, UPSC की अपनी शब्दावली में। शब्दशः अनुवाद नहीं।

तथ्य कहाँ से आते हैं
प्राथमिक स्रोतों से: संविधान, संसद के अधिनियम, सर्वोच्च न्यायालय के निर्णय, PIB, मंत्रालयों के पेज, आर्थिक समीक्षा और NCERT।

Aspiran क्या नहीं करता
• चयन का कोई वादा नहीं
• कोई रैंक, लीडरबोर्ड या सार्वजनिक स्कोर नहीं
• यह आपके शिक्षक की जगह नहीं लेता

निजता नीति: https://aspiran.in/privacy
