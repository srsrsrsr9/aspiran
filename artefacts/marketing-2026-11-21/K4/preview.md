## Day 7 (English) · tone NEUTRAL

**Subject:** Why you forget, and the simple fix _(34/50)_  
**Preheader:** Spaced recall in plain words: bring it back just before it slips. _(65/90)_

```
Hi {{first_name}},   [fallback: Hi there,]

Most of what we read is gone within a few days. Not because we're careless. That's simply how memory works.

The fix is called spaced recall. It has two parts.

First, recall, don't reread. Close the book and try to answer. The effort of pulling it out is what makes it stay.

Second, space it out. Try again after a day, then a few days, then a week or more. Each time it gets harder to forget, so the gaps can grow.

Doing this by hand for a whole syllabus is a lot of bookkeeping. Aspiran keeps track for you and brings each question back just before you'd lose it.
```
