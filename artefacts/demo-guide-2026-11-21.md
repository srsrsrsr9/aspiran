# Aspiran: demo guide (21 Nov 2026)

Three ready demos: an **aspirant** (5 minutes), a **coaching centre** (12 minutes) and an **investor or partner**
(15 minutes). Each is a click path with what to say. Logins are the ones from Part 0 of
`artefacts/test-runbook-2026-11-21.md`; this file names them but holds no passwords or keys.

## Before any demo (10 minutes, the day before)

| Check | How |
|---|---|
| A **demo account** that looks lived-in | Use test user 1 (`aspiran_test1`): do Today's 3, one Prelims set, one short mock and one Mains answer the day before, so Progress and Review have something in them. |
| A **Demo staff key** (role Editor) | Admin → Team → Staff and roles. Never type the owner key in front of anyone. |
| A test coaching centre | "DEMO Academy" in Admin → Coaching centres, with one batch, one announcement, one study plan, one question set and one answered doubt (test user 2 as the student). Resume it if paused. |
| Tabs open in Chrome | 1 https://aspiran.in · 2 https://app.aspiran.in/app/#try/path-pol-federalism (private window) · 3 https://app.aspiran.in/app/ signed in as test user 1 · 4 https://app.aspiran.in/institute · 5 https://admin.aspiran.in |
| Phone | Android app signed in as test user 1; Do Not Disturb on; brightness up. |
| Time of day | Between 07:00 and 23:30 IST, or Chotu and Jeetu stay quiet (by design). |
| Sound | Sound is off until pressed. Unmute the laptop before you press play on a video. |
| Backup | If Wi-Fi fails: the installed web app opens offline from its last copy; the phone app has today's content. |

**Words to use and avoid**
- Say "Chotu", "Jeetu", "study helpers", "the app marks your answer". **Never say "AI"** and never say a person checked
  or wrote something the system did.
- Don't say content is "checked by a human editor".
- Price is **₹3,999 a year**. There's no founding offer and no monthly plan.
- Android Plus isn't on sale yet (web and UPI are). Coaching centres: a free 30-day pilot.

## Demo 1. An aspirant (5 minutes)

Goal: they try one topic and join free.

| Time | Click | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Tab 1, aspiran.in, scroll to the marked answer | "Your coaching teaches it. Aspiran makes it stay. Here's a real Mains answer marked on five parts." |
| 0:45 | **Try one topic free** | "No account. One whole Polity topic: Federalism." |
| 1:00 | Cover → Express → the first guess step | "You guess first. Getting it wrong first makes it stick. Each step says why we do it." |
| 2:00 | A past-paper question → "How sure are you?" | "Real UPSC questions. We ask how sure you are, because guessing has a cost: minus a third." |
| 3:00 | Skip to **Keep it** | "These are your cards. We'll bring 5 back on Monday by email. One email, then the address is deleted." |
| 4:00 | Finish → **Join free, keep your cards** | "Join free and the cards come with you. Pods, daily Chai and practice are free; Plus is ₹3,999 a year." |

If they're Hindi-medium, start from https://aspiran.in/hi/ instead; the whole demo runs in Hindi.

## Demo 2. A coaching centre (12 minutes)

Goal: they agree to the free 30-day pilot.

| Time | Click | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Tab 1 → **For coaching centres** | "You teach; Aspiran keeps it in their heads between classes, under your name." |
| 1:00 | Tab 4, the console, DEMO Academy → Branding | "Your name, colour and logo. Students see your centre, not us." |
| 2:00 | Batches and invites | "A code per batch, a link and a ready WhatsApp message. Students join in a minute." |
| 3:00 | Chat and announcements, then Doubts | "Announcements and batch chat. Doubts with a photo; your teachers answer here." |
| 5:00 | Study plans, Notes and tests | "Your own plans and question sets. Answers are marked for you; students never see answers first." |
| 7:00 | Weekly report | "Each batch's week: who's active, study minutes, weakest topics, who has gone quiet. No rankings, and we never show you a student's private practice, journal or chats." |
| 9:00 | Phone, Android app → Prep → Practice & Mocks | "On their side, the whole Aspiran library too: Chai, practice, mocks, videos, recorded notes." |
| 10:30 | Back to For coaching centres → the form | "Free 30-day pilot. Leave your details and we'll set it up." |

Questions they ask:
- *Under-18 students?* Not yet; the pilot is 18+.
- *Price after the pilot?* Say you'll send it with the pilot plan (not decided yet; it's one of the 11 decisions in
  `artefacts/coaching-centres-plan-2026-11-14.md`).
- *Can students' answer copies be checked here?* Not yet.

## Demo 3. An investor or partner (15 minutes)

Goal: they see the whole journey, the rules behind it and the numbers.

| Time | Click | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Tab 1, aspiran.in | "UPSC aspirants: a million-plus a year, mostly studying alone, mostly in Hindi and English. Aspiran is community first: small pods, daily current affairs, practice that sticks." |
| 1:30 | Tab 2, the open demo, two steps | "The open demo converts strangers. It's built on retrieval and spacing, and every step cites why." |
| 3:00 | Tab 3, signed-in web app → **Adda** → **Pod** | "Small pods of aspirants. The study helpers have a cup, not a face, and a tag, so nobody mistakes them for people." |
| 4:30 | **Chotu** → ask "Explain cooperative federalism in two lines" → **Listen** | "A study buddy in English, Hindi and Telugu, with a voice. Quiet at night; never talks about ranks or feelings, and points people to someone they trust." |
| 6:00 | **Prep → Chai** | "Three current-affairs cards a day, drafted each morning, checked, published by 7 am." |
| 7:00 | **Practice → Mains** → the answer written yesterday | "Answers marked on five parts, in Hindi too. It names the weakest part." |
| 8:00 | **Mocks** → yesterday's result | "Real negative marking; we show a range, not a rank, and whether their guessing pays." |
| 9:00 | **Practice → Videos** and **Listen** | "Illustrated shorts and over 25 hours of recorded notes." |
| 10:00 | **Learn → syllabus garden** | "The whole syllabus as a creeper: 297 leaves, one per UPSC line, lighting up as they learn." |
| 11:00 | Phone: same app on Android | "Same thing on Android; it's in testing on Play now." |
| 12:00 | Tab 5, Admin → Home, then Growth → Insights | "Website to demo to sign-up to paid, return rates, what gets finished. No personal data on these screens." |
| 13:30 | Back to the site's Plus section | "₹3,999 a year. Coaching centres as the second channel." |

Rules worth saying out loud: no streaks, no countdown timers by default, no public scores, sound off and nothing
autoplays, every piece ends, distress messages are held and get care, not posted.

## If something goes wrong mid-demo

| Problem | Do |
|---|---|
| "Loading…" stays on the demo | Reload once. Still stuck: switch to the signed-in tab 3 and show Prep → Learn. |
| Chotu doesn't answer | Check the time (quiet 23:30–07:00). Otherwise show Practice instead and tell Claude afterwards. |
| Video has no sound | Sound starts off by design; press the speaker icon and unmute the laptop. |
| Wrong language | Me → Language (web) or the हिन्दी switch on the site. |
| Signed out | Sign in with the test user (password manager). Never type the owner key on screen. |
