# Exam-success central ideas: real-world examples by country

*Compiled 1 November 2026, from the Part 1 catalogue (`Exam success tools and methods.md`) and the Part 2 report (`Exam success central ideas.md`). All examples are drawn from the Part 1 catalogue, so nothing is invented; links are included only where the official site is well established.*

---

## Original request

> For each central idea below (they are ideas that study tools use to help people pass exams), give up to three real examples of how it is put into practice in different countries or exam cultures (for example the US, UK, India, China, South Korea, Japan, Germany, Brazil, Nigeria). Each example: name, country, type (app, book, method, school, place), link if any, and one line on how it uses the idea. Prefer examples that are well known in their own country but little known elsewhere. Don't invent anything; leave a row empty rather than guess.

## The 26 central ideas (from Part 2)

| Code | Idea | One-line definition |
|---|---|---|
| RET | Retrieval practice | Pull the answer out of your own memory (quiz, flashcard, blank page) instead of looking at it. |
| SPA | Spacing and scheduled review | Come back to the same material several times, with gaps that grow, instead of all at once. |
| MNE | Memory techniques | Turn hard-to-hold facts into vivid images, places, stories or codes that are easier to recall. |
| TCH | Expert teaching and curated content | Learn from a skilled teacher or a trusted, well-chosen source. |
| TUT | Tutoring and mentoring | One-to-one guidance that asks questions and adjusts to you, from a person or an AI. |
| ELA | Understanding and elaboration | Make sense of material by explaining, asking why, linking to what you know and picturing it. |
| EXM | Worked examples and model answers | Study a finished solution or a top performer's answer, then imitate it. |
| NOT | Note-making and organising knowledge | Put what you learn into your own structured notes, maps or linked files. |
| MIX | Interleaving and varied practice | Mix different topics or problem types in one session so you must choose the method each time. |
| ADP | Working on weak spots | Find what you cannot do yet and practise exactly that until it is mastered, by machine or by design. |
| FBK | Feedback and error correction | Find out what was wrong and why, then fix it, including keeping a record of mistakes. |
| TST | Exam rehearsal and technique | Practise under real exam conditions and learn how to handle the paper itself. |
| TGT | Exam targeting | Decide what to study from the syllabus, past-paper patterns and what the setters use. |
| PLN | Planning and self-monitoring | Plan when to study, track what you actually did and adjust. |
| LHL | Learning how to learn | Learn which study methods exist and how memory and thinking work, then apply that to yourself. |
| FOC | Attention and distraction control | Protect stretches of focused time from phones, sites and noise. |
| HAB | Motivation, habits and mindset | Keep going through rewards, routines and beliefs about effort and ability. |
| SPC | Study spaces and co-presence | Study in a place set aside for it, or alongside other people who are also studying. |
| GRP | Study groups and teaching others | Learn by discussing with peers, teaching them, or sharing material in a community. |
| CMP | Public comparison and rankings | See how your effort or scores stack up against others. |
| LIF | Body basics | Sleep, exercise, food, water and caffeine set the physical conditions for study and exams. |
| ANX | Exam nerves and mental wellbeing | Manage stress and anxiety, and get help when it becomes too much. |
| GEN | AI-made study material | An AI turns your files or a topic into cards, quizzes, notes or summaries for you. |
| OFF | Answer lookup and offloading | A tool or person supplies the answer or does the work, plus the guardrails built against that. |
| QFX | Quick fixes and popular beliefs | Products or beliefs that promise an easy boost or a fixed 'type' of learner. |
| BAS | Default habits | What many students do without being taught: reread, highlight, cram, multitask. |

## The mapping

## Remembering

**RET — Retrieval practice**
- **Memorisation pen & red sheet (暗記ペン / 赤シート)** — Japan, stationery method, no single link — key terms written in orange ink vanish under a red sheet, forcing recall before checking.
- **Blank-page review (백지복습)** — Korea, method — close the book, write everything you know on blank paper, then check gaps against notes.
- **5·3 (五年高考·三年模拟)** — China, book — five years of real gaokao and three of mock questions, sorted by topic, for volume self-testing.

**SPA — Spacing and scheduled review**
- **SuperMemo** — Poland, app, supermemo.com — home of the SM-2 algorithm family that nearly every spaced-repetition app later copied.
- **1-3-7-21 revision rule** — India, method — re-revise on days 1, 3, 7 and 21 after first learning; a fixed timetable standing in for an algorithm.
- **MaiMemo (墨墨背单词)** — China, app — vocabulary scheduled by its own SSP-MMC optimal-control algorithm, little known outside China.

**MNE — Memory techniques**
- **Baicizhan (百词斩)** — China, app — pairs every vocabulary word with a picture; one of China's most downloaded English apps.
- **Dominic O'Brien, *How to Develop a Brilliant Memory Week by Week*** — UK, book — weekly exercises in the journey method and the Dominic number system.
- **Norman Lewis, *Word Power Made Easy*** — US book, cult classic in India — vocabulary built from Latin/Greek roots with built-in self-tests.

## Understanding

**TCH — Expert teaching and curated content**
- **Megastudy** — Korea, website — star-teacher online lectures built around the Suneung.
- **Toshin High School (東進ハイスクール)** — Japan, place/course — star-teacher video lectures broadcast to franchise classrooms nationwide.
- **PhysicsWallah** — India, app — low-fee classes fed by mass free YouTube lectures; arguably India's best-known coaching brand.

**TUT — Tutoring and mentoring**
- **Khanmigo** — US, app, khanacademy.org — Socratic AI tutor that prompts reasoning and refuses to hand over answers.
- **Yuanfudao (猿辅导)** — China, app — one-to-one and small-group tutoring for K-12 and gaokao preparation.
- **Gongsin (공신닷컴)** — Korea, website — top-university students mentor aspirants and share their own study methods.

**ELA — Understanding and elaboration**
- **Feynman technique** — US, method — explain a concept simply to an imagined beginner to expose gaps.
- ***Concepts of Physics*, H. C. Verma** — India, book — theory tightly interleaved with worked problems; a rite of passage for Indian engineering aspirants.
- **GeoGebra** — Austria, website, geogebra.org — manipulable graphs and geometry that let you see a concept move.

**EXM — Worked examples and model answers**
- ***The Art of Mathematics* (수학의 정석)** — Korea, book — near-exhaustive catalogue of problem types, each with a worked method.
- **Chart-shiki (チャート式)** — Japan, book — colour-coded difficulty levels map every problem type to its solution method.
- **theiashub topper copies** — India, website — library of successful UPSC candidates' handwritten Mains answers to imitate.

**NOT — Note-making and organising knowledge**
- **Cornell note-taking system** — US, method — cue column and summary strip; cover the notes and answer your own cues.
- **Zettelkasten (as popularised by Sönke Ahrens, *How to Take Smart Notes*)** — Germany, method/book — atomic notes in your own words, linked into a web.
- **Clearnote** — Japan, app — students photograph and share handwritten class notes and answer each other's questions.

## Practising and feedback

**MIX — Interleaving and varied practice**
- **Seneca Learning** — UK, website — short revision lessons that mix topics within a subject (coded secondary; almost nothing is built primarily on interleaving).
- **Chart-shiki (チャート式)** — Japan, book — mixed problem sets within each chart force you to choose the method (secondary).
- *(Third row left empty: the catalogue records no product in any country built mainly on interleaving.)*

**ADP — Working on weak spots**
- **Squirrel AI (松鼠AI)** — China, programme — diagnoses gaps across fine-grained "knowledge points" and teaches each to mastery.
- **Embibe** — India, app — a knowledge graph selects the next practice question based on what you can't yet do.
- **ALEKS** — US, website, aleks.com — maps your knowledge state and only teaches topics you are "ready to learn", then re-checks retention.

**FBK — Feedback and error correction**
- **Mistake notebook (오답노트) culture** — Korea, method — every wrong answer is logged with its cause and re-tested before the next mock.
- **Zuoyebang Miaomiaoji (喵喵机)** — China, product/app — a small printer that photographs wrong questions and prints them into a physical 错题本, then pushes similar variants.
- **SuperKalam** — India, app — grades handwritten UPSC Mains answers in about a minute against rubrics.

**TST — Exam rehearsal and technique**
- **Akahon (赤本)** — Japan, book — one red book per university of its past entrance exams, with answers; the core of Japanese entrance-exam prep.
- **National Test Abhyas** — India, app — official NTA practice tests on the real exam pattern and interface.
- **Bluebook (College Board)** — US, website — full-length adaptive digital SAT mocks on the actual test software.

## Steering your own study

**TGT — Exam targeting**
- **EBS Suneung Special Lectures (수능특강)** — Korea, book — official workbooks whose passages feed Suneung questions; studying them *is* targeting the setters.
- **AnKing deck** — US, course/Anki deck — community deck explicitly mapped to the US medical exam syllabus.
- ***Mission IAS* (Disha)** — India, book — exam-trend analysis plus topper interviews for UPSC.
- *(Notably small everywhere: the catalogue has only 3 primary entries for this idea.)*

**PLN — Planning and self-monitoring**
- **Hengshui model (衡水模式)** — China, programme — minute-by-minute boarding-school timetables; planning as total institution.
- **Barbri** — US, course — day-by-day study plan with graded practice essays for the bar exam.
- **Studyplus for School** — Japan, website — students log study time; teachers monitor the logs and message students.

**LHL — Learning how to learn**
- **Learning How to Learn (Oakley & Sejnowski)** — US, Coursera course, coursera.org — chunking, diffuse mode, recall and spacing; one of the most-enrolled MOOCs ever.
- ***The Study Skills Handbook*, Stella Cottrell** — UK, book — self-assessment plus routines for reading, notes, time and exams; a standard UK university text.
- **iCanStudy (Dr Justin Sung)** — Australia, course/creator — priming and mind-mapping for first-pass encoding; big in studytub circles, little known elsewhere.

**FOC — Attention and distraction control**
- **Pomodoro technique** — Italy, method (Francesco Cirillo) — 25-minute blocks with 5-minute breaks; born in Italy, now global.
- **Tomato ToDo (番茄ToDo)** — China, app — Pomodoro timer that literally locks you out of other apps, plus online study rooms.
- **Forest** — Taiwan, app, forestapp.cc — a virtual tree dies if you leave the app during a focus session.

**HAB — Motivation, habits and mindset**
- **Duolingo** — US, app, duolingo.com — streaks, points and leagues keep daily practice going.
- **Kahoot!** — Norway, app, kahoot.com — live quiz games with speed points and public leaderboards.
- **Pariksha Pe Charcha / Exam Warriors** — India, programme — the PM's annual town hall reframing exams and urging parents not to compare children.

## Other people and places

**SPC — Study spaces and co-presence**
- **Paid self-study rooms (付费自习室)** — China, place — rented quiet desks, often open 24 hours; a booming paid industry.
- **Noryangjin gosichon (노량진 고시촌)** — Korea, place — a district of cram schools, tiny rooms and cheap meals for full exam immersion.
- **Kota** — India, place — the JEE/NEET coaching town where Allen and Allen-style institutes, hostels and peer pressure concentrate.

**GRP — Study groups and teaching others**
- **CivilsDaily mentor-led Telegram groups** — India, programme/community — paid mentor groups sharing notes, daily targets and discussion.
- **Jigsaw classroom** — US, method (Elliot Aronson) — each member masters one part, then teaches it to the group.
- *(Third row left empty: the catalogue's other GRP entries are the US/UK research methods — peer instruction, reciprocal teaching, teach-back — and Indian Reddit/Telegram forums.)*

**CMP — Public comparison and rankings**
- **YPT – Yeolpumta (열품타)** — Korea, app — logs study hours and publicly ranks your group by daily hours studied.
- **Studyplus** — Japan, app — friends see each other's logged study time and send "cheers".
- **Fenbi (粉笔)** — China, app — national online mock exams rank you against every other test-taker.

## Body and mind

**LIF — Body basics**
- ***Why We Sleep*, Matthew Walker** — UK, book — the popular case for sleep before and after learning as consolidation.
- **Sleep Cycle** — Sweden, app, sleepcycle.com — smart alarms and sleep tracking; Sweden-based.
- **Sleep-before-and-after-study rule / nap practice** — international, method — the research-derived habit itself, taught explicitly in coaching cultures such as India's NEET/JEE circles.

**ANX — Exam nerves and mental wellbeing**
- **Tele-MANAS** — India, government programme — 24×7 multilingual tele-counselling for students, launched around exam stress.
- **Kooth** — UK, website, kooth.com — anonymous online counselling and moderated peer community for young people.
- **Smiling Mind** — Australia, app, smilingmind.com.au — free mindfulness app with school programmes.

## Shortcuts, handovers and defaults

**GEN — AI-made study material**
- **NotebookLM** — US, app, notebooklm.google.com — quizzes, answers and audio overviews built only from your uploaded sources.
- **Vaia** — Germany, app (founded in Munich as StudySmarter) — AI turns uploads into cards, then schedules them.
- **Revprep** — India, app — AI-made cards and quizzes aimed specifically at Indian exams.

**OFF — Answer lookup and offloading**
- **QANDA (콴다, Mathpresso)** — Korea, app — photograph a problem, get step-by-step solutions plus an AI tutor.
- **Zuoyebang (作业帮)** — China, app — photo homework answers plus AI tutoring; one of China's largest education apps.
- **Photomath** — Croatia (founded), now Google, app, photomath.com — camera-based step-by-step maths solver.

**QFX — Quick fixes and popular beliefs**
- **Brahmi / Shankhpushpi memory syrups** — India, product — herbal "memory boosters" marketed to exam students; a household staple of Indian exam culture.
- **"Smart Kids Brain Boost"** — South Africa, product — children's supplement whose claims were ordered withdrawn by regulators.
- **Kwik Learning (Jim Kwik)** — US, course — paid speed-reading and memory-training courses marketed to students.

**BAS — Default habits**
- **Speechify** — US, app — text-to-speech "revision by listening" is essentially rereading with your ears.
- **Readwise** — US, app — resurfaces your highlights daily: a scheduled habit of passive re-reading.
- *(Third row left empty: cramming, highlighting and multitasking are habits, not products, and the catalogue records no third tool built around them.)*

## Caveats

- Links are included only where the official domain is well established; for most locally famous products (Akahon, Miaomiaoji, Yeolpumta, topper copies) the link is blank rather than risk a wrong URL.
- For MIX, GRP and BAS the pickings are genuinely thin in the catalogue itself — that pattern (interleaving has almost no dedicated products anywhere) is itself a finding worth carrying into Part 3.
- Examples come from the Part 1 catalogue and inherit its limitations (single-coder coding, India- and English-source over-representation).
