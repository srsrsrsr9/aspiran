## 1. Ideas that don't fit v0

- **Learning-science literacy** (learning how learning works, as background rather than a method): Baddeley, Genova, Ranganath, Joordens course, MIT OCW psychology, APA Top 20, InnerDrive, Zakrajsek (8). Currently forced into C. Propose a code (say L), or fold into P as "knowing which strategies work".
- **Reasoning and problem-solving skills** (general thinking tools, not memory): Science of Everyday Thinking, 5 Elements, Academic Skills, Mindware, Mathematical Thinking, Pólya (6). Propose a code; Pólya also has a metacognitive "look back" step.
- **Pretesting / generation** (producing an answer before or instead of reading it): 308, 309; also a component of 341 answer writing and PEEL. Either split from R ("generation before study") or state in R's definition that pretests count.
- **Deliberate practice** (targeted practice on weaknesses with feedback): 364; overlaps question-sea drill. Could be F+R bundle rather than a code, but the "target weaknesses" element is not in F.
- **Answer-writing frameworks / production practice**: PEEL, 341 daily answer writing, UPSC AI and Vaidra evaluators. v0 has no code for practising the output format (essays, long answers). Propose "writing and answer construction".

- **Code tweaks**: O mixes "answer lookup" with "cognitive offloading"; GTD, Google Keep and Levitin offload task memory, which is healthy, so split O into "answer lookup" and leave offloading of tasks under P/N.
