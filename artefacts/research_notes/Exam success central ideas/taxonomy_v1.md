# Central ideas v1: codes for mapping (Part 2)

Final list for the exam-success research project, settled 1 November 2026. Replaces `taxonomy_v0.md`.
26 ideas in 7 families. Codes are stable: Parts 3 and 4 should use them.

**Primary rule.** The primary is the idea a user mainly does or gets when using the entry, judged from its Part 1 description. Tie-breaks: (1) a book, course or creator that teaches several study strategies is LHL, with the strategies as secondaries; one that centres on one strategy takes that strategy. (2) A product that bundles features takes the feature the catalogue leads with. (3) A flashcard tool is SPA if the scheduler is the point, GEN if AI making the cards is, NOT if notes come first. Up to three secondaries. Lenses (trust and claims; access, price and language) are not codes.
