Conventions: research papers, reviews and free PDFs are typed "book" (a publication). Coaching institutes are tagged by what they mostly sell. ## 1. Ideas that don't fit v0

- **[Q] Prioritising what to study** (syllabus mapping, PYQ/trend analysis, "few sources", foundation-first). Not really P (P is about *when/how* and self-monitoring; this is *what*). Entries: 178 Testbook, 187 SuperKalam, 213 Indian Express, 216 NCERT, 217 Laxmikanth, 220 Shankar, 228 Mission IAS (primary); also implicit in the section intro (topper pattern: "PYQ analysis to decide what to study"). Recommend a new code.
- **[Y] Modelling experts / toppers** (imitating model answers, topper strategy, worked examples at the strategy level). Partly U (worked examples) and partly G (mentors), but distinct: learning by copying a successful person's outputs or process. Entries: 195 theiashub (primary), 224, 225, 227 (primary), 193, 199, 201, 209, 223, 226, 228, 233, 274. Recommend a new code, or a sub-code under U ("worked examples") with a note for strategy imitation.
- **[V] Vetting claims, trust and regulation** (myth-busting, coaching red flags, scam warnings, CCPA/FTC/ASCI action). Entries: 211 Bharatnotes, 268 Urban Myths (primary); 236, 240, 246, 251 (secondary). Also the regulatory notes on 162, 165, 166, 196–206 are this idea in the catalogue's own commentary. Recommend a new code; it is the mirror of K.
- **[L] Access and reach** (low price, free tier, vernacular/Hindi, accessibility/UDL, YouTube funnel). Not a learning mechanism but a recurring selling idea: 241 CAST UDL (primary); 170, 173, 179, 180, 197, 232. Suggest a non-learning "delivery/business" tag rather than a central idea.
- **Products and drugs** (166–169) have no Type that fits; used "? (product/drug)". Add type "product".
- **Counselling and crisis support** (149, 151, 152, 158, 159) all sit in W but are a different thing from lifestyle tips (144–147). Suggest splitting W into **W1 lifestyle/physiology** (sleep, exercise, food) and **W2 anxiety and mental-health support** (CBT, counselling, helplines, expressive writing).
- **B is stretched**: 137 reappraisal and 138 self-affirmation are brief pre-exam interventions, closer to W2; 150 Pariksha Pe Charcha is "reframing exams + don't compare". Consider "stress reappraisal" under W2 and keeping B for long-run beliefs (mindset, grit).
- **Deliberate practice** (262, 272, 270, 274) was coded F; it is really practice at the edge of skill + feedback. Could be a named sub-idea of F, or merged with "drill" in A (mastery).
- **G mixes two things**: peer community/support (233, 158, 231) and mentorship (192, 194, 284), plus competitive rankings (181, 186). Consider splitting G into peer community, mentorship, and rank comparison (the last overlaps X).
- **C is the catch-all for coaching**: classroom coaching, video courses, newspapers, standard books, primary sources (PIB). Consider splitting into C1 live/star instruction and C2 curated content (books, digests, CA).
- **X vs F for test series**: Indian test series sell both simulation and ranked feedback; coded X with F/G secondary. Percentile analytics might sit better under F or a rank sub-code.
