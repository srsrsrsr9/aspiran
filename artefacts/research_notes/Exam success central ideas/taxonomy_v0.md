# Starting list of central ideas (v0) — codes for mapping

Neutral test-prep view: all exams (school, university entrance, standardised, professional, civil service, languages).
This is a starting list. Mappers may propose new ideas or splits; the report writer finalises.

| Code | Central idea | Covers (examples) |
|---|---|---|
| R | Retrieval practice (testing yourself) | flashcards, blurting, blank-page recall, quizzes, practice questions, past papers used for recall |
| S | Spacing and scheduled review | spaced-repetition algorithms (SM-2, FSRS, Leitner), revision cycles, successive relearning, 2-3-5-7 |
| I | Interleaving and varied practice | mixing topics/problem types, varied contexts |
| U | Understanding and elaboration | self-explanation, Feynman, concrete examples, worked examples, concept maps, dual coding, active reading frameworks (SQ3R, THIEVES), questioning |
| M | Mnemonics and encoding tricks | memory palace, peg/major systems, acronyms, image association, story method |
| N | Note-making and knowledge organisation | Cornell, outlines, Zettelkasten, note apps, condensed notes, mind maps used as storage |
| F | Feedback and error correction | answer evaluation, explanations of mistakes, error logs / wrong-answer notebooks, tutoring corrections |
| X | Exam simulation and test-taking technique | full mocks, timed papers, CBT simulators, time allocation, option elimination, exam wrappers |
| A | Adaptive personalisation and mastery | diagnostics, adaptive engines, mastery learning, AI tutors that adjust to the learner |
| C | Expert instruction and curated content | lectures, star teachers, video courses, standard textbooks, summaries, current-affairs digests, text-to-speech delivery |
| P | Planning, goals and metacognition | study planners, backward planning, time-blocking, WOOP, self-monitoring, calibration |
| T | Attention and distraction control | blockers, Pomodoro, deep work, phone friction |
| H | Motivation, habits and gamification | streaks, points, rewards, habit stacking, identity-based habits |
| G | Social learning and accountability | co-study, body doubling, study groups, teaching others, mentors, peer comparison and rankings |
| E | Study environment and place | libraries, paid study rooms, study cafés, coaching hubs, context-dependent study |
| W | Wellbeing, stress and physiology | sleep, exercise, nutrition, anxiety techniques, breathing, expressive writing, mental-health support |
| B | Mindset and beliefs | growth mindset, grit, self-affirmation, reappraisal of ability |
| K | Shortcuts and enhancement claims | brain training, nootropics, speed reading, sleep learning, learning styles, binaural beats, Mozart effect |
| O | Answer lookup and cognitive offloading | photo solvers, answer services, AI that does the work for the student |
