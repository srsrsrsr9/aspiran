## AI tools launched or prominent in 2024–2026

- **UPSC AI** provides 60-second evaluation of uploaded handwritten Mains answers, with rubric-based scores and feedback.[^1_6][^1_7]
- **PadhAI** combines an AI tutor, PYQs, current affairs, gamified practice and Mains-answer evaluation.[^1_8][^1_9]
- **Vaidra**, founded in 2024, offers UPSC GPT, an AI Mains evaluator, daily current affairs and adaptive Prelims tests based on PYQs.[^1_4][^1_10]
- **SuperKalam** positions itself as a personal AI mentor and evaluates handwritten Mains answers in about 60 seconds.[^1_4]
- **PrepAiro** and **Smart Prepper** focus on structured UPSC content plus AI-based Mains answer feedback.[^1_4]
- **SATHEE** expanded after its late-2023 rollout to cover CUET, SSC, Banking, Agriculture, CLAT and Railway exams in addition to JEE and NEET.[^1_17]
