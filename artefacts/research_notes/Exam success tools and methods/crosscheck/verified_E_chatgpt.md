None of the 40 names is in the catalogue. Near neighbours: SWAYAM sits next to NPTEL (462) and DIKSHA (458); e-PG Pathshala is a different site from ePathshala (NCERT, 459); box breathing overlaps slightly with "slow breathing" inside item 142.

Worst errors:
1. **CBSE Pariksha Sangam** is described wrongly. It is CBSE's exam-admin portal (school logins, admit cards, results, special-needs requests), not a bank of sample papers and model answers. Rejected; the real place for sample papers and marking schemes, CBSE Academic (cbseacademic.nic.in), is added instead.
2. **Broken or wrong links**: the NCERT Exemplar link uses a host that does not exist (ncert.ncert.org.in; the right one is ncert.nic.in). The NaturalReader link is wrong (.org; the real site is naturalreaders.com). The MAA review link for Burger and Starbird is dead.
3. **Made-up citation**: "The University of Toronto recommends a test-anxiety guided meditation" could not be found. The method stays; the claim is dropped.
4. Smaller fixes: the Five-Step Study Cycle ends with **Check**, not "Assess", and it grew out of Frank Christ's PLRS system. The MTG title is "Objective NCERT at your Fingertips". Mathigon's Polypad has moved to Amplify (polypad.amplify.com).
5. Status caveat: **NTA National Test Abhyas** is still in the app stores (iOS version 1.4.4), and 2025 guides still point to it. One App Store review says the backend is dead. Treat it as live but shaky.
