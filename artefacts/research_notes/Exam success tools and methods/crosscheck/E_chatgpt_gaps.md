| **22. Pólya's Four-Step Problem-Solving Method** — Mathematical reasoning framework | George Pólya's approach: understand the problem, devise a plan, carry out the plan and look back to evaluate the solution. |
| **23. PEEL paragraph method** — Academic writing framework | Structures written answers as **Point, Evidence, Explanation, Link**, making essays and descriptive or analytical exam responses more coherent. |
| **24. Jigsaw method** — Cooperative learning technique | Learners become experts on separate portions of a topic and then teach their sections to their group, assembling a complete understanding collaboratively. |
