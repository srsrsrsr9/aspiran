# Fact-check of Gemini's list (C_gemini_books.md)

Checked 1 November 2026 against the catalogue's master list (390 entries) and about 25 web searches. Gemini called all 58 items "verified". They were not.

## (a) Summary

| Verdict | Count | Rows |

**Worst errors**
1. **All 8 Indian topper and UPSC strategy books (rows 51–58) look invented.** The people are real (Akshat Jain AIR 2 2018, Anudeep Durishetty AIR 1 2017, Gaurav Agrawal AIR 1 2013, Junaid Ahmad AIR 3 2018, Nitin Singhania, NV Sir), but none of these titles turned up at the named publishers or anywhere else. Several links point to unrelated pages. One example: the "Jaico" link for Akshat Jain's book is a page for *Anthropology Simplified*. The real books close to these are already in the catalogue: *The Secret Code of UPSC Toppers* (Avinash Agarwal, Disha) and *UPSC Files* (Shubham Gupta, Disha).
2. **Three courses don't exist as described.** There is no "Supercharging Your Memory" course by Joordens on Coursera. Athabasca's course is "Learning to Learn Online", and it did not run on edX. No OpenLearn course has the exact title "Improving your study techniques".
3. **The MIT OCW link is for the wrong course.** It points to an infant-cognition course. Gabrieli's course is 9.00SC Introduction to Psychology (Fall 2011), not "Introduction to Cognitive Psychology" (2014).
4. **Wrong publishers and links:** *Remember It!* is from Abrams Image, not Little, Brown/Voracious. Genova's *Remember* is from Harmony, but the link goes to Simon & Schuster. *The Science of Everyday Thinking* launched in 2014, not 2015.
5. **Subscriber counts and biographies are made up or unverified.** Kharma Medic "1.5M", StudyMD "1M", "Dr. Sean" and Faye Bate "Birmingham graduate" were not confirmed. Mike Dee has about 1.14M subscribers, not 800K. Cajun Koi Academy is run by two brothers, not "medical doctors and visual artists". The tally of "26 cognitive researchers" counts Gwern and a single InnerDrive entry as researchers.

## (c) Corrections to existing entries

1. **"Mike and Matty" and "Cajun Koi Academy" (listed as unidentified).** Cajun Koi Academy is run by two brothers, Mike and Matt ([Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/articles/youtube-content-helping-tweens-and-teens-navigate-school-and-life)). @mikeandmatty is a US creator duo with a similar audience size whose most-viewed video is a procrastination tutorial. These are very probably the same channel under a new name. Check the channel's About page before stating it as fact. Remove both from the "Unidentified" paragraph and use the single new row above.
2. **#267 *The Science of Learning: 99 Studies* (Busch and Watson, 2019).** The first edition was *The Science of Learning: 77 Studies That Every Teacher Needs to Know* ([Research School](https://researchschool.org.uk/unity/news/the-magnificent-77-studies-that-every-teacher-needs-to-know/)). *99 Studies* is the expanded later edition. The year 2019 likely belongs to *77 Studies* (Routledge, 2018/19). Re-check the year of *99 Studies*. Busch and Watson run InnerDrive (new row above).
3. **#297 (studytube creators).** Jade Bowler also wrote a book (new row above).
4. **#265 *How Learning Works*.** The 2nd edition (2023) has **eight** principles; the first edition had seven. The catalogue already notes the 2nd edition, so just make sure "seven principles" isn't stated for it.
5. **"Unresearched: the answer-elimination literature".** Not resolved by this list.
