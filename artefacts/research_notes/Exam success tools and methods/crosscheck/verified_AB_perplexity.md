**Worst errors:**
1. **B's status note is wrong.** It says "no shutdown or acquisition is confirmed". In fact upGrad completed its purchase of Unacademy on 1 Sep 2026, BYJU'S has been in insolvency since 2024, and Toppr (bought by BYJU'S in 2021) does not load.
2. **JEET AI's link, jeetai.com, belongs to a Chinese company that makes vehicle exhaust (AdBlue) parts.** The real JEET AI is a paid tutor from Extramarks, launched 21 Oct 2026.
3. **A lists Socratic and StudyBlue as live.** Socratic was withdrawn in 2025. studyblue.com now redirects to chegg.com/flashcards.
4. **Several links are broken or fake.** yptourcn.com does not exist, so it is neither the official YPT site nor a working copy. Flora's link uses the wrong company domain (appfinanza instead of appfinca). The My Study Life link is malformed. "prepairio.com" does not exist (the real site is prepairo.ai). Toppr's main domain doesn't load.
5. **A presents Quizizz as current.** It has been Wayground since June 2025, and quizizz.com redirects there.

## (c) Corrections to existing entries

1. **Aakash (entry 184).** Change the tag from BK to V and update the ownership. Manipal Group (Ranjan Pai) is now the largest shareholder, at about 39–40%; CCI approved its larger stake in June 2025. BYJU'S held about 25.7% after a rights issue in November 2025. In June 2026, BYJU'S lenders were reported to be negotiating for about 30% of Aakash, valuing it at about $2 billion; this is not final ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/byjus-lenders-in-talks-to-take-30-stake-in-aakash-end-legal-battle-with-founder); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/cci-clears-manipal-groups-stake-boost-in-aakash-educational-services)).
2. **BYJU'S (entry 176).** Add three facts. Manipal filed a fresh bid to join the insolvency process in November 2025 ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/latest/corporate/story/manipal-group-submits-fresh-eoi-to-join-insolvency-process-of-byjus-parent-company-502134-2025-11-13)). The tribunal paused bidding until 31 Aug 2026 (PTI report of 24 Jul 2026, seen only in search results). GeoGebra and Toppr belong to BYJU'S, and GeoGebra is on the list of assets for sale.
3. **IMS, TIME, Career Launcher (entry 186).** All three own sites are live (imsindia.com, time4education.com, careerlauncher.com), so the tag can become V (own sites). Fees are still not verified.
4. **Turbo AI (entry 55).** Add the official site [turbo.ai](https://www.turbo.ai); turbolearn.ai redirects there. It is the same product as "Turbolearn" in Answer A.
5. **YPT – Yeolpumta (entry 103).** Add the official site [yeolpumta.com](https://www.yeolpumta.com); it links to the official app store listings (Android package com.pallo.passiontimerscoped). "yptourcn.com" in Answer A doesn't exist; no website is registered at that address.
6. **Headline claim: "no bilingual (English and Hindi) spaced-repetition product with a clear lead".** This still holds. But QuizCherry (free, English and Hindi, daily UPSC, SSC and banking quizzes with a "spaced revision" feature) is the closest product found so far and belongs in the competitor notes.
7. **No change needed:** Socratic is correctly shown as withdrawn. Flora's link (flora.appfinca.com) is correct, and Answer A's "appfinanza" link is wrong. My Study Life's link (mystudylife.com) is correct.
