What your centre gets

Your name, your colour

Students join with your code; from then on the app shows your logo and colour, "with Aspiran" under it.

Every answer, marked in minutes

Students write answers daily and get marks on five parts, with a fix for each, in minutes. A teacher view to confirm marks is being built with our first centres.

Who has gone quiet

Weak topics per batch, and the students who haven't turned up for a few days, so you can talk to them before they drop out.

Batch chat and announcements

Each batch has its own chat, with announcements pinned at the top. Teachers can hide messages and see what was reported.

Doubts and study plans

Students ask doubts, teachers answer. Day-by-day plans for a batch or for one student.

Your own material

Add your notes and question sets, for one batch or all, next to Aspiran's library.

Plus everything Aspiran gives every student: the daily review, 2,000+ practice questions, 11 past papers on UPSC's keys, 250+ short videos and 20+ hours of recorded notes, most of it in Hindi too.
