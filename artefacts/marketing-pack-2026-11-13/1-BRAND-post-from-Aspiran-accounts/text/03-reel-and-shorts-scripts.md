# Reel and Shorts scripts (8)

Each works with the sound off: the words on screen carry it. Shoot on a phone, or build from the carousel frames
(each frame held for the time shown). Vertical, 9:16. No music, or a soft instrumental kept low.
Last two seconds of every video: **"Try one topic free · aspiran.in"**. Put the tagged YouTube or Instagram link in
the description and the pinned comment.

Where a script says [YOU], it is you or a friend on camera or a hand on paper. Never a stock "topper".

## R8. Who is behind this (45 to 60 s) [YOU, on camera]
No script to read out. Four things to say in your own words, one take, at your desk:
1. Who you are and why you built this (one true sentence about what you saw aspirants go through).
2. What it is: "You read the books. This makes it stay, has you write, and puts you in a small group."
3. What it is not: not coaching, no toppers to show yet, no promises about results.
4. The ask: "Do one topic. It's free and needs no sign-up. Tell me what's wrong with it." Then your email on screen: hello@aspiran.in.
This one video does more for trust than everything else in this folder.
