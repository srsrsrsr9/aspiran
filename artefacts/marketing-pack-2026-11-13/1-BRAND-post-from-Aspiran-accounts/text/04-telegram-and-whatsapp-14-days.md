# Telegram and WhatsApp Channel: 14 days, ready to post

Three posts a day. Copy each as written.

- **7:15 am, "Today's Chai":** this slot needs that morning's cards from the app, so it can't be written ahead. Use the
  template at the bottom. Skip it on days no Chai is published.
- **1:00 pm, the question:** post it as a **quiz poll** on Telegram (pick the option marked ✔ as correct). On WhatsApp,
  post it as plain text.
- **9:00 pm, the answer, and yesterday's again:** this is the method, shown in public.

These are **practice questions written for this pack**, not UPSC past questions. Don't label them PYQ. Every fact is in
`FACTS-TO-CHECK.md`: tick them against your book once before you start.

Link for the last line of each night post: `https://app.aspiran.in/app/?utm_source=telegram&utm_medium=post#try`
(on WhatsApp use the WhatsApp link from file 01).

## Sundays: one Mains question for the week

These two are real UPSC questions, from Mains 2025, GS Paper II. Post one each Sunday; the next Sunday, post a skeleton
(six lines) and invite people to compare with theirs.

> **Week 1 (UPSC Mains 2025, GS II, Q1, 10 marks, 150 words):** Discuss the 'corrupt practices' for the purpose of the Representation of the People Act, 1951. Analyze whether the increase in the assets of the legislators and/or their associates, disproportionate to their known sources of income, would constitute 'undue influence' and consequently a corrupt practice.

> **Week 2 (UPSC Mains 2025, GS II, Q2, 10 marks, 150 words):** Comment on the need of administrative tribunals as compared to the court system. Assess the impact of the recent tribunal reforms through rationalization of tribunals made in 2021.

Add under each: `Write it on paper. Mark your own answer first. Skeleton next Sunday.`
