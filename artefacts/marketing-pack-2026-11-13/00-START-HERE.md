# Aspiran marketing pack, 13 Nov 2026: start here

Everything in this pack is finished and ready to post: 154 images, a printable sheet, and the words for every channel,
in English and Hindi. This page tells you what's where, what to do first, and what to post when.

## Before you post anything (three things, about 30 minutes)

1. **Make sure the door opens.** Every post sends people to "Try one topic free". On 13 Nov that demo was still empty:
   the Federalism topic hadn't been imported and approved. Open `https://app.aspiran.in/app/#try` on your phone. If you
   see the topic, go ahead. If not, **wait**, or for now use a topic page link instead (`https://app.aspiran.in/learn`).
2. **Tick the facts.** Open `FACTS-TO-CHECK.md` and check the 17 lines against your book. Once.
3. **Set up the accounts.** Bios, the pinned post and the link for each place are in
   `1-BRAND…/text/01-bios-pinned-post-and-links.md`.

## The two folders, and the one rule between them

| Folder | Who posts it | From where |
|---|---|---|
| **`1-BRAND-post-from-Aspiran-accounts`** | You | Aspiran's own Instagram, YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn page; and (file 06 only) your own name as founder |
| **`2-FRIENDS-ONLY-never-from-Aspiran-accounts`** | Your friends | Their own personal accounts, in their own words |

**Never cross them.** Nothing from folder 2 goes on an Aspiran account, and friends shouldn't post the brand
carousels as if they made them. Folder 2 has its own first page; send friends only that folder.

## How to post each kind

- **Instagram carousel:** New post → select frames 01 to the last, in order, from `en/` (or `hi/`) → paste the caption
  from `text/02`. Post the Hindi set as its own post, a day or two later, not as extra frames.
- **Single post / story:** one image, its caption from `text/02`. For stories, add the link sticker with the story link.
- **Reel or Short:** the scripts in `text/03` say which frames to hold and for how long. Any phone editor (CapCut,
  InShot, Instagram's own) will do: import the frames, set each one's time, add the on-screen line, export.
- **Telegram:** create each 1 pm post as a *Quiz* poll and mark the ✔ option. Schedule the whole fortnight in one
  sitting (long-press Send → Schedule).
- **Print:** print `recall-sheet-fundamental-rights-A4.pdf` at 100%, no scaling. The QR code already carries the
  print link.

## After two weeks: read the numbers

In PostHog → Web analytics → Sources, each place shows up under its own name (instagram, telegram, youtube,
friend-NAME…). Look at which one sent people who **finished** the topic, not which sent the most clicks. Do more of
that one and drop the weakest.

## Three things this pack does not contain, on purpose

- **Testimonials, user counts, toppers.** There aren't any yet. When real ones exist, they go in.
- **News.** The daily "Today's Chai" post needs that morning's cards; there is a template for it in `text/04`.
- **Posts written to look as if a stranger found Aspiran.** The friends' posts all say "a friend of mine built this".
  That is what makes them safe to post and, usually, more convincing.
