# FRIENDS ONLY. Never post anything in this folder from an Aspiran account.

This folder is for a few friends who are happy to tell people about Aspiran **from their own personal accounts**.
It is kept apart on purpose. If a line from here appears on an Aspiran page, it reads as the brand pretending to be a
user, and that is the one thing that would hurt.

## For you (the owner): how to hand this out

1. Send each friend **only this folder**, not the brand folder.
2. Ask each one to **do the free topic first** (about 20 minutes): `https://app.aspiran.in/app/#try`
3. Give each friend their own link, so you can see whose post brought people. Replace NAME with their first name, lower case:
   `https://app.aspiran.in/app/?utm_source=friend-NAME&utm_medium=post#try`
4. Ask them to pick **one or two** posts, change the words until it sounds like them, and post over a couple of weeks.
   Ten friends posting the same sentence on the same day looks exactly like what it is.

## For the friend: three small rules

1. **Say how you know about it.** Every post here says "a friend of mine built this" or similar. Keep that. It's true,
   people trust it more than a stranger's praise, and it keeps you clear of the advertising rules on undisclosed
   endorsements.
2. **Say only what you actually did.** If you did the free topic, say so. If you're not preparing for UPSC, say that
   too ("I'm not an aspirant, but…"). Don't say it helped your score, your rank or your exam.
3. **Your own words beat ours.** These are starting points. Cut, rewrite, add your own screenshot.

Don't post in Telegram or WhatsApp study groups without asking the admin first. Most have a no-promotion rule, and a
friendly "may I share something a friend built?" usually gets a yes.
