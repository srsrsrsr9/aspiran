Sound kit

Twelve short effects and five soundscapes, all synthesized, so they add almost nothing to the app's size. Each is exported as Opus (.ogg) and AAC (.m4a) under content/audio/sfx/. Templates name effects by id, so swapping in a recorded sound later changes no content.

Effects play only after the member turns sound on, never on TRAGIC content, and peak well below the music beds. Turn sound on above to try them.
