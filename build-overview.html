The thesis

"The syllabus is vast, but what breaks people is the multi-year endurance test: isolation, uncertainty, comparison, family pressure and money running out. Content is everywhere. Belonging, honesty and safety are not."

Proposed first build: Honest Tools page

A single web page that runs from this folder with nothing to install. It has three tools: the Attempt Compass (attempts and age window as a calm timeline), the Cost of a Year calculator (rent, library, coaching, food and opportunity cost), and the Parent Page (a one-page plan with a dated Plan B to send home). Each one ends in a share card, and the page works in English and Hindi. No login, and nothing sensitive is stored.
